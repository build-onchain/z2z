## 3. Refund-before-fund: recovery component, chưa complete fair exchange

**Proposed/INFERENCE; không recommend triển khai thành complete swap.** U bán ZEC; S cấp EVM asset. Joint note dùng per-swap threshold key, không party reconstruct full `ask`. Construction dưới đây giúp source recovery khi never-funds, nhưng có failed-claim secret leak counterexample ở §3.3; chỉ fixed C expiry không đủ fair exchange.

### Transaction graph

- **F:** funding Ironwood/v6 vào joint note J. Freeze exact note/effects trước protocol setup; selected value/recipient/note randomness/nullifier material phải locally derived và kiểm tra. Không broadcast F trước recovery package.
- **C:** fixed source-success spend J → S, đúng amount/fees/recipient, consensus branch và finite expiry `H_C`. S chỉ có authorization cho **C**, không source master key hoặc quyền ký sweep khác.
- **R:** fixed source-refund spend cùng J/nullifier → U. Full signatures, note/FVK/prover/IO/binding artifacts và user-controlled inputs phải có durable ở U **trước F**. R có future locktime; một transparent timing input với non-final sequence do U kiểm soát enable consensus locktime. Principal vẫn Ironwood; public input tạo metadata/UTXO/privacy dependency thực.
- H_C là last-valid height C; H_R là first-valid height R sau đánh giá exact locktime off-by-one. Yêu cầu **H_C < H_R** trên **cùng Zcash chain**, không so với EVM timestamp.
- V6 cho bổ sung witness/anchor/proof sau F mine mà không ký effects mới. Người giữ R tự prove/extract/broadcast, không đòi S ký lại. RefundReady server flag/partial signature không đủ.

### Setup và success

1. U/S tạo fresh threshold source key + view/proving material; authenticate quote/network/branch/exact assets/recipients/fees.
2. Chuẩn bị C/R trước funding. U verify complete R và khả năng prover/transparent input; cả hai dùng nonce signing riêng, không nonce reuse. Nếu setup lỗi, abort **chưa có tiền locked**.
3. Candidate ưu tiên có **verifiable encrypted/adaptor SpendAuth của C**, decrypt bởi secret x **độc lập spend key**. U biết x; S có encrypted C authorization và proof liên hệ với EVM hashlock. Exact C kept private giữa parties; không đưa full C signature/body lên EVM rồi claim unlinkability.
4. Sau recovery setup, F được fund; S kiểm exact canonical source note/value và fund exact EVM escrow. U kiểm deployed contract/code/config, asset/amount/fixed U payout recipient/claim bounds và finality; không reveal x trước khi lock đúng. Funding order có thể S trước để giảm never-funds exposure, nhưng **không substitute R**.
5. U claim EVM trước contract-enforced safe cutoff; x unlock chỉ fixed C signature. S cập nhật proof/anchor và execute C trong validity window. EVM claim guard/permit chỉ mở sau actual F evidence, tránh U claim khi chưa trả source. Permissionless submit phải luôn payout đúng committed recipient.
6. Nếu không claim: EVM S refund theo contract; U execute R sau H_R. C đã expired; không party giữ full ask để tạo fresh unrestricted success spend.

### Điều kiện không được giấu

- **Liveness/timing:** malicious U có thể claim lúc cuối contract cho phép; phải chứng minh vẫn còn đủ source inclusion margin trước H_C. EVM timestamp và Zcash height không đổi trực tiếp; cần rõ bounds về chain progress, broadcast/inclusion/confirmations và finality, hoặc chain-aware verifier thiết kế riêng. Không tự gán số block/giây. Ngoài assumptions này, EVM paid nhưng C expired vẫn có thể làm S mất tiền.
- **Cryptography:** compatible threshold RedPallas verifiable encrypted signing + secret binding chưa được xác minh như library audited dùng ngay. Ordinary FROST không cung cấp nó. Đây integration/security prerequisite **cụ thể**, không TODO mơ hồ hay lời hứa đã solved.
- **Refund data:** R phải chứa actual effects/spend-authorizing signatures/tất cả dummy-action/binding/proving dependencies; audit malicious coordinator/unknown full key/rogue key. Fresh anchor không sửa branch/fee/expiry/effects. Fees muốn tăng cần upfront authorized variants hoặc cơ chế fee relay chứng minh được, không mutate signed transaction.
- **Timing UTXO:** reserve user-exclusive input; S không được spend/grief pin. U mất key/double-spend timing input có thể làm R invalid; honest-U backup/UTXO policy cần thực. Transparent input public, không âm thầm coi đủ contest privacy.
- **Bad payments:** exact F duy nhất từ locally constructed wallet; wrong amount/extra note không tự nằm trong C/R. Không nhận arbitrary deposit/top-up vào shared inbox rồi hứa refund mọi note. Wallet khác gửi thêm cần explicit unsupported/error recovery contract, không giả independent refund.
- **Privacy:** solver thấy own joint note details/FVK; avoid long-term wallet keys. x không full ask giảm quyền nhưng không proof anonymity. Public timing input/destination recipient/amount/time/funding/API/IP remains; threat model và organizer ruling riêng.

### 3.3 Counterexample đã tìm và exercise: claim revert

U gửi claim EVM chứa x nhưng transaction out-of-gas/revert; x vẫn public. S decrypt C, lấy source ZEC trước H_C. EVM không ghi successful claim, tới timeout S refund external escrow: **S giữ cả hai**. Đây case khác delay bound: fixed transaction authorization không gắn execution source với successful destination payout. Model đã exercise asset ownership transitions, không actual chain.

Đặt `Ready` irrevocable entitlement trước reveal chỉ sửa user safety: nếu U không reveal x, S có thể bị locked vô hạn. Trả lại quyền refund S theo timer tái tạo attack. Vì vậy không promote adaptor candidate thành solution safe chỉ vì đã có R.

Một sửa thiết kế khả dĩ: EVM payout theo **canonical source success C inclusion** (positive proof), refund theo complete canonical **noninclusion** của C sau expiry, không theo “claim chưa gọi”. Khi đó không cần reveal x làm payout trigger; có thể giản lược thành direct exact payment protocol §4.1. Missing proof không safe refund; escrow giữ locked tới khi có witness hợp lệ.

### 4.1 Absence-branch research construction: exact-payment proof-aware escrow

**Proposed, không claim verifier đã tồn tại hoặc contest privacy pass.** Root change: U không trả vào solver inbox rồi chờ watcher và không khóa principal trong unrecoverable joint note. S lock external inventory **trước khi U phát hành executable payment authorization**, không chỉ trước thao tác broadcast. Refund external không dựa đồng hồ/time-out im lặng, mà dựa một statement kiểm chứng về **exact named transaction** đã hết khả năng mine.

1. U dựng **unsigned effects** của payment **P** Ironwood/v6, exact S receiver/amount/valid encrypted output, finite `nExpiryHeight=H`, unique per-quote effects và txid. V6 effect txid không cần real-input SpendAuth signatures. U giữ spend keys và **mọi real-input SpendAuth signatures/threshold shares đủ để hoàn tất authorization** kín; mặc định chưa ký real inputs ở bước này. Không chia signed/executable P, spend secrets hoặc PCZT fields cho phép S tự hoàn tất authorization. “Giữ spend key” và “chưa broadcast” một mình không đủ bảo vệ.
2. S chỉ nhận **unsigned/redacted effects + output-validation evidence** cần kiểm P receiver/value/ciphertext/branch/expiry, không real-input authorization. Dùng redacted PCZT hoặc effect/proof representation phù hợp; bỏ signatures/secret signing material trước export và kiểm artifact không executable. Receiver/openings chia riêng trong phạm vi cần, không full long-term wallet FVK. U/S verify và ký **quote-domain riêng**, bind P/recipient/external asset+amount/expiry/checkpoint; quote signature không SpendAuth cho P. Không chỉ accept cmx/arbitrary compact ciphertext; wrong/changed P không payout guarantee, P không top-up deposit address.
3. S fund EVM escrow với fixed U payout recipient/exact asset/amount, binding P hoặc hiding commitment, activation/checkpoint **h0 trước admissible payment**, H, source verifier policy và unique quote consumption. **Trước real-input signing/authorization release**, U independently kiểm code/config, exact effect/quote binding, funded amount/asset, activation interval và finality policy. Nếu funding/activation chưa final, sai binding hoặc interval không còn hợp lệ: không ký/release P; abort/renegotiate unsigned effects. S không được sở hữu executable P ở giai đoạn này. P mined trước h0 không được tái dùng để claim swap mới; quote/deployment replay bind domain.
4. Chỉ sau gate (3), U ký real-input SpendAuth cho exact checked P, hoàn tất cần thiết và broadcast; signature/executable P chỉ được release tới solver/relayer/network **sau funded/finalized activation gate**. Nếu U dùng offline presigning, signatures vẫn private/nonexported tới gate đó, không đưa trong pre-funding PCZT. P canonical+validated inclusion trong admissible interval → **irrevocable U entitlement** theo source-finality assumption; permissionless proof chỉ pays U. S được ZEC trực tiếp, không key extraction/adaptor. Failed EVM proof submission không đổi source fact; retry không cần S ký/cooperate.
5. P không mined: U vẫn giữ original source notes vì payment không xảy ra; sau H thì P không mine trên valid accepted canonical chain tiếp được. S chỉ được refund nếu verifier xác nhận **complete exclusion**: mọi block interval h0..H (exact boundary), **complete valid raw-block transaction witnesses** với mỗi txid derived từ valid parsed transaction, authenticated count/depth/tree structure hoặc một separately sound equivalent circuit, không P; cùng accepted source history/finality policy qua H. Không accept claimed hash lists chỉ root-match; không refund khi gap/body/header/competing-chain view incomplete, trước H hoặc missing callback.
6. Interval có P → exclusion proof fail dù positive-proof relayer chậm. Không valid interval witness → escrow locked, không mặc định S lấy lại. Permissionless proof submission có data/gas/prover/liveness dependencies. **Relay completeness cũng là SAFETY assumption:** honest competing headers/heavier chain phải available tới verifier trước terminal decision, checkpoint fresh/valid và anti-eclipse/challenge policy rõ. Best submitted cumulative work không tự prove globally canonical; withholding honest headers có thể cho no-P fork refund dù S nhận P thật. Halting/censorship/unbounded reorg không finite-refund guarantee.

**Pre-activation authorization attack:** U gửi signed P để S “kiểm tra” trước funding; S broadcast P trước h0/activation, nhận ZEC rồi không fund escrow hoặc P ngoài admissible interval. Không payout proof hợp lệ cho U. Chặn bằng authorization-release gate (1)–(4), không bằng cam kết “S sẽ không broadcast”. Signature redaction giữ nguyên effect txid để quote/escrow commit trước; phải kiểm every exported artifact không chứa complete real-input authorization hoặc material hoàn tất nó.

**Proof statements phải implementation thật:** connected valid source chain từ agreed fresh valid checkpoint; difficulty/PoW/cumulative-work/finality với honest competing-header availability/anti-eclipse policy; complete parsed valid raw blocks và source-derived txids + authenticated actual leaf count/depth/tree structure, không block omission; valid exact effect/receiver/value/Ironwood ciphertext; unique quote/network/deployment và H. Merkle root không authenticate count ở header: `[A,B,P,Q]`, `[hash(A|B),hash(P|Q)]` và `[root]` có cùng root nhưng latter omit P, không hash collision. Reject internal-node-as-leaf bằng transaction-validity/serialization/depth/circuit constraints, không một câu “kiểm count” không authenticated. Valid Equihash orphan không canonical; light-client source-consensus/full validation assumptions explicit. Public data availability không guarantee verifier đã nhận honest chain.

**Privacy gate:** public P txid/receiver/openings deterministic link hai chain. Public-proof version chỉ engineering experiment, không private entry. Product cần separately implemented **hiding effect/inclusion và complete-exclusion proofs**, same committed P/expiry/checkpoint/payout binding + canonical history và nullifier/quote uniqueness, không reveal source locator; ZK public statement/circuit/data path phải reviewed. Không gọi có chữ ZK là tự unlinkable; source timing/output amount/operator/funding vẫn đo. Một generic ZK verifier không implementation các statements này.

Absence branch này đóng logical local-timeout race bằng consensus expiry+complete source evidence, đổi prerequisites sang light-client/effect/exclusion/privacy verifier và availability/cost, chưa solved. Đây alternative lịch sử; current canonical chọn positive-conflict/local-Q priority. Không tự enable absence fallback hoặc code từ approval docs.

**Terminal predicates trên SAME valid accepted finalized history:**
- Exact eligible P included và validated payment effect → payout U.
- Exact Q included với authenticated SAME committed real input n → P không thể included trên history đó → refund S.
- **Không chỉ exact Q:** malicious U có thể mine transaction **T** khác chi n trước Q; khi đó P và Q đều invalid, exact-Q-only refund bị stuck. Candidate phải evaluate `ConflictingSpendIncluded(T)`: valid source inclusion, same committed REAL input/pool/network, **effect identity T != P**. P thay auth bytes/anchor/proof nhưng same v6 effect id vẫn P, không conflict. Nếu T từ trước activation/checkpoint, cần authenticated earlier membership/spent-state evidence; không silently omit lịch sử rồi chờ Q không thể mine.
- P chưa mine và n unspent, S có thể broadcast Q; P/Q tranh same n. P thắng → pay U; Q thắng → refund S, source return U trừ fee. Chỉ một tx accepted trên valid history; inclusion proof không authorize refund trên orphan/fork khác.

**Premature cancellation:** pure-shielded Q không có delayed-locktime enforce bằng một field đơn giản. S giữ executable Q có thể submit trước funding/activation/payment deadline. Nếu Q fixed self-output hợp lệ, đây **cancel/fee grief/option risk**, không principal theft: U giữ source qua Q, S refund chỉ bằng Q proof. Wallet phải reveal trước chính xác capability/fee. Không promise payment window guaranteed hay “S không broadcast sớm”. Muốn delay Q bằng source timelock cần independently audited enforceable construction, có thể transparent timing input và privacy/UTXO risk; không thêm ngầm.

**Competing inputs/transactions:** first scope một real note tránh Q invalid do input khác bị spent. Nếu P nhiều real inputs, Q đủ subset/all proof dependencies phải được định nghĩa; U có thể chi một input khác làm P invalid nhưng Q selected-input predicate không phản ánh recovery hết order. Accept valid conflict trên ANY proved real input của P có thể chứng minh P impossible, nhưng cần authentic input-set relation/real-vs-dummy/conservation và valid Q path cho no-conflict case. Không arbitrary topups; fee-bumped P' trả S nhưng T!=P có thể khiến original escrow refund—wallet fixed-effect policy vẫn cần, không tự gọi đó supported payment variant.

**Không source-enforce wallet policy:** U giữ spend key nên có thể tự mine P trước activation/ngoài eligible `[L,H]`, dù intended wallet không release. P consumed n → Q impossible; T!=P predicate không refund và eligible-only payout không nhận → stuck. Cần explicit allocation/admission/history semantics cho exact P đã trả S; không coi gate UI tự chứng minh không có early P. Tương tự arbitrary T có thể trả S bằng different effects: nullifier conflict chứng minh exact P impossible, **không chứng minh S chưa được trả một payment khác**. Exact-effect-only là contract/product restriction, chưa replacement-safe payment-class design hay atomic outcome guarantee cho mọi user-authored spend.

**Chi phí và privacy:** nhánh này có thể thay **whole-interval absence** bằng positive inclusion+real-conflict relation cho case recoverable; không measured gas/time win và vẫn cần canonical/valid-chain/effect/authentication verifier. Public n/Q/P txid deterministic link; private product cần hiding SAME-real-input relation/inclusion, per-order uniqueness và private witness custody. Raw nullifier trong registry không privacy solution. T khác/source reorg/censorship/Q expired, fee pinning/branch changes/invalid witness/prover unavailable vẫn có thể stuck. Q không expiry có thể giảm expiry failure nếu valid policy cho phép, không vượt consensus branch upgrade; fee variants chỉ Q self-spends có upfront verified authorization, chưa chosen protocol.

**Recommendation delta:** ưu tiên audit/feasibility **positive-conflict + independently executable Q + general-conflict T** trước coi complete-exclusion là critical path bắt buộc. Điều kiện adoption vẫn unresolved: Q real note/signature/proving completeness, third-spend/history semantics, cancellation grief, user self-output ownership, witness scope/privacy, target verifier/resource/finality. Canonical docs không đổi, không gate Passed.

### Lifetime and kill criteria

1. **Branch lock is concrete, not just prover downtime.** If J is unspent when current branch B becomes B', both old-B C/R fail [Zebra's branch check](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/zebra/blob/e3eef2f37c35127ad1769f19a1ebc7eaa5d5d291/zebra-consensus/src/transaction/check.rs), including expiry-zero R. The branch is signed; re-proving/re-anchoring cannot change it. Neither 2-of-2 signer can renew alone, even the owner-local prover. Zero expiry ([ZIP203](https://github.com/zcash/zips/blob/eaccf9a90ca0b860712fb3d05aa337d409f1c67e/zips/zip-0203.rst)), fixed finished handoff and finite known branch variants establish **no indefinite funded-lifetime solution** in the inspected sources. This is a kill criterion for this variant under the current lifetime obligation, not a general native-Zcash impossibility theorem.
2. **Fee changes are not the same automatic consensus rejection.** A market/policy increase may delay/exclude an otherwise consensus-valid transaction; not every fee change invalidates it. However, signed fee-bearing effects cannot be repaired by adding inputs/reducing outputs, and S cannot re-prove a higher-fee presigned variant without a finished packet. Finite reviewed fee packets can extend a disclosed envelope, not guarantee future inclusion. An unqualified fee envelope or assumed third-party sponsorship is a terminal-capability failure, not evidence all fee changes inevitably strand funds ([§7 pinned builder](https://github.com/zcash/librustzcash/blob/f7b1cfb0b5bb1bdb10564079ac51b54aa18b07a1/zcash_primitives/src/transaction/builder.rs); [ZIP374 effect immutability](https://github.com/zcash/zips/blob/eaccf9a90ca0b860712fb3d05aa337d409f1c67e/zips/zip-0374.md)).
3. **Handoff-specific failures:** S arms before durable verified finished R; U disappears before required handoff; S's retained anchor becomes inadmissible with no independent re-proving capability; late F outlives recovery; or C contributions escape before protection. No finished-packet variant inspected removes all these lifetime/order obligations. Public history supplies a path, not missing private inputs.
4. **Required regardless of handoff variant:** authentic composed C/R inclusion **and output/effect** verification on ETH; same-J/pool mutual exclusion; exact context/finality and cross-version once-only consumption; independent U C-payout and S R-return witnesses/provers; immutable funded allocation with actual fixed-beneficiary transfers/retries; sound fresh threshold setup/no retained full key, nonce/session/external-alpha qualification, and each signer's receiver/value/ciphertext/fee semantics checks without forbidden disclosure. C/R signatures alone establish neither setup soundness nor those financial predicates. These remain missing capabilities under [current blockers](../BLOCKERS.md), not facts proven by the parent's allocation model.

The consolidated [construction comparison and decision menu](CONSTRUCTION-COMPARISON.md) (2026-10-07) restructures these findings into one capability matrix and an explicit owner decision menu.

**Precise next decision required:** **No construction selection/GO follows from this handoff analysis.** If independently usable recovery throughout the funded lifetime remains required, keep this variant **NO-GO** and investigate/select another construction only after its complete capability/lifetime evidence exists under unchanged isolation. Alternatively, the owner must explicitly decide whether to accept a **disclosed bounded-window contract** instead: enumerate supported branches/anchors/fees, latest safe F release/inclusion, mandatory recovery opportunity, early-R cancellation/fee rights and the residual risk of stranded ZEC/armed ETH if actual recovery does not finish before the envelope ends. A cutoff, expiry-zero or promised automation cannot enforce completion; accepting that residual exposure is a real lifetime/rights decision, **not already granted** and not itself crypto/chain GO. Without either a qualified alternative or explicit bounded-window acceptance, retain financial **NO-GO**; do not reopen the cancelled private-witness-sharing exception. No checks/builds/tests, keys, wallet/prover or network actions were performed for this continuation.

### 9.1 NEW-FINDING: pool choice changes what C/R can contain (not in §7/§8)

**[NEW SOURCE EVIDENCE, pinned cohort]** The installed Orchard 0.15.5 [`bundle.rs`](https://github.com/zcash/orchard/blob/29d1d55db62153dcaeef8ef631c8991c53ed1248/src/bundle.rs) `permits_cross_address_transfers()` returns **false** for the Orchard pool under `ProtocolVersion::V3` (post-NU6.3) and true for the Ironwood pool. [ZIP326](https://github.com/zcash/zips/blob/eaccf9a90ca0b860712fb3d05aa337d409f1c67e/zips/zip-0326.md) (same pinned commit as the other ZIP pins) states: after NU6.3, **every Orchard-pool action's output expanded receiver must equal its spent-note expanded receiver**; a wallet pairs each real spend with a fabricated zero-valued output to the spent note's own receiver, and "exposing the address or viewing key is not enough to send Orchard-pool funds to a `use_qsk = true` account after NU6.3 … a sender would need the spending key".

Consequences for J (U funds J; C pays S; R returns to U):

- **J in the Ironwood pool (cross-address permitted, PCZT v2 `ironwood` field (`orchard::v2::Bundle`, built with `BundleVersion::ironwood_v3()`), note version V3):** C can be a single action `spend J → output to S's address`; R likewise to U's address. Prover needs only J-side data plus the counterparty's **public** address. No fabricated/dummy actions. This is the material-minimal variant and matches the observed v6/Ironwood metadata in the §7 Skyhook pin.
- **J in the Orchard pool post-NU6.3 (same-address mandated):** C's J-spend action can only return value to J's own receiver, so the ZEC payout to S requires a second action, and [ZIP374](https://github.com/zcash/zips/blob/eaccf9a90ca0b860712fb3d05aa337d409f1c67e/zips/zip-0374.md) "Fabricated same-address outputs" constrains both shapes:
  - (a) fabricated zero-valued spend at S's receiver + real output to S's receiver: ZIP374 says such a spend "is a real spend from the Signer's perspective … its `spend_auth_sig` MUST be produced through the normal Signer flow using the spend authorizing key for that receiver's address" — i.e. **S's own wallet ask signs it, and the single bundle proof's Prover needs S's wallet FVK** ([pinned prover](https://github.com/zcash/orchard/blob/29d1d55db62153dcaeef8ef631c8991c53ed1248/src/pczt/prover.rs) requires `fvk` per action; the bundle proof covers all actions in one `Proof::create`). That is existing-wallet FVK disclosure — excluded by the unchanged isolation boundary.
  - (b) fully-dummy action (fabricated spend *and* output at one fresh random address): signed by the IO Finalizer from `dummy_sk`, which it must clear before Signers see the PCZT ("Signers MUST reject PCZTs that contain `dummy_ask`/`dummy_sk` values"). The ZEC then lands on a **fresh session-scoped payout address whose spend key (`dummy_sk`) must be handed to S** for S to claim it. `dummy_sk` is spend-key-equivalent for that one address (bounded to the payout value), not a wallet key and not J's key.

The pool choice is therefore itself a material-enumeration decision: Ironwood minimizes disclosures; Orchard post-NU6.3 forces either a wallet-FVK violation or a bounded `dummy_sk` handoff per payout direction. **Working design default for route-1a downstream work (recorded 2026-10-07, revisable at construction freeze): Ironwood-pool J** — it is the only variant needing no fabricated/dummy actions and no wallet-FVK exposure. It is a working assumption for the design sketches (not a selection; Q6 stays formally open until the construction freeze).

## 11. Route 1a — Q3/Q4 design draft: semantic-review package and share-release gate

**Design draft, not a source finding, not a selection.** This section turns the §10-recorded gaps into a design sketch: Q3 (signer-side semantic validation without coordinator-trusted displays) and Q4 (C-share release gating). §11.1–§11.5 are **[DESIGN]** unless a sentence cites a pin; **§11.3a records the implemented primitives and the 2026-10-07 integration transcript**, which authorize nothing and leave the R1.3 NO-GO standing. The section assumes the §9.4 disclosure model (owner-accepted in principle) and the §10 capability map.

### 11.1 Threat model recap (from §7/§8 pins)

- Withholding only the aggregate C signature is insufficient — an escaped **usable share** is executable authority once combined with the counterparty's share (§7 gate row).
- A coordinator display (sighash hex, alpha hex) is not trustworthy by itself; ZIP312 requires signers to check the SIGHASH against the data or recompute it themselves (ZIP312 at pin eaccf9a; PR593 implements no such check — §10.3 divergence 2).
- A signature that verifies against `rk` proves **which message** was signed, not that the message encodes the agreed financial semantics (outputs, recipients, values, fees) — orchard `apply_signature` re-verifies only against `rk` (locally verified, §10.1).

### 11.2 Q3 — semantic-review package [DESIGN]

**Principle:** the two signing participants are exactly U and S; each must be able to recompute, from data it holds, (a) the shielded sighash and (b) the financial semantics of the C/R transaction it is about to authorize, before emitting any share. The coordinator (either peer or a neutral process) transports bytes and cannot substitute meaning.

**Package sent to each signer per transaction X ∈ {C, R}:**

| Item | Why | Source of truth available in-cohort |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical transaction effects (or the PCZT) | Basis for independent sighash recomputation | `Pczt::into_effects()` (pczt 0.9.3) / `Signer::shielded_sighash()` |
| The signing package (message + commitments) | FROST round-2 input | frost-core 3.0.0 / reddsa |
| The per-spend alpha | So each signer signs rk-consistent shares and can check `rk == vk_group.randomize(alpha)` | `orchard::pczt::Spend::alpha()` getter (orchard 0.15.5; the pczt 0.9.3 wire field has no public getter — review-fixed attribution) |
| Expected quote terms (amounts, recipients, fee, expiry) | Independent semantics baseline | session terms (agreed earlier), held by both peers |

**Checks each signer performs before signing (all locally):**
1. Recompute the shield sighash from the effects; require equality with the signing-package message (ZIP312 signer requirement).
2. Check `rk` of each action equals the group verification key randomized by the carried alpha; reject mismatched alpha (prevents signing a re-randomization the counterparty chose adversarially).
3. Compare transaction outputs/values/recipients/fee against the signer's own copy of the agreed terms (for C: S's payout; for R: U's return minus agreed fee).
4. For its own side's spend references, verify nullifier/note consistency where it holds the material (§9.3 per-role table: S holds `nk`/FVK_J under the accepted disclosure model).

**Explicit non-goal:** signer checks do not replace the Q8 recovery capability or the composed target-side verification; they only bind each share to the terms the signer itself believes.

### 11.3 Q4 — share-release gate [DESIGN]

Follows the §8 conditional order; concretizes the gating state machine.

**Phase order (each transition gated by the previous observable):**

| # | Event | Gate condition | Notes |

| 3 | F funding → J membership accepted | U (or F) releases the funding authorization only after (2) | exact funding order per §8 |

| 5 | S arms ETH (target escrow) | | nothing source-side moves here |
| 6 | **C-share gate:** both parties verify irrevocable arming on the target (finality per target policy) | only then release usable q(C) contributions | the exact "sufficient material" definition: a share is usable iff it plus the counterparty's share plus public transcripts completes C — so gate ALL share material, not just the aggregate |
| 7 | U completes C and/or hands off the finished C | per §8: S needs only the finished C to broadcast; U needs both contributions to finish it | |
| 8 | Abort paths | pre-arming failures: no shares released, R returns J to U; post-arming failures: C or R proceed per the winning source effect | no timer-based refund; unknown states stay pending (S5) |

**Additional gate rules [DESIGN]:**
- Every signing session (C and R) is one-use with fresh nonces; re-signing the same effects with reused nonces is forbidden (threshold nonce-reuse = key recovery risk).
- Shares are transferred as opaque byte packets with the sighash+alpha they were made over; the receiver re-verifies each share against commitments (frost-core `verify_signature_share`) before aggregation.
- No party ever aggregates the other party's C share before the gate condition is observed verified; the assembly of the final C signature is done by U (per §8), S receiving only the finished extraction.
- **Alpha freshness per spend**: alpha is freshly generated for each spend/session (C and R get distinct alphas); reusing one alpha across two spends of the same ak makes `rk` identical and links them to any observer (ZIP312's per-spend randomizer derivation normally prevents this). Aggregation itself would still reject cross-session shares (challenge binds message+commitments), so this is a privacy rule, not an authority rule. (Review finding.)

## 12. Route 1a — DKG transport channel (requirements sketch, Q1 remainder)

**Design sketch, not reviewed, not implemented.** Q1's transcript evidence (§10.3a) covers the cryptography locally; the missing operational piece is how the DKG round packages travel between U and S. Sizes from the installed sources, measured under frost-core 3.0.0's postcard encoding (see §12.1 for the pin and the complete-envelope arithmetic): `dkg::round1::Package` = header + VSS commitment (2-of-2 → two 32-byte coefficient commitments) + proof-of-knowledge (64-byte signature) = **135 bytes**; `dkg::round2::Package` = header + 32-byte `SigningShare` = **37 bytes**. Both need a new kind: every established envelope already costs 322 bytes of fixed header+signature overhead (§7.2), so they do not fit "far below the existing caps" without one. [DESIGN] Requirements sketch:

1. **Round 1 = authenticated broadcast.** frost-core documents that round-1 packages require an authenticated broadcast channel (a malicious participant sending different commitments to different peers breaks the DKG's agreement guarantees). Over route-1a this can be the existing `/z2z/session/1` request-response between the two authenticated peers, with each side forwarding the counterparty's round-1 package bytes verbatim — authenticity comes from the live authenticated connection plus a session/quote binding, not from a public bulletin board.
2. **Round 2 = targeted, confidential.** Round-2 packages are per-recipient secrets (signing-share contributions); they must never transit any third party and must be bound to the exact round-1 transcript (identifier + commitment hash) they respond to.
3. **Transcript binding.** All DKG messages should carry a digest of the agreed quote context (session id, both peer identities, pool choice §9.1, J parameters once fixed) so a DKG from one session cannot be replayed into another; the KeyPackage/PublicKeyPackage derived must be committed (digest) into the subsequent quote/terms exchange.
4. **No dealer fallback.** The flow must refuse the trusted-dealer construction (`generate_with_dealer`) — it contradicts the no-complete-`ask` requirement; this is a construction-level invariant, testable by API absence.
5. **One-use sessions.** Round-1 nonces/commitments are one-use; a re-run of DKG outside an explicit recovery ceremony invalidates the previous J (new ak would re-derive a different address).
6. **Where it lands.** If route 1a matures into a selected construction, this runs inside the local companion against the authenticated peer, with the same owner-local-secret handling as other session material; no company service holds any round package.

**Open items as of 2026-10-07:** the reviewer passes on §10/§11 and on this section plus §12.1 are **done** (findings applied: cap/size corrections, kind 12 added, ordering, digest preimage, confidentiality-persistence, retry wording; code gaps listed in §12.2). Remaining: Q5 lifetime (owner), Q6 pool pin (freeze input recorded in §9.7), R2.2-grade finality for the arming gate, the §12.2 session-layer prerequisites, and spec-owner approval of the §12.1 amendment. Nothing here is implementation authorization.

### 12.1 Session-kind amendment — owner-APPROVED 2026-10-07, applied to the spec (review-fixed draft)

The frozen §7 kind table has no DKG kind, so running this ceremony requires a **spec amendment** (owner-approved amendment to [V1-P2P-PROTOCOL-SPEC](../V1-P2P-PROTOCOL-SPEC.md) §7.2). Proposal, following the table's conventions (kind byte, body contract, complete-envelope cap) and corrected against the independent review:

| Kind byte | Body contract | Maximum complete envelope |
|---|---|---:|
| 10 DKGRound1 | `context_digest[32]` + serialized `dkg::round1::Package` (encoding pinned below; measured **135 B**, complete envelope 489 B) | 512 bytes |
| 11 DKGRound2 | `context_digest[32]` + serialized `dkg::round2::Package` (measured **37 B**, complete envelope 391 B; per-recipient secret) | 512 bytes |
| 12 DKGFinalCommit | `SHA256(PublicKeyPackage)[32]` | 354 bytes |

(The fixed §7.2 overhead is 322 B = 258 header + 64 signature, which is why the original 128-byte draft cap for kind 11 was wrong and could never send; and why rule 5's transcript commitment needs its own kind — no existing body 0–9 carries a free 32-byte slot.)

**Encoding pin:** the embedded packages are the frost-core 3.0.0 `serialization` feature's format (**postcard**) at the pinned crate versions (`reddsa 0.5.2` ciphersuite over frost-core 3.0.0). A cohort change silently moves these bytes, so the amendment freezes: measured lengths (135 B / 37 B) asserted by an exact-length test on the DKG round trip, and a validation rule that the embedded package must **exhaust the body remainder** (postcard's `from_bytes` does not itself reject trailing bytes, so the envelope validator must).

Rules the amendment would add (review-corrected):
1. **Established messages + transcript-confirmation ordering**: kinds 10–12 are valid only after Hello/Ack, with both keys/challenges, unchanged context pins and strictly next per-sender seq. §7.2's confirmation gate applies as an **explicit step** (implemented in the session table): the initiator's first established message must be the `Ack` carrying `SHA256(signed bytes)` of the signed Hello response; only after that is a DKG kind accepted, and only then does the responder perform its first private DKG step (round-1 secret generation).
2. **Round-1 crossing is once-per-session-id**: each party sends DKGRound1 at most once per session id; an *exact retry* means a **byte-identical complete envelope** (digest-identical, per §7.3 retry accounting), and any second **different** DKGRound1 for the same session id aborts. Rationale (corrected): at N=2 there is no cross-recipient equivocation to defend against — the binding that matters is the session/context commitment plus once-per-session uniqueness.
3. **Round-2 targeted/confidential, session-memory only**: DKGRound2 travels only over the direct authenticated connection, is never relayed, and — beyond "no logging" — is **never persisted to any durable store, retry journal or event stream**; the body is kept in session-scoped memory and zeroized after delivery or abort (the `ziquid-threshold` module now returns it in a zeroizing buffer). It is refused if the peer's DKGRound1 for this session id was never received.
4. **No dealer kind**: the amendment deliberately provides no trusted-dealer message (`generate_with_dealer` stays out; API-absence invariant).
5. **Transcript commitment (kind 12)**: after finalize, each side sends `SHA256(PublicKeyPackage)`; both peers must observe the same digest before any signing session starts.
6. **`context_digest` canonical preimage and check**: `SHA256("Z2Z_DKG_CTX\0" || lane_id:u16BE || session_id[32] || initiator_coord_key[32] || responder_coord_key[32] || chain_context_digest[32] || pool_id:u8 || canonical J-param bytes)`. The session/identity fields are defense-in-depth (the envelope already carries them); **pool and J parameters ride nowhere else**, which is why the digest is required. The **receiver must recompute and compare** the digest on kinds 10–12 and abort with reason 1 on mismatch — required to agree on the **pool pin and J parameters before DKGRound1** (they cannot be hashed otherwise), i.e. §9.7's pin and the J-parameter freeze precede the ceremony.
7. **Abort semantics**: kind 8 with reason 1 (invalid-peer-data) for malformed, equivocated or digest-mismatched DKG bodies.

### 12.2 Session-layer prerequisites — status after the 2026-10-07 implementation wave

The transport review's gating findings are now implemented as the §7 session state machine (`crates/runtime/src/node/session.rs`: `SessionTable`, `Session`, `Admitted`), which replaced the process-global dedup and the initiator-key-only check:

1. **Direction-aware admission — done for the implemented direction.** The gate verifies against the peer's §7.2 role key (the initiator key for responder-side sessions). The node runs only responder-side sessions today: it answers inbound Hellos and gates established messages of those transcripts. **Node-initiated outbound sessions (dial → Hello response → session completion) remain unimplemented** — they have no driver yet; the one-shot `send-hello` client keeps its own equivalent checks.
2. **Response-path gating — done.** Inbound responses run the same table admission as requests; with no outbound sessions, unknown sessions reject there too.
3. **Per-sender seq — done for the peer direction.** Strictly next from the peer, no wrap (checked add; exhaustion closes the session); exact retries are idempotent through the digest check and never consume seq.
4. **Per-session dedup scope — done.** Each session owns a ≤1024 SHA256(envelope) table; at capacity the negotiation closes instead of forgetting. The process-global table is gone.
5. **Counterparty binding — done at transport level.** A session binds PeerId ↔ both coordination keys ↔ all pins; messages for a session from another peer reject, changed pins end the negotiation, and a closed connection drops the peer's sessions. The application-level *selected-counterparty* check before release of DKG secret material remains for the DKG wave (the transport now records everything needed to enforce it).

Also implemented from the §7.3 table: 4 global sessions / 1 per PeerId including Hello (exact Hello retries idempotent), 30s unconfirmed-session waiting timeout, 1800s monotonic lifetime, and the §7.2 transcript-confirmation gate — the initiator's first established message must be the `Ack` carrying `SHA256(signed bytes)` of our signed Hello response before any financial kind is accepted, else the negotiation ends.

**Evidence (2026-10-07):** 12 `node::session` unit tests (bootstrap limits/exact-retry, pins/peer/auth/seq rejections, confirmation + idempotent aged retry + financial-kind opening, abort closure, lifetime/drop rules); `cargo test -p ziquid-runtime --lib` 52/52 with the documented fixture env; clippy clean; two-node smoke — 6 sequential `send-hello` handshakes accepted by the real node (0 rejects; the 4-session cap not hit proves close-drops-sessions), and a lane-7 Hello rejected (`session_rejected`, no `session_request`) with the client exiting nonzero.

The proposed DKG kinds (§12.1) still need spec-owner approval before implementation, and the proving/privacy gates are untouched by this wave.
