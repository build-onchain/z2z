## The sentence the judge should remember

**Cross tokenized-stock orders directly.** This is the exact canonical one-liner. The longer explanation is: buyers and sellers of the *same mint* can meet in a periodic call auction before the unfilled remainder, if opted in, reaches a separately funded maker quote. Whether this beats executable alternatives after all costs is **U**; see [market and competition](../business/10-MARKET-AND-COMPETITION.md) and [validation](../business/12-GTM-AND-VALIDATION.md).

The customer is a distributor that can route eligible holders' orders, not every stock investor. The economic question is whether that distributor repeatedly sees two-sided, same-mint interest in the same minute, and whether both sides improve net versus an executable route. Neither condition is proved (E17–E18, U). Kerb is a Solana-track *submission candidate*, not a live market.

No exact mainnet xStock mint has been checked against Kerb's extension/authority allowlist. **Production eligibility is BLOCKED**; whether any candidate xStock lacks a permanent delegate and meets all other requirements is **U**. Every footage scene must use a distinctly labelled **SYNTHETIC** test mint, never a named xStock presented as eligible.

## Story spine for a spoken pitch

- **Once upon a time…** A distributor serving eligible tokenized-stock holders had a buyer and seller seeking to trade the same mint within a short window. This is a hypothetical situation, not an observed Kerb customer.
- **Every day…** Current executable choices include maker quotes through JupiterZ and AMMs; xStocks xChange is issuer issuance/redemption for onboarded clients during its operating window, not the same secondary crossing service. Two users may each pay for dealer intermediation, but the incidence and cost are unmeasured (E1–E3, U for Kerb savings).
- **One day…** Kerb proposes a one-minute, same-mint auction: buyer locks quote tokens, seller locks raw base tokens, and the program computes the single primary price from their limits. No external feed sets that price.
- **Because of that…** When opposite limits overlap, eligible user orders can cross at a common price; only afterwards can opted-in remainder meet a pre-funded maker at the maker's own quoted price. The residual price can differ; a primary cross cannot be repriced by the maker.
- **Until finally…** If repeated flow, net bilateral improvement, maker participation and lawful distribution are demonstrated, a distributor could offer another execution route. If those gates fail, this story ends at a synthetic experiment rather than a deployed venue.

This is an adaptation of the story-spine sequence in Ashwin Kumar, [“Nail Your Startup Pitch: Use Pixar's Story Formula”](https://startuppitch.substack.com/p/nail-your-startup-pitch-use-pixars) (retrieved 2026-09-28); the source proposes a traction beat, but Kerb has no traction to insert. Do not invent it.

### Demonstration sequence, not a slide masquerading as software

1. Establish exact base mint, quote mint, the separately recorded token programs, lot and tick; show funded opposite-side orders and the epoch boundary. The receipt must label `CROSS`, the computed uniform price and filled quantity; inspect actual instruction/event output, not a hand-edited screenshot.
2. Leave a remainder deliberately; only an order with `allow_residual = true` may use a signed, fully pre-funded maker ladder posted while Open. Show distinct `RESIDUAL` price and receipt. A zero-maker variant must still finish the primary auction.
3. With no cross in a **Cleared** epoch, recover unspent escrow via `settle_order`/`settle_maker` where transfers permit; separately exercise a missed-clear-deadline **pre-clear** `RefundOnly` path using `refund` (which is never callable after `Cleared`) and an extension-hash or multiplier-window refusal before clearing. Establish one base and quote PDA vault pair **per epoch**. V1 refuses any mint with a permanent delegate at market creation; whether that excludes candidate xStock mints is **U** until each is checked. In an adversarial **SYNTHETIC** vault-balance shortfall scenario (without listing a prohibited permanent-delegate mint), show `VaultShortfall`, the affected leg `Impaired`, stopped payouts and unchanged nominal claims (original deposits pre-clear, entitlements post-clear) while the unaffected leg keeps settling. There is no recovery path; claims may never be recoverable. After `Cleared`, show `settle_*` preflight detect a fixed entitlement account's frozen vault/account, paused mint or missing/closed canonical recipient account: it succeeds without transfer, emits `SettlementBlocked(reason, leg, slot)` and leaves the entitlement `Unpaid`. The marker is non-sticky and never gates retry; a later successful transfer settles the leg. Wrong destination/mint/program hard-reverts, never records a block. Unexpected CPI error after passing preflight reverts; the off-chain crank/indexer reports an incident, not a separate marking instruction. A frozen issuer vault can strand funds indefinitely. Do not call this production xStock support.
4. Show a comparison *method*—paired executable quotes, all-in fee and latency—not a fabricated saving. No real quote comparison has been observed (U). See [test plan](../security/08-TEST-AND-VERIFICATION-PLAN.md).
