## Instrument identity and amount discipline

A Kerb market identifies the **exact** `(base_mint, base_token_program, quote_mint, quote_token_program)`; normally the base will be Token-2022 and the quote is qualified USDC under its actual owning token program, not implicitly Token-2022. Distinct issuer certificates, chains or mints for one equity ticker are different instruments and never net. Mint owner, decimals, all TLV extension types/values, authority pubkeys and vault/account initialization state are program-verified. Unknown extension tags/unsupported layouts fail closed, not decoded with permissive default values.

One lot = `base_lot_raw` raw base atoms, regardless of displayed token amount. One tick = `tick_quote` raw quote atoms **per lot**. `q` lots at `p` ticks entails exactly `q·p·tick_quote` quote atoms. Never compute escrow, fills, limit satisfaction or fee from a floating point `uiAmount`, a `getTokenAccountBalance` display value, a ticker, or a rounded shares figure. Scaled UI Amount can change displayed balances without changing the raw token-account amount; its conversion helpers use floating-point arithmetic and are not guaranteed to round-trip exactly ([Solana Scaled UI Amount](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/scaled-ui-amount), retrieved 2026-09-28). User UI shows **both** raw atoms/lots and issuer-adjusted display indication with the observed multiplier, timestamp and update authority; only raw quantities are binding.

**Illustrative**: if `base_lot_raw=1,000`, `tick_quote=1,000` quote atoms and an ask sells two lots for 5 ticks, the transfer is 2,000 raw base atoms against 10,000 quote atoms. A multiplier change from 1 to 1.1 alters the corresponding displayed exposure but not those 2,000 raw atoms or 10,000 quote atoms. Pricing across the transition without a guard would change the economic meaning of the same raw tick. xStocks documentation states Solana on-chain balance stays constant while the UI multiplier changes, with updates published and activation at 00:30 UTC after the ex-date; venues are advised to pause around activation ([xStocks dividends and stock splits](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/dividends-and-stock-splits.md), retrieved 2026-09-28; canonical E5). This is issuer documentation, **not** a current on-chain observation of a candidate mint.

## Proposed extension allowlist

“Conditional” means **no market** until the named predicate is proven on the exact mint and source/vault/destination accounts, encoded in program checks and issuer-risk disclosure; it is not a blanket allow. “Refuse” means V1 cannot process that configuration. Inventory uses Token-2022's first-party [extension enumeration](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions) (retrieved 2026-09-28). The qualification record must show both mint-level and required token-account extensions; some account extensions are induced by the mint at initialization. A hash alone is not enough to prove a transfer is currently possible.

| Extension / authority | V1 policy | Reason and check |
|---|---|---|
| `ScaledUiAmount` | **Conditional** | Read current/next multiplier, update authority and `new_multiplier_effective_timestamp`; enforce activation envelope on-chain; do not use scaled UI amount in arithmetic. |
| `Pausable` and induced `PausableAccount` | **Conditional** | Read paused state at open/close/clear and settlement preflight; paused mint rejects transfers, mint and burns ([Solana Pausable Mint](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/pausable), retrieved 2026-09-28). Store pause authority in fingerprint. |
| `PermanentDelegate` | **Refuse any mint with delegate set in V1** | Mint-level delegate can transfer/burn **any** vault holdings without owner signature and can rotate ([Solana Permanent Delegate](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/permanent-delegate), retrieved 2026-09-28). Fingerprint does not detect delegate exercising existing privilege. Verify absence at market creation and guard checks. Future reviewed exception is a new policy, blocked until audit and counsel review. |
| Mint freeze authority / `DefaultAccountState` | **Conditional only when every funding/payout vault and destination is initialized and unfrozen at operation time** | Mint may make new accounts Frozen by default; prearranged thaw/issuer cooperation may be required. A Frozen vault or recipient blocks transfer; freeze authority is in fingerprint. Refuse market if predictable unfrozen vaults and return path cannot be demonstrated, rather than promise withdrawal. On-going per-account freeze is a runtime guard, not fully covered by mint fingerprint. |
| `ConfidentialTransferMint` and induced `ConfidentialTransferAccount` | **Conditional only for public balances and public `transfer_checked` route** | Mere mint extension presence does not prove confidential activity or compatible public transfers. Require read of public balances and any confidential pending/available/withheld state on all relevant accounts; V1 refuses a flow requiring non-zero confidential balances, deposit/apply/withdraw/proof account handling, or hidden accounting. No confidentiality feature in Kerb. [Solana Confidential Balances](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer), retrieved 2026-09-28. |
| `TransferHook` / `TransferHookAccount` | **Refuse in V1** | Reject either base or quote mint carrying `TransferHook`, whether its hook program is set or unset. A hook authority can repoint the program and a pinned program can be upgraded before a later payout CPI; a CPI revert is not a safety boundary against a hook that succeeds. [INFERENCE] Token-2022 extensions are fixed at mint initialization, so a mint without `TransferHook` cannot gain one later; re-verify against the exact token-program version at qualification. Hook support requires a future reviewed, audited amendment ([Solana Transfer Hook](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/transfer-hook), retrieved 2026-09-28). |
| `TransferFeeConfig`, `TransferFeeAmount`, `ConfidentialTransferFeeConfig/Amount` | **Refuse** | Withholding changes received raw atom counts; simple fixed lot/quote entitlements would not equal spendable balances. A distinct reviewed fee-aware design would be a new version, not a client-side adjustment ([Solana Transfer Fees](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/transfer-fees), retrieved 2026-09-28). |
| `InterestBearingConfig` | **Refuse** | Display economics change with time under a distinct mechanism; incompatible with Scaled UI Amount on one mint per first-party docs; no equivalent V1 guard. |
| `NonTransferable`, `NonTransferableAccount` | **Refuse** | A spot transfer between owner and vault, then buyer, is impossible. |
| `CpiGuard` | **Refuse for involved accounts unless proven transfer path remains valid** | Account-level CPI restrictions may prohibit program-mediated transfers; qualify actual source/recipient account state and stop before escrow when incompatible. |
| `MemoTransfer` | **Refuse when required on involved receiving accounts unless exact transaction memo flow is reviewed** | Otherwise placement or payout fails in a way unrelated to auction price; do not require a user's unsignaled sidecar instruction. |
| `ImmutableOwner` | **Allow on involved token accounts** | Prevents owner mutation; still verify account owner and mint on every transfer and qualification. |
| `MintCloseAuthority`, `ConfidentialMintBurn` | **Refuse pending separate review** | Mint closure or confidential supply changes are outside the bounded public-balance transfer policy. |
| `MetadataPointer`, `TokenMetadata`, group/member pointers and metadata | **Conditional** | Do not infer market identity from mutable display name or group; bind mint pubkey and review mutable pointer/authority changes and recipient-account behavior. No price or eligibility decision from untrusted metadata. |
| Unknown or future extension type/authority | **Refuse** | Fail closed until parser, settlement semantics, CU and security review are updated. |

USDC is **not automatically safe**: qualify exact quote mint/program, decimals, freeze/delegate authority and any extension against the same requirements, including refusal of a set permanent delegate. Refuse the quote mint if it carries `TransferHook` (program set or unset), has fee-on-transfer or confidential-required balances, or has unsupportable freeze/account state. A qualified base does not qualify a quote and vice versa.

**U: candidate xStock extension composition.** Stockless-like prior research reported xStock mints carrying `ConfidentialTransferMint` and `DefaultAccountState`, but Kerb has **not re-read exact mint accounts on-chain** and does not claim a current universal xStock extension set. An initialized (not frozen) `DefaultAccountState` and purely public balances might qualify **only after** live-mint TLV/account-state verification and exact public transfer tests; frozen-by-default vaults without reliable thaw/return **fail**. A required nonzero confidential balance/proof path **fails**. No market is considered qualified merely because the issuer's general documentation names Scaled UI Amount.

**Production eligibility: BLOCKED.** No exact mainnet xStock or other issuer mint has passed the allowlist, and this draft does not establish eligibility for any named xStock. Every demo must use test mints labelled **SYNTHETIC** until an exact mint clears byte-level policy, issuer operational review and counsel gates. No blanket qualification follows from the issuer's product family.

## Activation-window and guard protocol

At candidate market creation, read **both** mint accounts at an explicit slot and parse their extension sets plus mint/freeze/permanent-delegate/transfer-hook/pause/scaled-UI-update authorities. Reject either mint with `TransferHook`, whether the hook program is set or unset. Form the canonical fingerprint over normalized extension **types and required authority values**; persist full qualification evidence off-chain. Dynamic values such as the pause flag, frozen token-account state and scheduled multiplier timestamp are separately re-read and guarded, not treated as immutable authority identity. Re-check each mint's extension/authority fingerprint at `open_epoch`, `close` and `clear`. An opening mismatch blocks new epochs pending requalification; a mismatch before clearing an existing epoch sends it to `RefundOnly`; a fingerprint change after `Cleared` blocks new epochs until requalification but does not change results or block `settle_*`, which still runs freeze/pause/recipient preflight.

Let `g` be market-configured guard seconds and `d=clear_deadline_ts`. `open_epoch` MUST refuse if a scheduled `new_multiplier_effective_timestamp` falls within **inclusive** `[open_ts−g,d+g]`, including an issuer-reported schedule that is later than close but earlier than clearance deadline. If an affected schedule appears while an epoch is Open/Closed, `close`/`clear` or permissionless guard evaluation makes it `RefundOnly` before result persistence. A schedule outside that interval is not permission to ignore unscheduled immediate issuer changes: reread mint state at close/clear; operator suspends new epochs when uncertain. The issuer can update immediately according to Token-2022 docs; no contract can assume the published schedule never changes. An activation during post-clear settlement does not retroactively reprice fixed raw entitlements, but may require warning/blocked-leg review. Disclosure must show the raw amount and that displayed exposure can differ after issuer action.

If a mint is paused or a vault/recipient frozen **before** clear, enter RefundOnly where recoverable; if the vault itself is frozen, even a RefundOnly CPI can fail and the user awaits issuer thaw, potentially indefinitely. A pre-clear vault shortfall instead makes the affected leg `Impaired`: each escrow owner keeps a nominal claim equal to the original deposit, all payouts from that leg stop, and the claim may never be recoverable. `refund` is callable only in `RefundOnly`; `settle_order`/`settle_maker` are callable only after `Cleared`. Each runs deterministic preflight over the entitlement-fixed epoch vault, mint and canonical recipient token account for vault/recipient frozen, mint paused or recipient missing/closed. The caller cannot substitute a destination, mint or token program: such a mismatch hard-reverts without recording a block. A proven obstacle succeeds with no transfer, emits `SettlementBlocked(reason, leg, slot)` and stores an advisory, non-sticky marker; the entitlement remains `Unpaid`. Every retry rechecks preflight; the first passing preflight and successful transfer settles the leg. Anyone may recreate a missing canonical recipient account. An unexpected CPI error after a passing preflight reverts, leaving the entitlement untouched and retryable; crank/indexer surface an off-chain incident, not an on-chain block. If a per-epoch vault's actual raw balance is below all **outstanding nominal entitlements** on its leg, record `Impaired` and `VaultShortfall` (uncovered amount), stop payouts on that leg and preserve nominal claims; the solvent leg continues. V1 has no recovery path. Excess direct deposits over recorded escrow are quarantined, not fee income or user entitlements. Quarantine/sweep and impaired recovery remain open policies (canonical §3.9). If an equity-market halt is merely *reported off-chain*, the team's crank/gateway avoids new epochs and users may cancel while Open, but V1 has **no on-chain trading-halt oracle**; full halt policy is also open (§3.9). User disclosures must not equate issuer token pause with exchange halt or imply guaranteed fund availability under issuer control.

## Per-mint qualification checklist (one record per exact address)

- [ ] Record chain/cluster, slot and UTC time; copy base/quote mint pubkeys, mint owners/token program IDs, decimals, mint/freeze and all extension authorities. Verify issuer documentation and legal/distribution eligibility separately; do not infer eligibility from bytecode.
- [ ] Decode **all** mint TLVs and required account TLVs with first-party-compatible versioned parser; compare to the above allowlist. Record `DefaultAccountState` actual value (Initialized/Frozen), confidential extension public/hidden balance requirements, presence of `TransferHook` on either leg (reject even if hook program unset), pause flag, next scaled multiplier and effective timestamp. Candidate xStock names are not proof.
- [ ] Snapshot canonical hash of extension types and designated authorities, and include exact checked parsing specification in program and SDK. Simulate authority rotation, extension-hash change and time-window overlap for `open_epoch`, `close` and `clear`; reject unknown/missing extension bytes. Re-verify [INFERENCE] that a mint lacking `TransferHook` cannot gain the extension after initialization against the exact token-program version.
- [ ] Qualify a base deposit and **both** base return paths (pre-clear refund and post-clear payout) and quote deposit/payout/fee-vault transfer with actual token-program IDs and required accounts. Check starting/ending spendable raw atoms exactly; test frozen vault and recipient, pause, **refusal of a `TransferHook` mint on either leg with set or unset hook program**, **refusal of a mint with set permanent delegate**, simulated post-escrow shortfall and per-leg `Impaired` stop. Use synthetic local tests until live permissions exist; no production claim from a successful simulation.
- [ ] Verify vault account initialization/thaw prerequisites and on-chain per-account statuses for default frozen state; obtain issuer cooperation if required before considering a market. Disclose that an issuer may later immobilize vault assets despite Kerb's permissionless instructions.
- [ ] Size `base_lot_raw`, `tick_quote`, deposits and fee reserve under checked `u128→u64` bounds at the **provisional scale targets** of 64 orders and 8 levels/side × 4 makers; run LiteSVM/SBF CU and transaction-size benchmarks. Hackathon MVP bounds are ≤8 user orders, 1 maker and 1 level/side; settle/refund one recipient leg per instruction across transactions. Ensure output quote mint has no unsupported fee or movement side effect.
- [ ] Record an explicit guard duration and clear deadline. Halt handling remains off-chain and its detailed policy is open (canonical §3.9). Require counsel review of issuer/jurisdiction/market structure before live eligibility ([`../legal/09-REGULATORY-AND-LEGAL.md`](../legal/09-REGULATORY-AND-LEGAL.md)); no mint here is currently approved.
