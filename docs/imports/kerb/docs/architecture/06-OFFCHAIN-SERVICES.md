## Current library and private-state operations — docs direction adopted 2026-10-01

Reuse [Zoss source/context/domain/Solana client libraries](../../../../../../zoss/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) **inside Kerb-controlled scanner/coordinator/client processes**, not a new mandatory service. Source acquisition must use an actual pinned coherent upstream cohort; parse/decrypt yields caller-private observation. Kerb must reject unknown/gapped/reorged/wrong receiver or pool data, verify claimant authorization and controlled unallocated inventory, then durably deduplicate stable occurrence/credit issuance before funding any hold. Scanner IVK detection alone is not outgoing-spend/nullifier/witness inventory or accepted-history validation. FAA, MPC, entitlement/hold journals, inventory locks, FROST nonces/native intents, both-chain finality, payout/refund and recovery remain Kerb implementations and authority.

Operational ownership is explicit: viewing capability stays with Kerb's designated scanner/custody domains; spending shares, ordinary Solana authorizer keys and Arcis SHA3-512 certificate keys stay separate. No cross-app IVK/FVK, private note/claimant maps, cache, witness store, DB or common key daemon is created by library reuse. Logs/metrics/backups/support exports must not carry raw viewing/signing secrets, decrypted memo/source locators, full certificates/PCZT/witnesses or order↔funding/result maps into matcher, Ziquid or optional Zoss-runtime access. Public data fetches may be shared only without changing caller-private state ownership; each caller retains isolated cursors/reorg handling/private retention.

The Solana client library returns exact caller-selected unsigned submission requests and reconciled inclusion/finality observations. Kerb still checks program/cluster/method/accounts, authorizer and fee-payer authority, finality and business transition before state changes. Unknown submission/inclusion/history fails closed for money; shared client code cannot synthesize `Complete`, reset journals or sign spends. Restart/restore must reconcile Kerb's non-rollback business-intent/signing-nonce history and live input/change reservations, not recreate credit from scans or callbacks.

[Optional Zoss message diagnosis](../../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md) remains **OFF / non-money**, with no required watchers/receipt/quorum/relay/daemon or viewing-key handoff. Kerb workflows must operate with the **message runtime disconnected**, even if they use compiled shared libraries. Any later enabled same-note diagnostic needs separate viewing/access/privacy/runtime evidence; reconnect has zero credit/hold/payout/refund authority. Atomic destination rollback permits only the same immutable still-valid receipt retry, not cross-chain recovery. Core one-source→one-receipt scope across routes/deployments is unresolved; Kerb's financial credit/portion/native-intent uniqueness stays independent. Funded evidence/witness/recovery lifetime cannot be shortened by message epoch/revocation or a library/SDK upgrade.

The [Oct1 production-demo candidate](../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-kerb-production-demo-design.md) is not an executable runbook; known **GD2 structural FAIL** blocks private funding under the unchanged requirement. [Zoss BLOCKERS](../../../../../../zoss/docs/BLOCKERS.md) alone owns core readiness; Kerb operations/privacy/financial gates are neither duplicated there nor passed by this docs cutover. The following non-custodial services/runbooks retain the different historical public-Solana proposal.

## Historical public-Solana non-custodial service boundary

**Not the current cross-chain operating model.** The non-custodial crank/gateway/maker roles below belong to the old Solana-only proposal. The cross-chain candidate additionally depends on private admission, MPC and committee custody; those are not removed by library reuse. See [Zolana concept and actor review](../reviews/2026-10-02-zolana-concept-and-actors.md) for required roles, transaction submission and user stories.

Nothing here is required to trust a single backend. Clients can read program-owned market/epoch accounts and send wallet-authorized placements/cancellations; any funded operator can close, clear, settle and refund within program guards. A distributor gateway builds and relays transactions but holds **no user or maker signing key**, cannot change signed limit/nonce, cannot choose `p*`, and cannot claim finality from an indexer's pending state. The issuer controls mint-level transfer abilities separately; redundant cranks cannot override issuer pause/freeze. No counterparties, real markets, issuer integrations or mainnet deployments exist yet.

Production eligibility is **BLOCKED**: no exact mainnet xStock or other issuer mint has been checked against the allowlist. Demos must use test mints labelled **SYNTHETIC**; a receipt from such a demo is not evidence of executable production issuer integration.

Capacity is unbenchmarked: 64 user orders per epoch and 8 levels per side × 4 makers are provisional scale targets pending LiteSVM/SBF CU and transaction-size measurements. The hackathon MVP bounds are ≤8 user orders, 1 maker and 1 level per side. Cranks must split one-recipient-leg settle/refund instructions across transactions rather than bundle an epoch into one call.

## Crank scheduling and exactly-once effects

Run at least two independently deployed crank instances with separate RPC connections and fee payer accounts; they coordinate **only as an optimization**. Permissionlessness is the actual failover, not a guarantee of completion: issuer freezes, mint pause or impairment can make a leg stuck indefinitely or unrecoverable. Each worker reads finalized or suitably confirmed epoch state with fork awareness and simulates the *specific* next call before paying a network fee. It may use compact sorted-index hints, while the program sorts and sweeps distinct submitted limits in O(n log n), or O(n) after verifying a hint; no per-tick scan and no crank-supplied outcome. Use jitter and bounded backoff for account locks, `AlreadyClosed`, `AlreadyCleared`, `AlreadyPaid`, stale blockhash and RPC timeout. Persist job key/last observed slot/transaction signature for operator diagnostics, not to suppress a legitimate retry from another worker. If both submit the same lifecycle instruction, one may pay for an already-done response; on-chain paid bits and epoch states prevent double spend.

For `Open`, schedule `close` at first observed chain timestamp ≥`close_ts`; do not rely solely on local wall clock. For `Closed`, submit `clear` before `clear_deadline_ts`; an ordinary guard/deadline failure triggers RefundOnly, but vault insolvency instead marks the affected leg `Impaired`. For `Cleared`, enumerate unpaid entitlement legs and settle one recipient leg per instruction across transactions; each epoch has isolated base and quote vaults, and absent issuer shortfall no other recipient can consume an unpaid entitlement. `SettlementBlocked(reason, leg, slot)` is emitted only by a successful no-transfer deterministic preflight over entitlement-fixed vault, mint and canonical recipient account (vault/recipient frozen, mint paused, or recipient missing/closed). It is advisory and non-sticky: `Unpaid` remains and every retry rechecks. Wrong destination/mint/program hard-reverts, never records a block; a missing canonical account may be recreated permissionlessly. The first passing preflight with successful transfer marks `Settled`. `Impaired` records `VaultShortfall`, preserves nominal claims and **stops all payouts on that leg**; if pre-clear, each owner has a nominal claim equal to the original deposit that may never be recoverable. The solvent leg continues. Unexpected CPI failure after passing preflight reverts, keeps the entitlement retryable and is an **off-chain incident**: crank/indexer record signature, slot, accounts, simulation/logs and alert operators, without guessing a block reason or creating on-chain state. In solvent `RefundOnly`, issue one-leg refunds of original deposits where token rules allow. Fee claims use recorded floor splits; which caller earns the crank 1/10 and how is open (canonical §3.9). No cleanup job may close an unpaid ledger.

Crank fee revenues are at most a 1/10 share of **each integer user fee**, rounded down; attribution is open, not assumed to follow a particular settlement caller. At small notionals the share can be zero while network costs remain positive; redundancy is not economically self-funding by assumption. [INFERENCE] Budget externally for prototype operation and test incentives before claiming practical liveness. If all cranks disappear, owners/other users still have program rights; they may have to pay transaction fees, and issuer/token restrictions still govern transfers.

## Indexer and receipt schema

Persist append-only raw observation `(cluster, program_id, slot, blockhash, signature, instruction_index, event_index, commitment, tx_version)`, decoded account snapshot hash and a versioned derived ledger view keyed by `(market,epoch,order owner,nonce)` or `(maker,ladder nonce)`. Maintain a rooted checkpoint, re-fetch accounts/events for skipped slots, and reconcile across RPC providers when observations disagree. A successful signature is not a paid leg unless on-chain paid bit and token balances support it. For v1 transaction reads pass integer `maxSupportedTransactionVersion: 1` to `getTransaction`/`getBlock`/`blockSubscribe`; parse v1 `transactionConfig`, not only ComputeBudget instructions. Solana's [larger-transaction-sizes guide](https://solana.com/upgrades/larger-transaction-sizes) (retrieved 2026-09-28) documents v1 activation and indexer pitfalls. A `block: null` with error is an ingestion fault, not an empty block.

An execution receipt SHOULD expose:

- exact base/quote mint and program addresses, epoch ID, slot and commitment/root status, signed user limit/lots, `allow_residual`, recorded fee and raw deposit;
- CROSS `V*`, `[p_lo,p_hi]`, marginal-plateau midpoint `p*`, this user's filled lots/raw base and quote notional; **separate** RESIDUAL fills with each maker-level ticks, lots and raw quote notional; all three primary prices absent when no primary cross;
- total fee assessed **once per user order on aggregate CROSS+RESIDUAL notional** (`ceil(5N/10_000)`), with phase-attribution convention, unused deposit, paid vs advisory non-sticky `SettlementBlocked` vs `Impaired` nominal entitlement legs, uncovered atoms/reason/last attempted slot, and fee-vault dust separately; issuer fingerprint and known multiplier-effective timestamp at observation;
- `route_comparison.status` in `{OBSERVED_EXECUTABLE, OBSERVED_INDICATIVE, UNAVAILABLE}` with exact mint/program, side/size, quoted amount, provider, query timestamp, validity window, eligibility/jurisdiction conditions, network/route fee, and whether an **actual external fill** happened. An observed quote is not an executed counterfactual; a historical sample is not executable. If route is unavailable, display `UNAVAILABLE`, **not** an inferred saving or a zero fee.

No “best execution,” guaranteed saving, hidden order, issuer approval or production-liquidity claim belongs in the receipt. If the only external route quote is observed after the Kerb trade or for a different size, it is contextual, not a like-for-like result. Build route comparisons only with explicit cohort/jurisdiction eligibility and error bars when comparing delayed observations; show raw quote atoms rather than rounded display as the canonical economic quantity. See [`../business/12-GTM-AND-VALIDATION.md`](../business/12-GTM-AND-VALIDATION.md) for validation gates.

## SDK and gateway contract

The SDK obtains exact mint account owner, TLV set and authority snapshot from RPC, verifies the program's published market params and explains issuer controls, token decimals, raw lots/ticks and multiplier. It computes the maximum bid escrow `q·limit·tick_quote+ceil(5·q·limit·tick_quote/10_000)` with checked big integers, asks for `q·base_lot_raw`, and renders both raw and display amounts. It lets the wallet sign exact args/account metas; the signer sees mint/program addresses, fee estimate, residual opt-in, cancel cutoff and disclosure that issuer may freeze assets. No approval of mint eligibility is inferred from the UI. A stale fingerprint blocks **new** order construction while allowing direct cancel/refund/settle attempts under on-chain rules.

Gateway endpoints, if used, are read/status, unsigned-build and signed-relay only. Verify signed message bytes, expected cluster/program, max transaction fee, blockhash age, version/wallet support, account metas and duplicate nonce before relay; these checks protect gateway users but do not substitute for on-chain validation. Do not collect wallet secrets, custody assets, or issue server signatures on behalf of users/makers. A client can route to a different RPC and independently compute deposit amounts. A gateway cannot promise that a user will remain eligible to receive an issuer-controlled token after close.

## Runbooks

**Stuck epoch.** Read rooted state, **each epoch's** vault balances against outstanding nominal claims, exact guard/fingerprint and timestamps first. If Open past `close_ts`, send permissionless `close`; if Closed and before deadline, submit `clear` with verified sorted hint; wrong hint must not be retried unchanged. If not Cleared at deadline, invoke `trigger_refund_only` and refund original deposits **only on solvent, transferable legs**, one recipient leg per instruction across transactions. If an affected leg is short, record `Impaired` and `VaultShortfall`, stop its payouts; pre-clear owners retain nominal claims equal to original deposits that may never be recoverable. Other leg continues. If Cleared, never seek RefundOnly; enumerate unpaid solvent legs and retry bounded settlement. A deterministic blocked preflight emits an advisory, non-sticky `SettlementBlocked(reason, leg, slot)` without paying; wrong destination/mint/program hard-reverts. An unexpected CPI error after passing preflight leaves the entitlement `Unpaid` and is a crank/indexer off-chain incident, with logs and operator alert, not a block reason. Never label unresolved funds as refunded or invent restitution.

**Equity halt reported.** Record exact ticker/mint, report URL, observed timestamp, reporter confidence and expiry. Stop operator-initiated opening and gateway promotion of new epochs; explain that no on-chain halt oracle exists and public permissionless actions may still occur. Users may cancel only in Open. A closed/cleared epoch follows program-defined guard and settlement rules, not an ad hoc operator unwind. Halt source selection, open-epoch treatment and notification remain open (canonical §3.9); review legal/issuer instruction before resuming.

**Multiplier activation window.** Fetch mint TLV and issuer schedule, compare next effective timestamp with inclusive `[open_ts−guard,clear_deadline_ts+guard]`. Stop new openings in the window; before clear, invoke guard transition to RefundOnly; after clear, retain fixed raw entitlements, show timestamp/display-unit caveat and retry blocked transfers only when allowed. Recheck issuer schedule at each close/clear: immediate updates are possible. Do not reprice a cleared order from a UI display amount.

**Extension fingerprint change.** Verify on-chain at a rooted slot against market snapshot and independent RPC; the program re-checks both mint fingerprints at `open_epoch`, `close` and `clear`. Before Cleared, a mismatch triggers RefundOnly; after Cleared, preserve ledger and continue `settle_*` of fixed entitlements subject to freeze/pause/recipient preflight, while blocking another opening pending per-mint requalification. V1 refuses any base or quote mint carrying `TransferHook` at market creation, whether the hook program is set or unset; [INFERENCE] a mint without that extension cannot gain it after initialization, to be re-verified against the exact token-program version. Future hook support requires a reviewed, audited amendment. Different extension type or delegate is not waved through by same ticker/name.

**RPC outage / divergence.** Fail over to another independent provider, compare rooted hashes and program accounts, re-establish last rooted indexer checkpoint and replay missing slots. Continue permissionless rights through any available RPC; if none is available, show `UNKNOWN`, not “settled” or “safe.” Escalate approaching clear deadlines and communicate that the pre-clear refund right remains on-chain for solvent/transferable legs but cannot be exercised until submission is restored. Do not infer a halt, fill or route quote from absence of data.
