## Escrow, failure, fees and disclosure questions

10. Does the proposed PDA escrow create client-asset safeguarding, custody, client-money or money-transmission duties for upgrade controllers, distributors or other operators, despite no proposed admin withdrawal instruction? How do beneficiary title, segregated epoch vaults, reconciliations, bankruptcy remoteness, quote/base token-program differences, dispute resolution and settlement finality affect the assessment? What, if anything, does [FinCEN's 2019 CVC business-model guidance](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf) (P, retrieved 2026-09-28) address, and what does it leave unresolved for securities activity?
11. What disclosure and custody/remedy obligations arise if an issuer freezes the epoch vault or recipient account, pauses a mint, or otherwise prevents transfer? `refund` can be called permissionlessly only in `RefundOnly` before clear; `settle_order` and `settle_maker` only after `Cleared` (which never reverts to `RefundOnly`). No admin action is required to trigger these calls, but they complete only while token programs and issuer permit transfers; funds may be stranded indefinitely. Who must notify affected owners, hold records, handle complaints and discuss possible issuer action without promising release or a full refund?
12. How should disclosures distinguish `SettlementBlocked` from insolvency? An allowed `settle_*` or `refund` call preflights its fixed vault, mint and canonical recipient account; a detected freeze, pause or missing/closed canonical recipient account records an advisory, non-sticky reason and leaves the claim `Unpaid`, with retry if transfer later becomes possible. Wrong destination/mint/program reverts; an unexpected CPI failure reverts and is an off-chain incident. What notice, monitoring, incident and retry duties would apply without implying that retry guarantees transfer?
13. How should pre-clear `Impaired` claims be described? An affected escrow owner's nominal claim equals the original deposit, but payouts from that leg stop, there is **no V1 recovery path**, and the claim may never be recoverable; the unaffected leg may settle. For post-clear impairment, entitlements stay nominally unchanged with that leg's payouts stopped. Who, if anyone, might owe restitution, and what client-asset shortfall, separate-leg completion, records and remedies must be determined before real-asset use? Does a reviewed exception to the proposed refusal of permanent-delegate mints require independent audit and separate counsel review?
14. How should public warnings distinguish issuer-freeze-stranded funds from `Impaired` shortfalls, no immediate withdrawal, no guaranteed liquidity, no shareholder voting rights and no assumed issuer relationship? Which consent and presentation standards apply to original-deposit claims with no recovery path, residual fills at a maker price different from CROSS, and corporate-action changes to raw-unit value? What if off-chain halt reports cannot stop on-chain permissionless openings?
15. What legal, conflict, tax and disclosure obligations attach to the **proposed, untested** 5 bps/user-side fee, protocol/distributor/crank 6/3/1 split and any distributor rebate on **residual** fills? CROSS charges two user sides (about 10 bps of CROSS notional), but RESIDUAL charges one user side and a zero-fee maker (about 5 bps of RESIDUAL notional). Could residual fee plus rebate harm the user compared with trading that same maker directly? Should any rebate be prohibited or reduced, or residual fees lowered, pending [ADR-009](../product/16-ROADMAP-AND-DECISIONS.md)? Who earns the crank share remains an open design policy; does the eventual attribution change intermediary/agent classification?
16. Which person must pay transaction fees, KYC/onboard, verify beneficial ownership, screen sanctions/person/wallet risk, detect wash/self-trades, monitor market abuse and retain records in each jurisdiction? How might sanctions blocking obligations under [OFAC virtual currency FAQs](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/topic/1626) (P, retrieved 2026-09-28) conflict with permissionless settlement/refund? What operational or legal remedy exists without silently adding privileged seizure or refund-blocking powers contrary to the proposed governance rules?
17. How should public-chain order addresses, limits and activity and off-chain KYC, IP/location and trade histories be handled under relevant privacy law? Who is controller/processor for operator and distributor, what lawful basis, retention, minimization, breach and cross-border controls apply, and how does the [EDPB data-protection primer](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-protection-basics_en) (P, retrieved 2026-09-28) inform further counsel review?
18. How do market-abuse and halt/corporate-action obligations interact with the proposed program's off-chain-only halt reports and open halt-handling policy? What source, pause procedure and user notification would a regulated operator need? What about unsolicited vault surplus quarantine/sweep and unresolved crank-fee attribution under canonical §3.9?
