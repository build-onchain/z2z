## Current shared-library threat boundary — documentation update 2026-10-01

Adopted [Zoss library reuse](../../../../../../zoss/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) does not introduce a universal verifier, consensus client, proof engine or shared trusted service. `SourceObservation` must pass Kerb funding-owner/receiver/occurrence, canonical-history/finality, controlled unallocated inventory and durable credit-dedup checks. `QuorumAcceptedMessage` is optional message acceptance only; neither output can construct a financial verified fact or discharge FAA/MPC/ledger/FROST/payout/refund predicates. Ordinary wallet/Solana Ed25519, Arcis SHA3-512 certificate signatures and native FROST spend domains/keys remain distinct.

| Shared-reuse risk | Required Kerb boundary / evidence, not exercised |
|---|---|
| Parser/node/quorum output mistaken for funding or payment | Fail closed on unsupported context/history/ownership/inventory; credit only after Kerb's app checks and durable uniqueness. Real financial predicates stay in Kerb; source auth validity/history is not inferred from decryption or signatures. |
| Library convenience becomes an added viewing/key party | Compile inside Kerb-owned scanner/client processes; no mandatory shared IVK/FVK daemon, wallet, private cache or witness store. Explicit ACL/admin/support/recovery graph keeps all private note/claimant/order data outside matcher and cross-app access. Optional watchers would require separate viewing approval. |
| Cache/log/backups leak or co-mingle private state | Caller/per-pair isolated private state and cursors; no raw keys, witness/PCZT/certificate dumps or source↔order mappings in shared logging. A common public-data acquisition helper grants no decrypted-data access. |

## Scope, assets, and trust boundaries

**System under review:** a proposed Solana program for one exact base mint against a USDC quote mint per market, each with a separately bound token program; epoch-specific base/quote vaults hold funded user limit orders for a periodic call auction. The program calculates user-only `V*` and `p*` without an external oracle; after storing primary clearing it matches opted-in unfilled orders with funded maker levels at maker prices. Anyone may close and then clear after `Closed`; `settle_order` / `settle_maker` are callable only after `Cleared`, and `refund` only in `RefundOnly`, never after `Cleared`. Payout remains conditional on token-program and issuer transfer permission. Neither program ID, binary, deployed cluster, administrator signers, market mint addresses, nor implementation exists (**U**). No assertion here describes a completed audit or live venue.

**Assets at risk:** user bid quote escrow including reserved maximum fee; user ask raw Token-2022 base escrow; maker bid quote / maker ask raw base funding; unsettled user and maker entitlements; fee vault and protocol/distributor/crank fee shares; stored immutable clearing and allocation; order nonces, receipts, market/epoch identities, and eligibility state. External to the escrow: issuer certificate rights, issuer-controlled mint powers, the quote mint's ability to transfer, and any personal data collected by distributors.

| Boundary | Trusted for what | Untrusted inputs and privileged failure |
|---|---|---|
| Wallet → distributor UI/SDK → transaction signer | Wallet consent to exact market, epoch, side, raw lots, tick limit, residual opt-in, fee, and cancellation | UI/RPC can display wrong scaled quantity, route to a different mint or cluster, alter transaction bytes, or omit receipt distinction. A distributor is not an owner signer. |
| Caller/crank/maker → Kerb program state | Program-enforced authorization, escrow, ordering, and deterministic clearing | All instruction bytes, sort hints, account metas, remaining accounts, ladder levels, timestamps supplied by clients, transaction ordering, and fee recipients are adversarial. No solver-selected price. |
| Kerb → qualified base/quote token programs and issuer | Separately bound `base_token_program` and `quote_token_program`, with exact mint and authority qualification | Issuer may alter authorities, schedule multiplier, pause, freeze, or exercise permanent delegate. Any base or quote mint carrying `TransferHook` is refused at market creation in V1, set or unset; quote mint may use a different program and has issuer/freezer risk. |
| Program → RPC/indexer/receipts | On-chain finalized account state, not indexer assertions | An indexer can omit an epoch, forge display values, or report a transaction before finality. Off-chain halt signal is untrusted, not an on-chain halt oracle. |
| Governance/upgrade loader → program | Timelocked multisig only within the reviewed binary's rules | An authorized malicious upgrade can change rules or vault authority. The admin's intended pause is for *new orders only* and must not prevent refunds. |

**Actors/capabilities:** owners sign their own orders and Open-only cancellations; makers sign `(market,epoch)` ladder posts and Open-only cancellation and supply both-side inventory independently; any fee payer may crank/settle/refund on behalf of an owner but cannot redirect that owner's funds; distributors route and may receive a fixed rebate without owner rights; issuer/pause/freeze/delegate authorities and USDC issuer exercise token-program powers; governance signs timelocked upgrades / market additions / new-order pause; validators choose inclusion and slot timing; compromised UI, RPC or indexer can deceive or stall but must not determine economic state. Hook-bearing base/quote mints are refused in V1. Public orders expose side, size, limit and activity: this design has no confidentiality claim.

**Attacker goals:** take escrow or fee balances; obtain fills outside limits or at a chosen primary price; seize an unearned rebate; double settle or block recovery; exploit display/raw mismatch at a corporate event; extract price surplus by funded strategic orders; falsify a receipt; or censor settlement until timeout. Inclusion, Solana fees, issuer intervention, USDC credit/transferability, and securities permissions are outside program guarantees.

## Security invariants (design obligations, not verified)

| ID | Required property |
|---|---|
| I01 | Market isolation: every user/maker escrow, PDA, order, receipt and CPI source/destination binds exact market, epoch, base and quote mint and their respective qualified token programs; no ticker, substitute account, or foreign vault can redirect value. |
| I02 | Authorization/replay: owner or maker authorizes each funded obligation and Open-only cancellation; `(owner,market,epoch,nonce)` order identity is unique; a cancel/settle/refund consumes that obligation at most once, regardless of caller or transaction replay. |
| I03 | Pre-funding and conservation: bid locks `qty_lots·limit_ticks·tick_quote + max_fee` quote atoms (`max_fee = ceil(5·qty_lots·limit_ticks·tick_quote/10_000)`), ask locks `qty_lots·base_lot_raw` raw base atoms, maker levels lock aggregate exposure. Each epoch has its own two vaults. At clear, fixed entitlements reconcile to actual vault balances absent issuer-caused shortfall. Guards/settles test `vault_balance ≥ outstanding_entitlements` per leg; on shortfall record `VaultShortfall` and stop payouts from impaired leg, never invent restitution. |
| I04 | Primary result: for a fixed admissible order set and its `(placed_slot,nonce,owner)` metadata, `V*`, `p_lo`, `p_hi`, half-even marginal-plateau midpoint `p*`, eligible fills, price priority, marginal pro-rata, and leftover order are deterministic and independent of crank hint or clear-call account permutation. The maximizing ticks form a contiguous interval `[p_lo,p_hi]`, with `p_lo` an ask limit, `p_hi` a bid limit, and `p_lo ≤ p* ≤ p_hi` when `V* > 0`; an empty side or `V* = 0` means no cross and no price. Every CROSS fill obeys both user limits. |
| I05 | Residual isolation: only opted-in unfilled user remainders match posted, funded maker levels after stored primary; maker-level price obeys user limit; user priority and best-priced maker level are fixed. Across makers at the same price, the marginal-lot rule of §3.3 step 6 applies (proportional to remaining level quantity, floored, leftover lots by `(posted_slot, maker)`). `p*`, `V*`, CROSS fills are immutable. |
| I06 | Fee precision: each user order pays exactly one proposed `ceil(5·N/10_000)` quote-atom fee on aggregate filled notional `N = N_CROSS + N_RESIDUAL`; buyer pays `N+fee`, seller receives `N−fee`, maker pays zero. Protocol/distributor (or protocol fallback)/crank shares are individually floored from collected fees at 6/10, 3/10, 1/10, remainder stays as event-labelled fee-vault dust. Checked `u128` intermediate multiplication and bounded `u64` transfers prevent wrap/drain. |
| I07 | Lifecycle and conditional liveness: `Open → Closed → Cleared → per-leg Settled → Finalized`, or pre-clear `RefundOnly`, advisory per-leg `SettlementBlocked`, and per-leg `Impaired` on shortfall. No post-close cancellation or funding. `settle_order` / `settle_maker` are callable only after `Cleared`; `refund` only in `RefundOnly`, never after `Cleared`. After deadline/pre-clear guard, rightful owners retain a nominal claim equal to their original deposit; refund transfers occur only while token programs and issuer permit them. On pre-clear shortfall that leg is `Impaired`, pays nothing, and has no V1 recovery path; its nominal deposit claim may never be recoverable. No admin action is needed to trigger an ordinary refund/settlement, but issuer intervention can leave funds stuck indefinitely. Cleared results never revert to RefundOnly; settles are idempotent and bounded. |
| I08 | Issuer qualification: reject any V1 base or quote mint with a permanent delegate set, any `TransferHook` extension (program set or unset), or unreviewed extension. Hash both mint extension sets/authorities at creation and re-check at `open_epoch`, `close` and `clear`; multiplier overlap refuses open or causes pre-clear RefundOnly. Post-clear hash mismatch blocks new epochs until requalification but does not block `settle_*` of fixed entitlements, subject to freeze/pause/recipient preflight. Every guard/settle detects per-leg shortfall, marks `Impaired`, stops payouts from affected leg and emits `VaultShortfall`; unaffected leg may continue if transferable. |
| I09 | CPI/fee recipient integrity: token-program IDs, mint owners, token-account state/authority, PDA seeds and immutable distributor reference are checked; no hook extra accounts in V1 because hook-bearing mints are refused. Privileged fee-vault withdrawals cannot spend escrow or alter split. |
| I10 | Provisional resource bound/conditional liveness: hackathon MVP ≤8 orders, 1 maker, 1 level per side; scale targets 64 user orders, four makers with eight levels per side each, all pending LiteSVM/SBF CU and transaction-size benchmarks. Clear verifies a sorted hint and recomputes within measured limits; each settle/refund instruction handles one recipient leg. Permissionless calls do not depend on any particular crank or indexer, but payout depends on token-program and issuer transfer permission. |
| I11 | Receipts/truth: CROSS and RESIDUAL are separated with actual lot quantity, raw amounts, maker or uniform price, fees, and finality status; UI never treats an indexed event as proof of final settlement. |
| I12 | Entitlement isolation: after clear each fixed recipient's nominal base/quote/fee entitlement is paid at most once and never reweighted on preflight/CPI failure. `settle_*` only after `Cleared`, and `refund` only in `RefundOnly`, deterministically preflight the entitlement-bound vault, mint and recipient canonical token account for frozen vault/account, paused mint or missing/closed canonical account. A preflight block succeeds without transfer, records advisory non-sticky `SettlementBlocked(reason, leg, slot)` and leaves the leg `Unpaid`; the next passing preflight and successful transfer pays it. Wrong destination/mint/program reverts; unexpected CPI error after passing preflight reverts, leaving the entitlement retryable and requiring off-chain incident reporting. Issuer-caused shortfall makes the affected leg `Impaired`, with no payout or V1 recovery; nominal claims remain and the unaffected leg may continue. |

**Important limit:** I03's conservation applies absent issuer intervention. V1 rejects any mint with a permanent delegate set, but issuer-frozen vault or unexpected external vault depletion can still defeat availability. Solana's Token-2022 docs grant permanent delegates transfer/burn power and describe mint-wide Pausable transfer rejection; see [Permanent Delegate](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/permanent-delegate) and [Pausable Mint](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/pausable), retrieved 2026-09-28. The `Impaired` state prevents dishonest payouts; it is not a fund recovery mechanism.

## Release blockers and evidence grade

Release evidence terms are distinct: **audited** means independent review of an identified binary/scope; **tested** means a recorded executable case and observable assertion; **simulated** means a recorded local SVM/validator or operations rehearsal; **reasoned** means design analysis only. The current dossier is reasoned; **audited = none, tested = none, simulated = none**. No gate below is satisfied by this document.


| Gate | Required evidence before a real-money venue | Current grade (2026-09-28) |
|---|---|---|
| Asset and settlement correctness | Compiled program plus differential/proptest, stateful fuzz, adversarial CPI and balance-delta proofs for I01–I12, including issuer interventions. | **Reasoned only.** No implementation, tests or simulations exist. |
| Compute/conditional liveness | LiteSVM/SBF CU and transaction-size measurements for hackathon MVP ≤8 orders/1 maker/1 level per side and provisional scale target 64 orders/four makers/eight levels per side; verified deadline refund and one-leg settle without a privileged crank, conditional on issuer/token-program transfer permission. | **Reasoned only.** No CU results, no simulation. |
