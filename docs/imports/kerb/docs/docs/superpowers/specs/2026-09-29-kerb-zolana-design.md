## 1. Intent and immutable scope of this decision

Owner decisions: **one market architecture spanning Solana and Zcash**, three *separate* pairs, and a market in which **no single matching operator learns plaintext orders or participant identities from the matching workflow**; this is not a bilateral RFQ with a maker who reads the order. The owner also selected **threshold-committee custody for shielded ZEC** despite non-atomic execution and the loss of unilateral ZEC recovery. Pairs: (A) SYNTHETIC Solana test token / Ironwood ZEC on Zcash testnet in an authorized test scenario, (B) canonical Solana USDC / Ironwood ZEC, (C) exact qualified Solana xStock mint / Ironwood ZEC. `/` denotes denomination and intended exchange of native-chain assets, not wrapped ZEC, pooled liquidity, shared custody across pairs, or an already executable route. Each pair has separate orders, unit scale, limits, eligibility, committee inventory, proofs and settlement policy. The purpose is a real private spot-market thesis suitable for the *owner-provided* Zecathon “Private Markets — $15,000” theme; the [Idea Bank row #5](../../../sources/notion/idea-bank-row-5.md) [P, captured 2026-09-28] is a discovery hypothesis, not evidence of customers or contest eligibility. Public contest details have not been independently verified.

**Unmet release requirements, not weakened scope:** client-side encryption and independently governed MPC are candidates to hide plaintext order content from any one matcher; **they do not by themselves hide a participant's identity from that matcher**. The required identity boundary spans ingress, funding credentials, public Solana signer/account links, transport/RPC metadata and common control between intake, eligibility, custody and matching operators. An actual unlinkable ingress and funding/linkage design has **not been selected or tested**, so no funded market may claim or proceed under the owner-required single-matcher identity protection until an observer-by-observer test demonstrates it. This requirement is narrower than global anonymity: legal eligibility actors and the separate ZEC custody committee may learn identity/payment data, and public Solana activity may remain linkable to outside observers; those risks must be disclosed and cannot silently be attributed to a matcher. Do not claim that a single matcher cannot learn identities today. The owner accepts that the ZEC committee controls payout and exit: it may collude, lose shares or go offline, causing indefinite freeze or loss; **users cannot independently force a mined shielded-ZEC deposit to refund**. Solana's own locked-leg refund right is distinct and conditional on token/issuer transferability. The prior both-assets-under-user-unilateral-control requirement is replaced only for the approved custody trust model; real-asset use remains blocked by §4 testnet, security, legal and separate authorization gates. Public clearing price is possible; the exact auction objective and private tie-break remain to be specified per pair.

## 2. Options and decision

1. **Transparent ZEC hash/time-locked swap** [P: [ZIP 300](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0300), accessed 2026-09-29]: research comparator requiring a separately designed Solana SPL lock; Zcash address, amounts and refund path are transparent. It changes the selected shielded-ZEC product and is not the Kerb settlement route.
2. **Encrypted batch matching + threshold-custodied shielded ZEC + conditional SPL escrow** [I; recommended *candidate to build and test*]. The matcher computes sealed fills without giving an individual node plaintext orders; **preventing that node from learning participant identities is a separate, unmet release gate** requiring ingress and funding-linkage controls. Independently governed threshold custodians scan Ironwood deposits, sign payments/refunds and attest them to a Solana program. The program can condition SPL release on valid committee authorization and deadlines but **cannot conclude from a committee signature alone that a shielded recipient received ZEC**. The committee can censor, misdirect, freeze or lose ZEC; the matcher can selectively abort. This is an escrowed **non-atomic market**, not trustless exchange, and no implementation has been checked. No funded market order may rely on it before §4 gates.
3. **Solver-mediated encrypted RFQ / existing bridge** [P: [JupiterZ RFQ](https://jupiterz.jup.ag/docs/v2), [NEAR chain support](https://docs.near-intents.org/resources/chain-support), [NEAR Confidential Intents](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/market-makers/confidential-intents), [1Click terms](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/terms-of-service), accessed 2026-09-29]. A maker must see a bilateral request to quote; a solver can reject or reprice before signing. NEAR lists Solana and **transparent-only Zcash**; its FAR permissioned private shard uses a treasury/PoA bridge, not a direct Ironwood↔SPL atomic settlement, and access may require approval. This is a plausible execution comparator with different price-discovery and custody risks, **not** a substitute for the chosen blind-matching product. A switch to maker-visible RFQ requires a new owner decision.
4. **Trust-minimized shielded settlement research** [I]: a finalized-Ironwood-transfer/effect proof and on-Solana verification might remove some attestor trust later. [Skyhook preprint](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2210.pdf) [P for authors' proposal, not independently verified] specifies EVM/ERC-20 rather than Solana and no measured Ironwood proof circuit; §5 documents contradictions and late-refund risk. It is not a release path now. TEE/commit–reveal do not independently prove order completeness or eliminate settlement/custody risk.

**Architecture decision, not build evidence:** option 2 preserves market-wide private price discovery while permitting a distinct, explicitly trusted custody boundary. [Arcium encryption](https://docs.arcium.com/developers/encryption), [MPC protocols](https://docs.arcium.com/multi-party-execution-environments-mxes/mpc-protocols), [cluster governance](https://docs.arcium.com/clusters/permissioned-clusters) and [limits](https://docs.arcium.com/developers/limitations) [P, accessed 2026-09-29] offer a possible fixed-size detect-and-abort computation; they do not establish independently controlled operators, correctness of admission, Kerb access, benchmark capacity, or Solana↔Zcash settlement. No MPC provider, custodian roster/threshold, legal role or risk limits have been selected. Their selection and verification precede any funded experiment.

## 3. Components, custody and trust boundaries

`Trader client → encrypted order + pair-specific funding evidence → independently verifiable admission/availability → independently governed MPC matchers over funded orders → signed epoch result/individual sealed fills → distinct settlement coordinator + ZEC custody quorum → Ironwood custody payouts and Solana SPL escrow releases/refunds.` This is a proposed sequence, not implemented. Matching **must not create executable fills against unfunded orders**; the precise proof binding a private order to a real ZEC deposit/SPL lock without exposing order or identity is unresolved and is an R1 release gate. Matching committee, eligibility provider, ZEC custodians and Solana program upgrade/attestation authorities need evidenced separation; multiple servers controlled by one party do not provide independence.

### 3.1 End-to-end architecture [I; proposed, not implemented]

```text
TRADER CLIENT ── encrypted signed limits ──> admission + available ciphertext set
      │                                    BLOCKED: order↔funding privacy credential
      │                                    and no-single-matcher identity test unresolved
      │                                                      ╳
      │                                           executable funded-order set
      │                                                      ▼
      │                                     independent MPC matching committee
      │                                          sealed fills + rules root
      │                                                      │
      ├─ SELLER: exact SPL mint/program ──> Solana Kerb escrow ─┐
      │                              (release; SPL-only refund │
      │                               if still locked/transferable)
      │                                                        ▼
      └─ BUYER: shielded ZEC deposit ──> Ironwood Kerb custody receiver
                                              │                 │
                        Kerb scanner → unique credit     custody spend quorum
                         (only credit authority)         (ZEC seller payout;
                                              │           committee-only refund)
                                              └── Kerb settlement coordinator
                                              checks fill + both funded legs

      RESEARCH-ONLY ZOSS BRANCH — DISABLED FOR FUNDED KERB:
      Kerb custody receiver == Zoss watched route inbox AND same inbound note
          ↳ Zoss IVK watchers → positive-receive attestation → Solana endpoint
            → Kerb adapter: duplicate notification ONLY; not funding credit,
              outbound ZEC payout proof, SPL validation or settlement authorization
      Different receiver/note: no valid Kerb↔Zoss notification path.
      Proposed Zoss v1 leaks source metadata; only separately approved compatible
      revision + privacy tests could reconsider this branch for funded use.

      Solana state → Kerb off-chain coordinator → custody quorum → Ironwood payout
      Zoss v1 does NOT verify Solana→Zcash or provide shielded-ZEC refunds.
```

**Flow / invariants [I]:** (1) Scope a market by exact SPL mint/program and Zcash network/pool; require funding before an order becomes executable. (2) Admission commits an available accepted ciphertext set; Kerb alone binds one Solana lock or scanner-confirmed ZEC credit to one accepted order and verifies no matcher/control domain can recover plaintext content or participant identity. (3) MPC returns rule-bound sealed fills; neither a cluster signature nor a Zoss `VerifiedMessage` proves funding ownership or correct clearing. (4) Coordinator reconciles fill, both balances, finality and recipient; custody quorum signs ZEC payout/refund while Solana escrow separately validates a one-time release/refund authorization. (5) Reconcile two independently finalizing legs; an authorized message never means paid. No part of this design has an atomic two-chain transaction or a guaranteed user-initiated ZEC refund.

**Historical Zoss messaging boundary [P/I; Sep29]:** [Zoss architecture, current successor](../../../../../../../../zoss/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) was then design-only: an Ironwood **route inbox receives a note**; k-of-n watchers with the inbox IVK attest positive receipt; relayer/endpoint deliver to Solana. This path is valid for Kerb **only if that inbox is the same custody receiver and the same note/credit flow Kerb uses**. A different Zoss inbox would receive a different deposit and cannot inform Kerb's credit ledger. Kerb's scanner remains the *only credit authority* even with the shared inbox; Zoss cannot establish deposit ownership, bind it privately to an order, spend ZEC, prove payout, attest absence, force refund or report Solana→Ironwood settlement. Reusing scanner operators as Zoss watchers concentrates view/availability powers; separate watchers receive another IVK and add a privacy, quorum and liveness boundary. Neither choice is made; matcher access or common control over watcher/custody logs fails the privacy gate. See [Kerb↔Zoss assessment, current successor](../../../../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md); the banner distinguishes today's library contract from this historical message-only proposal.

**Historical public/private evidence [P/I; rejected preSep30 schema]:** Zoss's then-proposed `VerifiedMessage` contained `message_id = H(domain, route, src_chain, cmx)`, `route_id`, `src_height`, `value` and `envelope`; its attestation included `cmx` [current successor §§5–7, not that schema](../../../../../../../../zoss/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#5-interface-proposed). Destination delivery, instruction bytes and CPI data may expose those fields to Solana observers, including memo `app_fields`; `cmx` could link a specific shielded note directly to a trade, while height/value/time can aid correlation. No deployed wire format had been checked. A proposed Kerb-only authorization instead keeps Zcash tx/note/confirmation/memo and identity mapping in the custody audit record and puts only the minimal opaque trade/nonce, escrow-required recipient/amount/state and quorum signature on Solana; the public SPL transfer still leaks amount, mint, recipient and time. **Do not restore this historical Zoss payload to funded Kerb settlement**; current opaque-message policy and shared-library authority are in the banner, not a silent financial/private dependency approval.

- **Client/admission [I].** Client signs domain-separated `(network, exact mint and token program, market/epoch/rules, side, integer lots, zatoshi price bound, max funding, expiry, nonce, fee cap, per-chain payout/refund destinations)` and encrypts side/limit/quantity before intake; no spending key or viewing key reaches a gateway. A verifiable funding credential must bind the exact locked SPL amount or confirmed ZEC custody credit to the order and become single-use **before it can participate in matching**; its construction and linkage/privacy proof remain open. An admission receipt must bind ciphertext, funding eligibility, cutoff and rules; independently retrievable accepted inputs and a reject/abort path are required. An intake-signed Merkle root proves only that intake's chosen set; it cannot rule out censored orders. **Unmet gate:** design ingress and funding credentials so no individual matcher or entity under the same control can join an accepted order to its trader's identity via address, RPC/IP or custody/eligibility data. Merely encrypting order content or placing RPC and matching services on different machines does not meet that gate.
- **Matching [I].** A bounded fixed-size MPC circuit should compute a specified auction objective, round/tie-break, conservation, exact accepted-set root, rule version and sealed per-order fill; disclose only a justified public price. Under [Arcium's Cerberus assumptions](https://docs.arcium.com/multi-party-execution-environments-mxes/mpc-protocols) [P], an honest independently controlled member protects input confidentiality, but an offline or corrupt cluster may abort. Cluster output signatures authenticate its output, not necessarily full-set admission or external correctness. This MPC claim is about **order content, not identity metadata**; separately test the owner-required single-matcher identity boundary (§1). Never reuse legacy `(placed_slot, owner)` as a privacy-neutral tie-break. The committee's output is **not** a ZEC receipt or authorization to spend; parties must inspect signed fills and funding terms. No public proof of hidden-order correct clearing is currently established.
- **Ironwood custody [I].** Buyer deposits exact zatoshis to a *pair-specific* Ironwood receiver controlled by threshold key shares; a scanner with appropriate incoming viewing capability must verify receiver, amount, transaction, confirmations and a client-bound deposit reference before assigning a **single** credit (a Zcash txid alone does not prove who owns a shielded deposit). The wallet/prover coordinator needs note witnesses and transaction-construction data and sees receiver, amounts, memo/reference and fill-to-payout mapping; each threshold signer must inspect payout effects, destinations, amounts and sighash before signing. Individual signers therefore may see sensitive payment data; privacy of their signing channel and key distribution is **not established**. Decide who holds scanning/full viewing keys, note witnesses and recovery shares, plus logs and retention, before privacy claims. [ZIP 312](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0312#threat-model) [P, draft] and [ZF's reported Ironwood testnet signing](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/frost-tools/issues/591) [P for test report] support only investigation of threshold spend authorization, **not** note-level conditions or unilateral refund. Corrupt threshold can spend; unavailable threshold can lock funds indefinitely.
- **Solana custody [I].** Seller locks only the exact allowlisted SPL mint/program and bounded raw amount in a per-trade escrow, under a proposed program; buyer deposits ZEC to committee as above. Solana publicly exposes signer, mint, transfer amount, token accounts and timing [P: [transaction structure](https://solana.com/docs/core/transactions/transaction-structure), [token transfers](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/transfer-tokens)]. Program may release SPL to buyer only for one signed trade result and threshold custody authorization over exact recipient, amount, nonce, funded state and cutoff; no replay/cross-pair release. **These controls are requirements, not implemented checks.** Expired unexecuted SPL escrow can refund its depositor *if token-program and issuer allow transfer*; no timeout automatically returns a mined Ironwood deposit. Program upgrade/attestation authority is a separate theft/censorship boundary.
- **Execution ordering [I].** Only confirmed, single-use funding credits/locks matching the accepted orders may enter an epoch. On a filled trade, independently validate both funds and record a chosen release order and *Pending → Funded → Authorized → FirstLegSent/Final → SecondLegSent/Final → Complete/Disputed*; `Authorized` is not `Paid`. The committee cannot cryptographically perform one transaction across both chains. If SPL is released first, the seller risks nonpayment in ZEC; if ZEC is paid first, the buyer risks nonreceipt of SPL. A signed intent or custody attestation cannot prove ZEC receipt; a scanner/recipient receipt need not independently convince a Solana program. Committee collusion, liveness loss, freeze or chain reorg leaves unresolved loss even after both legs were initially funded. No ordering removes both risks; no blanket bounded recovery, atomicity or escrow solvency guarantee.
- **Cross-chain coordination [I].** Kerb needs authenticated observations and instructions across two ledgers, not automatically a generic bridge. The custody scanner privately records actual Zcash transaction/note identifiers, receiver, value and confirmation/reorg policy; the coordinator conveys that evidence to authorized custody signers and watches the Solana vault state before requesting an Ironwood spend. A *distinct Solana-facing instruction* from the signing quorum should disclose only what the escrow needs: domain and source/destination network, exact pair/mint/program, epoch, opaque trade ID and one-time nonce, transition kind, required payout recipient/amount or commitments, expiry, prior/next state and authorization signature. Escrow rejects stale, duplicate, contradictory or cross-pair transitions and rechecks its actual vault state. **Source txid/block/`cmx`/memo stay in the private audit record** unless the owner explicitly accepts the public correlation risk. This signature is a trusted committee claim, not independently chain-verified Ironwood payment, and does not make either release atomic. Zoss v1's proposed fields do not meet this minimal-public-payload design unchanged (§3.1); no provider or format is selected. A future trust-minimized Solana light-client/effect-proof construction needs separate proof of finality and refund races (§5).
- **View boundary [I].** Public sees Solana escrow/fills or claims as encoded, mint, amounts, timing and the public clearing price if enabled, plus Zcash transaction metadata/pool turnstile amounts; public cannot read an ordinary Ironwood note's recipient/value from its ciphertext. A matching node receives encrypted input/share state under MPC assumptions but can still infer a trader's identity from intake logs, packet/RPC metadata, funding links or access to eligibility/custody records. **This violates the owner's matcher-specific privacy requirement and blocks funded operation until prevented and tested.** Eligibility provider may know legal identity; distinct custodians/coordinator see private payment data and mappings needed to act, and may correlate with Solana through receipts. Custody privacy does **not** inherit matching privacy. R1 requires observer-by-observer account, packet, access-control and collusion traces; broader public anonymity is not promised.

### 3.2 Settlement states and failure matrix [I; none implemented]

| Trigger / state | Kerb-side action that would be required | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|
| Only one leg funded before admission | Reject executable fill; on Solana allow refund **only** from a state in which SPL remains locked and token transfers are allowed; on Ironwood ask a live custody quorum for refund. | ZEC refund is never unilateral or guaranteed. A Zoss receive notification does not establish ownership or the absence of another deposit. |
| Both legs funded, matching or admission fails | Abort the epoch and reconcile exact per-order escrow/credit entitlements before any release; request separate refunds. | Custody quorum can refuse ZEC refund; issuer freeze can prevent SPL refund; a signed MPC output alone is insufficient. |
| `Authorized`, neither leg final | Do not call the trade paid; choose and record release order before either send. No automatic timeout race across two ledgers. | Committee can authorize falsely or withhold; relay delivery and chain confirmation are separate events. |
| ZEC paid first; SPL release fails or refunds | Mark `Disputed`, retain Zcash and Solana evidence; stop further payouts. | Buyer may have paid ZEC and receive no SPL; no automatic Ironwood reversal. |
| SPL released first; ZEC payout fails or is withheld | Mark `Disputed`, preserve signed approvals and reconciliation evidence. | Seller may lose SPL without receiving ZEC; signing threshold does not enforce payout. |
| Late message, duplicate, wrong pair or conflicting refund/release | Escrow checks domain, signer set, nonce and its current trade/leg state; reject replay or incompatible transition. | Valid but false quorum attestation, private-data leak, and an already-final opposite leg are not cured by replay checks. |
| Zcash reorg, scanner error, lost signing quorum, Solana mint freeze | Pause affected pair and reconcile observed finality and liabilities; incident and recovery policy require separate approval. | Confirmations are probabilistic; shares may be lost, committee may collude, token program/issuer may block transfer indefinitely. No bounded recovery promise. |
| Optional Zoss watcher/relay unavailable | With no chosen Zoss dependency, use Kerb's separately designed coordinator path. If a Zoss route were chosen, suspend its delivery and apply an approved incident procedure, not an unreviewed verifier fallback. | Zoss `Delivered` is only a notification of positive receive, not a claim that ZEC payout is final; switching routes does not erase existing exposure. |

**Release boundary:** D0 may simulate these states without money; R1 cannot fund even testnet until matcher identity/content privacy, one-time funded admission, custody roster/viewing/spend authority, settlement ordering, loss policy, security/legal review and **separate explicit authorization for that transaction** are established (§4). Even then the approved trust model remains committee-controlled and non-atomic; it is not independently safe for real assets.

| Bind chain, exact mint/program, ZEC network/pool, pair/epoch/rules hash, signed limit, nonce, expiry and maximum funded amount to each accepted order, fill and committee attestation. | Reject wrong asset, replay, stale order or cross-pair funding. A ticker, wrapped ZEC or NEAR balance is not equivalent to native ZEC. |
| Treat cross-chain coordination as a quorum-attested, replay-protected state transition, not proof of Zcash consensus; scope each message to pair, exact asset, trade, leg, recipient, amount, expiry and observed finality (§3). | A valid signature may still represent false, stale or withheld observation; relay censorship can prevent delivery, and duplicate/conflicting delivery can cause double release unless the escrow checks its current state. A generic message bus or bridge does not independently establish Ironwood payment, atomicity or a ZEC refund. |
| No single matcher or operator under common control learns either plaintext order fields **or the participant identity linked to an accepted order**; demonstrate independent matching governance, ingress privacy, funding credential unlinkability and separation from intake/eligibility/custody records with observer-specific adversarial traces. | **Currently unmet and blocks all funded matching**, including synthetic-token/ZEC testnet trials. Encrypted MPC alone does not stop RPC/IP, signer, funding or shared-log linkage. Distinct eligibility and custody actors may know identities, and public Solana linkage can remain visible to others; neither fact waives the matcher-specific requirement. If unavoidable leakage lets a matcher join an order to a participant, redesign before any funded market rather than silently downgrading this requirement. |
| Bind matching to complete **accepted** ciphertext set and verifiable rules, and make missing input detectable. | Censorship before acceptance is still possible; missing receipt, selective abort, committee signature without correctness/inclusion proof or unavailable ciphertext stops the epoch.
