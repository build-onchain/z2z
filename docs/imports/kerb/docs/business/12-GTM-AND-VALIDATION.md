## Decision, not a waitlist

The central test is **E17 (U)**: are there repeatedly opposing, economically sized orders in the **same Solana base mint, same quote mint, compatible limit prices and same roughly 60-second window**, sourced through eligible distributors, such that the buyer **and** seller would each do better net of the proposed 5 bps **per charged user side** and incremental network costs than the route actually executable for that side? A CROSS has two charged user sides (approximately 10 bps of unique cross notional); a RESIDUAL has one charged user side (approximately 5 bps of unique residual notional), with zero maker fee. The second test is whether a maker would **pre-fund** an optional residual ladder at tolerable cost, not whether it likes the concept. Nothing is built or deployed; there are no named buyer, seller, distributor or maker commitments **(E18)**. The idea-bank row is explicitly “Verify first” and its SG/HK/Brazil desks hypothesis is not evidence of sales ([source row](../sources/notion/idea-bank-row-5.md)).

This is a startup/customer-discovery sprint, not a coding contest used to hide the missing market. Volarević's [founder playbook](../sources/x/status-2038643299221729462.md) warns against hackathon-only mindset, team ambiguity, vanity metrics, missing distribution, vague customer and ignorance of competition; those are his heuristics, not official rules. Colosseum's [founder-oriented guide](https://blog.colosseum.com/how-to-win-a-colosseum-hackathon/) and [World's Fair rules](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World's%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf) (retrieved 2026-09-28) distinguish a working product/business plan from an idea pitch. The investor-scale bar is quantified in [the business model](11-BUSINESS-MODEL.md); meeting a small discovery gate will **not** imply $100M-revenue feasibility or a category lead. The founder must directly conduct interviews and name the actual build/business owners in a submission *only if verified*, not imply a team exists. This document plans research; it authorizes no outreach, transaction, deployment or real-money order.

## Narrow beachhead and route to the first cross

**Preregistered scope, conditional on future qualification:** research historical flow in one high-activity Solana xStock *candidate* mint identified by exact address (not ticker), then review its Token-2022 extension/authority snapshot against the canonical allowlist; USDC quote mint with its independently verified token-program id; one observed repeated window selected from authentic flow, initially a 60-second epoch; one prospective distributor pair producing buyer and seller interest in that same asset; one prospective pre-funded residual maker, optional and strictly after the cross. **Production eligibility is currently BLOCKED:** no exact mainnet xStock mint has been checked against the allowlist, and no named xStock is represented here as eligible. V1 refuses any mint with a permanent delegate set; a hypothetical later exception would require audit and counsel. If no mint qualifies, stop rather than switch silently to a synthetically attractive one. Any demonstration before eligibility uses test mints labelled **SYNTHETIC**. No different-issuer equivalence, no HyperCore flow merged into Solana, and no Robinhood Chain port to enlarge the denominator ([canonical guard](../00-CANONICAL.md#37-issuer-aware-guards-the-wedge), [mechanism](../mechanism/01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md), [competition](10-MARKET-AND-COMPETITION.md)).

**Target cohorts and job** (proposed contacts, not observed adoption):

1. **Distributor A/B:** wallets, tokenized-asset apps and eligible brokers with both an active xStock route and timestamped order/quote history. Interview an execution/product owner and a compliance owner separately where necessary. Job: route eligible customers at measurably improved net outcome without creating support/licensing exposure. Request de-identified sample history only with permission; aggregate counts are insufficient to reconstruct paired windows.
2. **End-users:** actual eligible buyers and holders/sellers of that *exact* mint, especially repeat traders who have placed orders at the proposed overlap time. Job: fill an order at an acceptable price with transparent wait/refund/issuer-freeze risks. A user who trades only traditional shares, a different issuer token, a perp or a different chain is a useful counterexample, not a qualifying order.
3. **Makers:** desks already quoting those tokenized-stock mints on RFQ/AMM routes, with funding/risk operations. Job: price and hedge unexpected residual imbalance. They can win by declining Kerb: a signed and fully escrowed ladder before close, no post-close cancellation or last look, maker price for residual only, and no fee to maker still impose capital/quote risk. xChange operates 24/5 and is not weekend issuer liquidity ([maker guide](https://docs.xstocks.fi/developers/xchange-solana-market-maker-guide.md), retrieved 2026-09-28).
4. **Cranks/infra integrators:** potential settlement operators and RPC/indexing providers. Job: get paid to close/clear/settle permissionlessly with recoverable costs. They are not counterparties and 10% of collected fees is zero for a zero-fill epoch, so maker and distributor gates cannot be satisfied by a crank interview.

**Channel order:** warm, consented introductions from the source idea's Superteam Vietnam ecosystem; local Superteam groups for introductions to relevant builders rather than audience-size claims; direct B2B contact with known distributor execution owners and tokenized-asset desks; referrals at the end of genuine interviews. Review community rules before posting. No fabricated relationships, scraped customer data, generic follower campaign or mass announcement. The ask is “show me the last order and route decision” rather than “would you integrate?” Ask permission to retain anonymized examples and to cite an organization; default is no named endorsement. If legal advice rules out approaching or routing a cohort, drop it from activation; do not claim demand from prohibited users.

## Freeze the evidence rules before the first interview

The unit of observation is an **independent historical order episode** with timestamp and time zone, exact mint and chain, side, lots/raw size or a conversion sufficient to reconstruct it, limit/acceptance price, venue/route, quote available at decision time, outcome (filled, partially filled, declined, canceled), and eligible jurisdiction if it can be verified without storing unnecessary personal data. Attribute multiple orders from one desk or user to the same source; do not count a maker's self-cross or duplicate routed quote as two natural counterparties. A repeated-flow finding requires more than one distinct window for the same mint with both natural buyer and seller interest from distinct economic owners; three independently evidenced instances rather than a three-row export of one incident. Prospective interview recollection with no verifiable order detail is qualitative, **not** a qualifying instance. Compare session (regular/extended/weekend) separately. A distributor pair is not the same as a contracted pair: both must have independently actionable order sources and permission to discuss trial integration.

**Paired executable quote window** means a timestamped window for one qualified mint and size in which both an eligible buy limit and sell limit could match under Kerb's program-computed `p*` rule, with contemporaneous *size- and eligibility-matched* alternative quotes **executable for each participant**. Compute hypothetical Kerb allocation and distinct buyer/seller net costs, including the **one fee per user order** `ceil(5 × (cross + residual quote-atom notional) / 10,000)` (for a pure cross, just cross notional), actual placement/settlement/refund/priority fees if measured (otherwise label a range), any extra network sponsorship, and timing/partial-fill uncertainty. Buyer owes aggregate fill notional plus fee; seller receives aggregate fill notional minus fee. Compare the alternative net cash outlay for the buyer and net cash received by the seller at that user's actual size; maker residual is reported separately at its maker price with **one** charged user side (approximately 5 bps of residual notional), not 10 bps and not a second rounded fee on a partly crossed order. Compare residual routing with a **directly executable quote from the same maker** where available: the residual user fee and distributor rebate can leave that user worse off. The 5-bps-per-side fee and rebate are an unvalidated proposal; [ADR-009](../product/16-ROADMAP-AND-DECISIONS.md#adr-009--residual-fill-fee-and-rebate) remains open. A reference midpoint, last traded price, stale dashboard quote, unactionable issuer xChange quote for an un-onboarded user or a HyperCore quote without bridge/eligibility costs **cannot** be treated as an executable substitute. If one side loses, mark the window failure, even if the other wins; show each side's bps and dollars and include null/no-quote cases in the denominator as *not proven*, not wins. Public order history cannot establish full counterfactual firm quotes; obtain authorized quote snapshots or state this gate untested. This is not a best-execution determination or realized savings claim ([mechanism](../mechanism/01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md), [unit economics](11-BUSINESS-MODEL.md)).

A **trial ticket** is a dated, written, specific paper/sandbox request from an eligible decision-maker with exact mint, side, indicative size/limit/window and named *trial* owner willing to spend time evaluating a simulated route under stated compliance conditions. It is not a signed real order, committed liquidity, live user, revenue or issuer permission. Reject generic letters of interest and synthetic self-generated orders from the gate count. A **maker written indication** specifies mint, window, two-sided or explicitly one-sided ladder/size/price terms, funding requirement, no-last-look acceptance, expiry, and the desk's conditions; classify it non-binding unless actually executed and allowed. A verbal “sounds interesting” fails.

Record a snapshot of these definitions and threshold results **before** selecting favorable examples; maintain an exception log for rejected records (duplicate flow, stale quote, unsupported extension, restricted geography, missing consent), with denominator and collection window. Do not move goalposts after seeing data. Give the customer their own description of the job and workaround, record contrary findings, and strip names from public evidence absent consent.

## Preregistered gates and stop rules for the 2026-10-12 decision

All five are **AND**, not a score. They are prospective thresholds, not reported outcomes. No interview or paired quote experiment has been run as of 2026-09-28.

| Gate | Pass evidence by cutoff | Failure interpretation / action |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | **≥10** distinct substantive conversations with relevant distributor decision-makers, actual eligible buyer/sellers, and makers; record role, date, last episode, current route, workaround, objections and artifact/verification status. Avoid ten conversations from one employer. | Under ten or only endorsements = discovery incomplete; do not claim validation. |
| Flow | **≥3** independently evidenced instances of **repeated same-mint, opposite-side** economic-owner interest across distinct windows and at usable sizes; identify distinct sources and timestamps without public personal data. | If opposite orders are rare, mismatch in time/mint or are maker wash/duplicate flow, stop this beachhead; revise window/mint only as a separately registered experiment. |
| Trial behavior | **≥2** specific trial tickets from eligible decision-makers with exact mint/size/window and allocated trial time/owner under compliance review. | Waitlist sign-ups, “yes” answers, demo views and anonymous poll votes do not count. No route to integration means stop B2B channel thesis. |
| Maker feasibility | **1** written, sufficiently specific **non-binding** indication to consider a signed, fully pre-funded residual ladder without last look, with terms/conditions recorded. | Refusal to pre-fund means remove residual from the business case; because this gate is preregistered for the full V1 thesis, full thesis does not pass. Crossing may be examined separately without repackaging it as a passed gate. |
| Economics | **≥3** qualifying paired executable-quote windows in which **both** buyer and seller improve net against their available alternatives after fees and network cost; report tested denominator and failures, not just three winners. | Zero/one-sided improvement, unfillable quotes or missing quote proof = cost thesis not established; do not claim cheaper execution. |

Independent **stop conditions even if counts pass:** no legally permissible venue/distributor path or eligible reviewed mint; a permanent delegate set on the proposed V1 mint; inability to disclose issuer-caused vault shortfall, frozen vault and non-guaranteed recovery; economically meaningful adverse selection from public order exposure; pre-funded residual unavailable; too much delay/partial-fill rejection by observed users; active alternative routes beating the matched net price. Ordinary `RefundOnly` applies before clear with withdrawal of the original deposit **only where the issuer and token program permit transfer**. After clear, the entitlement ledger does not revert; an issuer pause/freeze can leave a leg `SettlementBlocked`, with a non-sticky advisory marker and permissionless retry after conditions clear; the freeze may persist indefinitely. A shortfall at any stage instead marks the affected leg `Impaired`, **stops that leg's payouts**, leaves nominal entitlements unchanged (pre-clear: original deposits) and emits `VaultShortfall`; the other leg keeps settling. There is **no automatic pro-rata payout or restitution**, no V1 recovery path, and claims may never be recoverable. Do not use a successful synthetic demonstration to elide either risk ([canonical issuer guard](../00-CANONICAL.md#37-issuer-aware-guards-the-wedge), [legal](../legal/09-REGULATORY-AND-LEGAL.md)). If gates fail, report the failure and decide whether a **new**, separately evidenced hypothesis merits testing; no cosmetic pivot to a different chain or generic exchange within this submission.

## Interview guide: the last real order, not a hypothetical product

Opening (2 minutes): “We are researching how people currently buy and sell tokenized stocks and where orders fail or cost money. There is no venue to sell you today. Can I ask about recent real transactions? May I take anonymized notes, and may I record? No pressure to share confidential client information.” If they decline recording, take notes only. Establish role, eligibility constraints and how the participant currently decides whether to quote/route/trade (3 minutes). The founder should ask follow-ups and listen; do not present a deck before capturing behavior.

**Distributor / broker (15–20 minutes):**

1. “Walk me through the last three xStock buy and sell orders that reached your route. Which *exact* mint, size, timestamp, location eligibility, order instructions and alternative quotes did you see?” Then: “Who owned each quote, how long was it good, which route won and why?”
2. “When was the last time a customer canceled or received a partial fill? What was the ticket/support cost and what did you do?” “Did a buyer and seller of the same mint appear in the same minute on different routes? Can we inspect a de-identified example, including misses?”
3. “Which compliance/issuer restrictions block which users? Who signs off a new trading venue? What was the last new route you integrated, how long did it take and what got delayed?”
4. “What are your present fee/rebate terms? Have you changed a route for better economics before; what customer outcome and internal approval mattered?” Do not ask whether a promised 30% rebate sounds attractive.

**Buyer / seller (10–15 minutes):** “Tell me about the last time you bought or sold this exact token. Show the timestamp and executed net amount if comfortable; what else could you have done then?” “What prompted the trade at that time; how long did you wait, and what if only part filled?” “What fees or displayed units surprised you? What happened when trading was paused, an account froze or a dividend/split multiplier changed?” “What did you try next? When was the previous similar trade?” Do not solicit restricted users to trade.

**Maker / desk (15–20 minutes):** “Tell me about the last quote you streamed for this mint off-hours. Where was your inventory and what funding was locked? How often did you reprice or decline a fill?” “What did the last halt/multiplier event do to your book?” “What would the last hour's P&L/financing look like if quotes had been irrevocably locked before each 60-second close?” “Can you share a redacted quote/risk example and a written set of terms your risk owner would actually evaluate for a *fully funded* residual ladder, including expiry and constraints?” Do not turn an indicative answer into a commitment.

**Crank/infra (5–10 minutes):** “How do you currently fund permissionless maintenance at zero executed volume? Which costs failed to be recovered last time? What would you monitor for missed clear deadlines or blocked entitlement transfers?”

Wrap-up for each: “What have I misunderstood? Who else can show a contrasting case? May we review a specific anonymized past episode or a paper trial ticket?” Only *after* the past-behavior segment, briefly explain the proposed 60-second cross, 5 bps/user fee, pre-funding, residual maker choice and blocked-settlement risk; ask for factual corrections rather than “would you use it?” A referral or document is useful; praise is not a gate.

**Private note template (one row per participant/episode):** participant ID and organization ID; role and jurisdiction eligibility (yes/no/unknown); consent/recording status; interview date; exact mint/chain/token programs and relevant multiplier window; last order timestamp/side/size/limit; executable alternatives and proof/expiry; selected route/net outcome; existing workaround and switching cost; recurrence; negative evidence; permission to follow up/cite; trial owner and written artifact reference; gate classification and reason. Store only necessary data securely and use aggregate/redacted evidence in the submission. An unknown field remains **U**, not zero or a positive.

## Scoreboard and submission wording

Track conversations with qualifying last-order detail / all conversations; independent same-mint opposing windows / all observed windows; compatible fillable lots and notional / eligible submitted lots and notional; buyer and seller net improvement in bps and dollars / all comparable windows; fill and no-cross/refund/blocked/**Impaired** counts separately; specific trial tickets / requests; written maker indications / maker conversations; exact issuer mints reviewed against allowlist and accepted / examined mints (currently **zero reviewed**, not zero approved from a completed review); distributor integration burden and actual routing availability. Break out natural users from maker churn, sessions, mint, chain and referral status. Do not use total tokenized-stock TAM, exchange volume, generic waitlist, impressions, award history, mock fills or de-duplicated wallets as Kerb users. Bitquery's one-month Solana study finds volume skewed to a few tickers and wallets ([Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/solana-tokenized-stocks-xstocks), retrieved 2026-09-28; E13); it is a warning about denominators, not a measured Kerb addressable market.

A truthful submission might say **“As of [date], [x]/10 discovery conversations had verifiable last-order detail; [y] of [z] independently observed same-mint opposing windows were fillable; [a] of [b] executable paired-quote windows improved both sides net; [c] trial tickets and [d] non-binding maker indications were collected; [gates] remain unmet/untested.”** Bracketed variables are an example template, **not results**. If all values remain zero/unknown, say so; if local/demo orders use test mints, label **SYNTHETIC** prominently. Distinguish measured quotes from real fills, verbal interest from written paper trials, and a non-binding maker note from a funded ladder. No issuer endorsement, committed user, live liquidity, mainnet deployment, private execution, guaranteed saving or best-execution claim. The decision after Oct 12 is to continue the narrow hypothesis only if counts and legal conditions justify it; otherwise stop or explicitly register a new experiment, not retrofit a victory narrative.
