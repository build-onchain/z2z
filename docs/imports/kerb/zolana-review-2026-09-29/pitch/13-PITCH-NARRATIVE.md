## A truthful short version [I/U]

“Kerb is **research toward** a cross-chain private-order market for Solana assets and native shielded Zcash Ironwood ZEC. We propose a fixed-set, rule-bound matching design in which independently operated matching nodes would not individually learn plaintext orders **or identities linked to accepted orders**; that second requirement is **not solved** by encryption alone. The selected settlement trust model is **threshold-custodied shielded ZEC plus a separate Solana SPL escrow**. It is not an atomic swap; custodians can withhold or misdirect ZEC, and there is no unilateral ZEC refund. We are building a **non-settling synthetic-order demonstrator** to test auction invariants, accepted-set integrity and metadata linkage, including ways the privacy goal might fail. No funded market, user demand, audit, legally cleared operator or supported real asset has been established.”

**If D0 has not actually run, replace “building” with “planning.”** If a real independently governed MPC cluster is unavailable, say that the demonstrator uses a local plaintext reference and **does not demonstrate MPC confidentiality**. If any attack maps an accepted order to an identity available to a matcher/control domain, report a failed requirement, not “privacy preserved.” The version delivered publicly must be reconciled with actual demo logs and permissions immediately before use.

## Narrative by slide or conversation [I]

1. **Problem hypothesis, not quantified market:** Existing fast routes can execute some trades; an eligible participant might instead value non-bilateral limit matching between holders of Solana assets and shielded ZEC. Counter-hypothesis: no repeat opposite flow at compatible times/prices, or simpler routes win on certainty and risk. No validated demand or liquidity claim. See [market landscape](../business/10-MARKET-AND-COMPETITION.md) and [discovery](../business/12-GTM-AND-VALIDATION.md).
2. **Exact asset boundary:** Three distinct pair proposals, not one liquidity pool: (A) **SYNTHETIC** Solana mint ↔ **testnet** Ironwood ZEC for separately authorized later research, (B) canonical Solana USDC ↔ native shielded Ironwood ZEC, (C) one exact issuer-qualified Solana xStock mint/program ↔ native shielded Ironwood ZEC. D0 exchanges **none** of these assets. There is no selected/qualified xStock. Ticker, wrapped token, NEAR balance or transparent ZEC do not substitute for exact assets. See [issuer profile](../architecture/05-TOKEN-2022-ISSUER-INTEGRATION.md).
3. **Mechanism under investigation:** Proposed client encryption, independent accepted-set admission, independently governed MPC computation and sealed fills. A cluster signature does not prove the admitted set was complete or funds exist. Funding-to-order binding without linking identity to a matcher, and access/log separation, are open blockers. Present an explicit malicious-matcher observation trace, not a dark-pool slogan. See [mechanism](../mechanism/01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md) and [security tests](../security/08-TEST-AND-VERIFICATION-PLAN.md).
4. **Settlement/trust disclosure:** The ZEC committee can sign spends or refuse; Solana's program can check proposed state/nonce/authorization for its own escrow but cannot infer Ironwood recipient payment from a committee signature alone. Whichever leg pays first creates a non-atomic loss window. Ironwood deposit/refund cannot be made unilateral with a transaction timeout; Solana SPL refunds require still-locked transferable assets and compatible trade state. A separate scanner owns one-time ZEC credit. [Proposed custody architecture](../architecture/04-ONCHAIN-PROGRAM-DESIGN.md) and [legal questions](../legal/09-REGULATORY-AND-LEGAL.md) remain unresolved.
5. **What the demonstrator can establish:** A local, fixed-size **non-settling synthetic order set** with clear input receipt/set, integer reference result, per-order limits/conservation, abort/reject, metadata deanonymization attempts and two-way settlement failure-state traces as **simulation**. If an independent MPC environment is truly available, compare against the reference and show governance assumptions separately; otherwise don't imply MPC ran. Do not show wallets, deposits, balances or fake “paid” badges as if live. See [D0 plan](../product/15-HACKATHON-PLAN.md).
6. **What discovery must establish:** Real past-behavior episodes and paper-only commitments independently for USDC/ZEC and each exact xStock/ZEC candidate, paired executable alternatives, economic owner and lawful cohort; no participants have been verified. Fees, volume, traction, savings, ROI and TAM are **unknown**. See [economics](../business/11-BUSINESS-MODEL.md) and [draft discovery gates](../business/12-GTM-AND-VALIDATION.md).
7. **Explicit stop point:** No funded matching until matcher-specific content **and identity** protection, single-use private funded admission, independent operators, custody and loss policy, settlement checks, security and legal/issuer review and **separate explicit authorization**. A D0 pass is not R1 funding permission; neither this pitch nor owner selection of committee custody is one.

## Q&A guardrails

- **“Does a single matcher know my identity or order?”** Requirement says no; **not proven**. MPC can potentially protect order content under governance assumptions; ingress, RPC, funding accounts and shared logs may reveal identity. D0 should attempt to break it, not advertise it.
- **“Is it private on both chains?”** No. Shielded Ironwood hides ordinary note recipient/value ciphertext from public view; Solana signer/account/mint/amount/timing and optional clearing price can remain visible. Independent eligibility and custody actors may have privileged views. No blanket anonymity.
- **“Can users self-refund or is it atomic?”** No ZEC unilateral refund or atomic cross-chain execution. Custody quorum is a trusted counterparty; both leg orders have a loss path; Solana SPL refund is separate and conditional.
- **“What is shipping?”** Only report actually executed D0 artifacts with dates and precise scope. If nothing ran, disclose that; a plan is not a working product, and paper scenarios are not on-chain evidence.
- **“How do you make money?”** Not chosen or tested; show the symbolic cost and break-even identity, not the old same-mint 5-bps proposal, invented GMV or ROI.
- **“Who wants this / which issuer approved?”** No verified Kerb counterparty or qualified xStock. The [Idea Bank #5](../../docs/sources/notion/idea-bank-row-5.md) **[P]** captured 2026-09-28 is an author's hypothesis; a prize or founder commentary is not user evidence.

All external claims require dated source links and the final [red-flag audit](14-JUDGE-RED-FLAG-AUDIT.md). Do not submit a future-tense feature as a present-tense capability.
