## Artifact-and-method audit before showing a demo

1. **Scope:** Exact D0 asset labels are synthetic; no account receiving assets, no token movement, no fabricated wallets/balances. Distinguish pair A later research from a non-settling D0. Check [D0 criteria](../product/15-HACKATHON-PLAN.md) against recorded run logs rather than slides. If no executable exists, say “planned” not “working.”
2. **Method:** Record circuit/reference versions, integer lot/price and rounding rules, accepted-set commitment and reject cases, input generation and reproducible commands. Separately note simulated adversarial metadata from actual observations. Show matched-set completeness, conservation, limits, tie-break, duplicate credit attempt, omitted accepted input, and both settlement orderings with disagreement/reorg/freeze branches. A passing reference result does not validate independent MPC governance or identity unlinkability.
3. **Privacy leakage:** Enumerate matcher node, its common-control ingress/RPC/eligibility logs, chain observer, distinct custody signer, scanner, legal identity actor and collusions. Test identity linkage via timing, reused addresses, unique amounts, funding account and public Solana fields even when order ciphertext is opaque. If the matcher sees a trader, **fail** owner gate; do not weaken definition to “no price plaintext.”
4. **Evidence:** Each material claim needs source URL and date, precise passage or reproducible observed artifact, evidence grade, contrary source and limitation. [Zwap discussion](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zwap-trustless-shielded-atomic-swaps-for-zcash-early-access/55874) **[U]** and authors' [paper](https://zwap.atheon.xyz/) **[P]** (accessed 2026-09-29) conflict on claimed shielded mechanism; don't assert resolution. [Colosseum official guide](https://blog.colosseum.com/how-to-win-a-colosseum-hackathon/) **[P]** and [official rules](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World's%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf) **[P]** should be rechecked for submission rules/date before any contest claim. [Founder commentary capture](../../docs/sources/x/status-2038643299221729462.md) **[P]** for that author's views, not official judging criteria.
5. **Governance and authority:** Verify owner-approved spec separately from staging; no document creates authority to sign, move testnet/real funds, solicit counterparties, deploy or publish. If any gate remains unmet, treat it as blocking real use rather than a slide footnote.

**Pass condition for this audit:** every affirmative claim in [pitch](13-PITCH-NARRATIVE.md) is either a precise, reproducible observed D0 result with limitations, a dated supported description of another source's claim, or clearly marked proposed/inference. Any unsupported traction, guarantees, deployment, audit, compliance or privacy claim is a **block**, not a cosmetic wording issue. This checklist itself is not evidence that a review has already happened.
