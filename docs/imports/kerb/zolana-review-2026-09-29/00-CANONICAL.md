## 2. Non-negotiable privacy and custody boundary

**Two independent funded-use requirements [I/U]:** no single matcher/control domain learns plaintext accepted-order fields **or** their participant identity. Its view includes all controlled/accessible intake, RPC/transport, admission, eligibility, custody, logs and public-chain data; several servers under one administrator are one domain. The later candidate chooses FAA admission: FAA privately sees full signed order+identity and certifies the SAME held tuple consumed by MPC. This is a disclosed privileged role outside the matcher, **not blind credential implementation**. Actual separate governance/access/recovery/support boundaries, exact private certificate/client verification, transport and public-output linkage remain unproven hard gates. Encryption/MPC alone hides neither signers/IP/timing/unique amounts nor funding links. Failure of either privacy gate blocks funded matching including synthetic testnet; no global anonymity or confidentiality against privileged FAA/custody/coordinator is promised.

**Known structural privacy failure — later candidate §6/GD2:** allowed padded8 singleton Alice plus attacker-owned contra orders publicly exposes her accepted seller membership; own buy8 result distinguishes private ask6 vs10. This defeats identity AND content requirements without breaking MPC. Not merely U/unmeasured: GD2 fails this permitted corpus and no currently viable private production-demo release exists. Padding, sealed price, independent FAA or fictional traders cannot waive it. Primary [integrated spec](../docs/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-kerb-production-demo-design.md) requires hidden collateral/membership with compositional proof/evidence or explicit owner-reviewed privacy-guarantee change; none approved.

**Custody trust model [I, owner-selected as acceptable to investigate]:** buyer native shielded Ironwood ZEC enters a pair-specific Kerb committee receiver; seller exact SPL enters Solana escrow. Scanner alone assigns unique privately claimant-bound confirmed credit after receiver/note/network/value/finality and controlled inventory checks. FAA's later proposed certificate binds verified ownership, exact order and durable hold; private verification is not implemented. Buyer FULL max debit and seller atoms move to **AdmissionPrepared before the accepted root**, then EpochEncumbered after observed finalized canonical commit. Until final allocation, neither side may refund/reassign even with zero Fill records, slow MPC, expiry or missing acknowledgement. Only definitive canonical irreversible ABORT/exclusion with stale-commit/authorization fences permits preallocation release; uncertainty stays held. Final allocation creates disjoint filled and unused portions, each at most one reservation, not whole-credit exclusivity. Scanner/viewing capability differs from spend quorum; colluding/offline signers can steal or strand ZEC. [ZIP 203](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0203) [P, 2026-09-29] expires an unmined transaction, never refunds a mined deposit. SPL return also depends on issuer/token permission. No atomicity or guaranteed recovery.

**Proposed state/evidence boundary [I]:** `Available → AdmissionPrepared → EpochEncumbered → FinalAllocation → FilledOutstanding / BuyerUnusedOutstanding`; seller atoms have analogous prepared/encumbered/filled/final-unfilled partitions. For each confirmed buyer credit C, `C = available + admission_prepared + epoch_encumbered + seller_payout_outstanding + buyer_unused_outstanding + seller_payout_final + buyer_refund_final` (later candidate zero protocol fee; sponsor fees separate). All categories disjoint; pending spends remain outstanding, change is inventory not new credit. Canonical COMMIT/FINAL/ABORT fences plus durable serializable private journal are **not** an atomic database+chain transaction. First leg requires observed finalized FINAL and matching journal activation. Filled leg states `Authorized → FirstLegSent/Final → SecondLegSent/Final → Complete/Disputed` do not substitute for pre-match holds. Both final observations and liability reconciliation required for Complete; no refund race with sent/ambiguous spend. Solana signatures authorize trusted transitions, not independently prove Ironwood payment. Never publish tx/note/memo/cmx mappings to make them appear verifiable; public SPL amounts/accounts/timing still threaten privacy. See [architecture](architecture/03-SYSTEM-ARCHITECTURE.md) and [threat model](security/07-THREAT-MODEL.md).

**ReserveFill is not Release authorization.** Later candidate requires durable `FirstLegPrepared` and canonical finalized first-leg fence BEFORE live Release signatures leave signers. Safe filled cancellation requires finalized mutually exclusive `cancel_fill`/`release_spl` state proving SPL still unreleased plus ALL signer journals showing no native payout intent/signature/broadcast; stale/missing/ambiguous evidence holds/disputes, no negative Zcash proof. Candidate reuses vendor ArcisEd25519 signer/account-backed sealed inputs and fixed-size one-transaction extraction callbacks; existence of SDK facilities is not runtime verification. Coordinator receives full certified orders/permutation privately for independent arithmetic checking.

**Historical optional Zoss message profile [P/I; Sep30 correction]:** [current consumer successor](../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md) and [core architecture](../../../../../zoss/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) now include the shared native libraries in the authority banner; this paragraph retains the separately optional non-money Zcash→Solana message contract. Sep30 public opaque receipt uses random delivery_id, route/destination/verifier context, app key/nonce, explicit public fields and ciphertext digest; **no default public cmx/txid/action/block locator, height/value/full memo or private-observation hash**. Source occurrence/value/height stay in private signed watcher provenance bound to the public receipt digest. Cmx-derived message_id and height/value/envelope defaults are rejected preSep30 history. Same receiver AND same note are necessary for Kerb diagnosis, which stays OFF/non-money. Kerb scanner/financial authority still needs claimant/ownership, canonical history, controlled unallocated inventory and durable credit dedup; neither source observation nor receipt proves funding/hold/payout/refund/absence/reverse effects. Timing/app keys/private viewing maps remain gates. Shared-library reuse is not an added viewer or message-runtime dependency.

## 5. Glossary

- **Accepted ciphertext set:** independently retrievable, cutoff-bound list/root of signed, admitted encrypted orders for an epoch. An intake-only root is not proof that all eligible traders were admitted; correctness/inclusion and availability still need design.
- **Matcher/control domain:** one matcher plus every service, administrator and log it controls or can access, including ingress, RPC, credential, eligibility and custody data if actually shared. Independently run eligibility/custody actors have a different disclosed privacy boundary.
- **Funding credit / lock:** scanner-confirmed unique controlled ZEC claim in the pair journal / exact SPL in escrow. FULL buyer max debit and seller atoms are held from admission PREPARE through canonical accepted-root resolution. Every entitlement portion is exclusive; disjoint portions may support different obligations without reusing units. A Zoss receipt, txid alone or MPC signature is not funding.
- **Ironwood ZEC:** native shielded Zcash asset/pool named in the proposed spec; not SPL ZEC or a transparent Zcash HTLC. **IVK** can inspect incoming note data but cannot spend; spend quorum can authorize ZEC transfers.
- **Sealed fill:** per-order result intended only for the entitled recipient and settlement actors; aggregate public price, pair/amount/timing and cross-chain audit mapping can still leak information.
- **Quorum attestation:** committee-authenticated statement about a trade/leg, which Solana could validate as authorization; it is **not** independently verifiable Ironwood consensus/payment-effect evidence.
- **Non-atomic:** Solana and Zcash legs can finalize separately; there is an irreducible funded loss/dispute interval under this committee trust model.
- **D0 / R1 / R2 / R3:** non-settling synthetic privacy demo / future authorized synthetic funded testnet study / possible USDC-ZEC / possible exact-xStock-ZEC, respectively. Naming a phase does not pass its gates.
