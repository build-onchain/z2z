[Proposed design spec](../../docs/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-kerb-zolana-design.md) has not been accepted as implementation authority. ## 3. ADR register — recorded constraints, proposed mechanisms, open decisions

These ADRs are **new cross-chain review entries** and do not carry forward the old Solana-only decisions as approved specifications. Every item states context, status/choice, consequence and revisit evidence; “selected by owner” records direction only. [I except explicitly attributed owner choices]

### ADR-Z01 — one cross-chain architecture; three isolated pairs

- **Context:** A Solana-only same-mint auction would not trade native shielded ZEC, and treating lookalike tickers as fungible risks wrong assets and participant rights. [ZIP 258](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0258) [P, retrieved 2026-09-29] specifies Ironwood for new post-NU6.3 shielded receipts and restricts new Orchard deposits/cross-address transfers.
- **Status/choice:** **Owner-selected topology** of one Solana↔Zcash architecture, distinct SYNTHETIC/ZEC, canonical USDC/ZEC and exact xStock/ZEC markets. [I] Proposed implementation binds exact Solana mint/program and Zcash network/pool to orders, funding, fills and settlement messages.
- **Consequences:** Separate entitlement and custody liabilities; no wrapped ZEC or generic bridge substitute; do not share inventory/qualification or pool orders across pairs. This is not evidence either side can settle.
- **Revisit:** Only an explicit owner change plus new threat, technical and legal review of a different topology.

### ADR-Z02 — matcher content **and** accepted-order identity protection

- **Context:** MPC encryption can protect content while intake, RPC, Solana signer, unique deposit, credential redemption or a shared administrator reveals the accepted order's owner. [Arcium Cerberus docs](https://docs.arcium.com/multi-party-execution-environments-mxes/mpc-protocols) [P for candidate vendor model, 2026-09-29] describe privacy with an honest independent cluster member and detect-and-abort; they do not prove Kerb deployment or metadata anonymity.
- **Status/choice:** **Owner-selected property**, *unmet*. No one matching operator/control domain with services/logs it can access may learn plaintext order **or** join an accepted order to a participant. Client encryption, independent MPC, anonymous/private ingress and unlinkable funding credential are **proposals**; none proves this boundary today.
- **Consequences:** Hard stop to *all funded matching*, including R1 testnet, until an observer-by-observer adversarial implementation test passes. Eligibility/custody actors may retain necessary privileged identity views only while truly inaccessible to matchers; no claim of global anonymity.
- **Revisit:** Candidate architecture and access graph fixed, then fail/pass evidence for both properties; if unsatisfiable, return to owner for scope decision rather than editing the property out.

### ADR-Z03 — threshold Ironwood custody, not trustless atomic exchange

- **Context:** Native shielded ZEC does not currently provide the proposed user-enforceable conditional swap against Solana SPL. [ZIP 203](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0203) [P, 2026-09-29] limits *unmined transaction* inclusion; it cannot reclaim a mined note. [ZIP 312 threat model](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0312#threat-model) [P, draft, 2026-09-29] explains coordinator/share-holder privacy privileges for threshold spends, not guaranteed refund.
- **Status/choice:** **Owner-selected trust model**: threshold committee holds shielded ZEC; custody signers may pay or refund, so no unilateral ZEC refund, no cross-chain atomicity. [I] Roster/threshold/transaction-construction controls and escrow counterpart are proposed, not approved.
- **Consequences:** Committee censorship, loss/collusion, insolvency, bad recipient, reorg and issuer-frozen SPL create user loss or indefinite lock. Signer power must be disclosed; a signature is not proof of Zcash receipt. Release ordering and loss allocation remain open.
- **Revisit:** Only after written policy, independent review, actual spend implementation, legal and owner review; any trustless alternative would be a separately proven architecture, not a rename of attestation.

### ADR-Z04 — scanner authority and one-time funded admission

- **Historical Context:** A shielded note/txid does not establish Kerb order ownership, and matchers must not accept unbacked executable orders. A Zoss message watcher attests only positive receipt, never payment/refund authority [P for Sep29 message proposal: [assessment, current successor](../../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md), then retrieved2026-09-29]. The current shared-library docs direction is separate in the banner.
- **Status/choice:** **Proposed, unresolved mechanism.** Kerb scanner privately confirms pair receiver, note, amount and depth and alone issues a single-use credit. Admission must bind one correct credit or exact SPL lock to an available accepted encrypted order without exposing identity to a matcher; exact cryptographic credential unselected.
- **Consequences:** No funded executable set before valid credit/lock, availability receipt, non-reuse and privacy proof. Scanner compromise is a monetary trust boundary, not cured by a Zoss endpoint.
- **Revisit:** Demonstrated one-use credential under reorg/restart and matcher join attacks, with authority segregation and liabilities reconciled.

### ADR-Z05 — auction confidentiality, availability and correctness

- **Context:** A cluster output signature authenticates signer keys but not a complete accepted input set, proper reference rules or no selective abort. A bilateral RFQ lets its maker read the request and is not the owner-selected no-single-matcher price-discovery model.
- **Status/choice:** **Proposed** client encryption plus independently governed fixed-size MPC auction and a retrievable, receipted accepted-ciphertext set. Concrete clearing objective, rounding, tie-break, public aggregate/price, fee cap and participation limits require separate owner/rule review; **do not inherit** the legacy on-chain same-mint user-only plateau/residual-maker mechanism.
- **Consequences:** Missing input or corrupt execution aborts; pre-acceptance censorship and liveness remain possible; actual MPC access, performance and governance untested. A local cleartext reference proves only arithmetic behavior.
- **Revisit:** Published exact rule vectors, independent reference implementation, adversarial accepted-set and privacy traces, independently operated execution, measured capacity and owner review.

### ADR-Z06 — two-chain release and failure accounting

- **Context:** There is no single transaction across Ironwood and Solana. ZEC-first exposes buyer to SPL failure; SPL-first exposes seller to ZEC nonpayment. Solana issuer freeze may block release/refund even if escrow authorization is valid [P for general token transfer mechanics: [Solana token transfer guide](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/transfer-tokens), 2026-09-29].
- **Status/choice:** **Proposed state invariant, release order open.** `Pending → Funded → Authorized → FirstLegSent/Final → SecondLegSent/Final → Complete/Disputed`; record exact finality, authorized signer, pair, nonce and current state. Solana release may be conditional on quorum authorization; it does **not** prove shielded recipient receipt. Refund SPL only if still locked in safe state and transferable; refund ZEC needs live committee approval.
- **Consequences:** Freeze/reorg/timeout/bad quorum are disputes with possibly irreversible loss, not a forced-atomic rollback. Need public policy and customer consent; no bounded recovery time promised.
- **Revisit:** Explicit owner selection of release order, cutoff/finality and loss policy after fault campaigns in [verification plan](../security/08-TEST-AND-VERIFICATION-PLAN.md) and counsel review.

### ADR-Z07 — Zoss v1 disabled for funded Kerb

- **Historical Sep29 Context:** [assessment, current successor](../../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md) [P for then-proposed message interface, 2026-09-29] described Zcash→Solana positive receive. The rejected preSep30 `message_id = H(domain, route_id, src_chain, cmx)` note-derived identifier, separate `src_height`/`value`/`envelope` `VerifiedMessage` fields and cmx attestation could be public and link note/trade. This is preserved schema history, not current opaque public wire or overall shared-core scope; no runtime support was confirmed.
- **Historical Status/choice:** at that date proposed non-money boundary, no owner adoption of messages; no funded dependency on v1. Diagnosis required exact Kerb receiver/same note; Kerb scanner alone issued credit only with app checks. Separate IVK watchers would add viewing trust. Current **shared-library docs adoption** in the banner requires no watcher/key handoff and does not enable this route or approve financial/private architecture.
- **Consequences:** v1 does not bind order ownership, confirm Ironwood payout/absence, refund, Solana→Zcash delivery or settlement atomicity. Passing transport tests would not erase correlation leakage. A Kerb-only minimal opaque Solana attestation is also trusted, not proof.
- **Revisit:** Separately proposed privacy-preserving Zoss revision, public-byte trace, governance/attestation threat model, security tests and express owner approval; never relabel existing v1 payload as private.

### ADR-Z08 — no assumed fees, users, issuer or legal authority

- **Context:** The [Idea Bank #5 capture](../../docs/sources/notion/idea-bank-row-5.md) [P for an author hypothesis, retrieved 2026-09-28] suggested privacy and regional buyers, not verified demand; its older V1 commentary is not this cross-chain design. [Issuer xStock overview](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/product-legal-overview) [P for issuer descriptions, 2026-09-29] describes certificate terms and restrictions but grants no Kerb permission.
- **Status/choice:** **Proposed evidence discipline.** Discovery, maker willingness, fee/rebate economics, solvency, security audit, counsel and issuer rights are independent open gates. No token/points, fee rate, customer count, execution volume, market legality, issuer qualification or partner relationship is declared here.
- **Consequences:** Product/pitch may accurately describe *research intent and risks*; not make live “private/atomic/regulated” claims. Real-asset phases stop until separate findings.
- **Revisit:** Verifiable participant observations and executable quotes, a defensible fee model, reviewed issuer Final Terms and written jurisdiction-specific legal analysis; each requires owner choice before action.
