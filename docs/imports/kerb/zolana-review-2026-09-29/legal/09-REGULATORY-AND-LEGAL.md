## 1. Facts to supply counsel; unknown facts are not answers

Owner chose **one** Solana↔Zcash architecture with **three separate pairs**: (D0/R1) SYNTHETIC Solana SPL test token against shielded Ironwood Zcash testnet ZEC, (R2) canonical Solana USDC against native Ironwood ZEC, and (R3) one exact issuer-qualified Solana xStock mint against native Ironwood ZEC. D0 is **offline/non-settling** synthetic orders and no deposits or token transfers; R1 funded testnet is *not authorized*, and R2/R3 are not live markets. The proposed Kerb custody committee controls shielded ZEC spending, scanner privately maps receipt→single credit→order, and Solana escrow could condition SPL release on signer authorization. There is **no atomic swap and no user-enforceable ZEC refund**; a live quorum must choose to pay or refund. No single matching operator or its accessible services may learn plaintext orders **or** identity linked to an accepted order; the ingress/funding privacy mechanism is unselected and blocks funded matching. Independent eligibility and custody actors may nevertheless have legally significant identity/payment views. Zoss v1 is disabled for funded Kerb; no generic bridge or payment proof is assumed. [I; [threat model](../security/07-THREAT-MODEL.md), [architecture](../architecture/03-SYSTEM-ARCHITECTURE.md)].

Counsel intake must identify, **per pair**, the actual entities and jurisdictions of incorporation and operation; operator/controller, independent matching nodes and common-control graph, client/gateway provider, eligibility/KYC provider, scanner, IVK holders, threshold spend participants, payout constructor/prover, Solana escrow/upgrade authority, interface/API distributor, market-data publisher, fee recipients, counterparties, permitted jurisdictions and participants, and contractual liability/insurance/insolvency arrangements. None is established in this draft. Provide exact mint/program and issuer final terms, Zcash testnet/mainnet scope, D0/R1/R2/R3 proposed user journey, custody sign and access controls, public/private record fields, retention plan, execution order, exposure scenarios and the [test plan](../security/08-TEST-AND-VERIFICATION-PLAN.md). ## 2. Activity, operator and market-structure questions [I]

1. For **each pair and target jurisdiction**, what legal characterizations apply to the Solana instrument, native ZEC, the Kerb auction/fill, and any *right to a committee payout or refund*? Does the answer change for a non-settling D0 demonstration, funded testnet trial, USDC/ZEC or xStock/ZEC? Which definitions, primary authorities and current issuer terms support counsel's answer? Do not substitute the source author's characterization for a legal conclusion.
2. Who is an operator, exchange/trading venue, broker/dealer, arranger, matching facility, payment service, custodian, transfer/virtual-asset service provider or other regulated intermediary under each applicable law, given MPC operators, admission/censorship, market rules, price computation, custody quorum, upgrade/pause rights, front end/API and compensation? Would distributing a protocol without an operated front end alter that analysis, and which persons remain responsible even if matching nodes are independently governed?
3. Are there trading-system, order-handling, best-execution, fair access, market abuse, surveillance, transaction reporting, market data, recordkeeping, consumer disclosures or dispute-resolution obligations? How should encrypted orders and independent MPC produce lawfully required auditability without giving one matcher an accepted-order identity or plaintext view? If a legal requirement is incompatible with that design, what product change or market exclusion is required rather than quietly weakening privacy?
4. Who may accept a customer, determine eligibility, block a sanctioned or prohibited person and retain the evidence? What obligations attach to a custodian holding shielded ZEC, an eligibility actor knowing identity and a matcher receiving unlinkable credentials? Could the client↔eligibility↔credit mapping be lawful and practicable while separated from matcher access? What records must be disclosed on demand, to whom, and with what due process/security controls?
5. Does cross-chain execution between Solana and shielded Ironwood ZEC trigger a separate payment, transfer, exchange, travel-rule, AML/CTF, sanctions-screening, suspicious-activity, reporting or source-of-funds obligation? Which role obtains the facts needed to comply when shielded recipients/amounts are private to the public but exposed to scanner and custody signers? Is a pair launch lawful if a legally required control cannot be implemented without leaking accepted-order identity to the matcher?
6. For the chosen multi-operator governance, what are the actual legal agreements, fiduciary/client-asset duties, threshold signer responsibilities, data-controller/processor allocations, breach procedures, governing law and recourse when signers collude, censor, lose shares, misdirect funds, or the coordinator proves/attests a false payment?

## 3. Custody, settlement and incident questions [I]

7. How should legal title, beneficial ownership and customer claim on a shielded ZEC custody note and on an SPL escrow token be documented **before and after** `Pending`, `Funded`, `Authorized`, each `Sent` and `Final` leg, `Complete` or `Disputed`? Who holds client assets, who is the creditor on insolvency, and are per-pair assets legally and operationally segregated? Is a threshold-controlled receiver distinguishable from a platform's custodial account in relevant regimes?
8. What disclosures and consent are required for both non-atomic release orders: ZEC paid first but SPL freezes/refunds (buyer's loss); SPL released first but committee withholds/misdirects ZEC (seller's loss)? Who allocates loss for chain reorg, insufficient custody assets, bad quorum, upgrade, false attestations, recipient error or issuer freeze? What remedy exists if a live quorum cannot or will not refund, given **no unilateral Ironwood ZEC recovery** and no guaranteed timeout? A Solana token refund may fail on issuer freeze or if a leg was already released; Zcash transaction expiry only rejects an *unmined* transaction [P: [ZIP 203](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0203)].
9. What asset safeguarding, proof/reconciliation, key custody, independent signer, capital, insurance, examination, audit, pause and incident-notification duties arise before **any** testnet trial involving externally held test ZEC? What separate duties would apply to real USDC/ZEC or xStock/ZEC? How are customer claims against a committee enforced if all signers become unavailable? Do not describe FROST alone as an asset-protection or refund mechanism [P: [ZIP 312 threat model](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0312#threat-model), draft, describes privacy-privileged coordinator/shareholders and signer limitations].
10. Is the on-Solana threshold attestation a legally adequate representation of payment or merely a signers' statement? What contract specifies correct recipient/amount, confirmation policy, evidence retention, dispute process and remedies when a valid quorum knowingly signs falsely or the Zcash chain reorganizes? How should the UI describe `Authorized` versus actual receipt?
11. What exposures arise if an issuer freezes, pauses, clawbacks via an authorized mechanism, or changes xStock eligibility while SPL remains locked or after ZEC has already paid? What fallback and customer communication are mandatory when **neither** a token transfer nor a ZEC refund can be compelled by a customer?
12. **Historical Sep29 message-policy question, not current wire:** what privacy/data-protection/liability consequences would the then-proposed public note-derived message_id/cmx/value/height/envelope and added watcher IVK have created? Those defaults are rejected preSep30 history; [current consumer successor](../../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md) separates opaque non-money messages from shared libraries inside Kerb-owned processes. Current reuse adds no mandatory viewer/shared key/cache/log domain; any optional watcher view or explicit public disclosure needs separate owner/counsel review and cannot serve as Kerb credit/payout proof. Do not infer deployment, lawful operations or financial/private approval from either proposal.
