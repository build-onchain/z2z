The **message branch shown in this historical diagram**, not all current Zoss, is optional **non-money diagnosis**, not admission/settlement authority: [current shared-core architecture](../../../../../../zoss/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) and [consumer successor](../../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md) govern today's library contract in the banner. Retained Sep30 public receipt has random opaque delivery_id, route/destination/verifier context, app key/nonce, explicitly public fields and ciphertext digest; no default public cmx/txid/action/block locator, source height/value, full memo or private-observation hash. Source occurrence/cmx/value/height remain privately signed watcher provenance bound to receipt digest. The preSep30 `message_id = H(domain, route_id, src_chain, cmx)` and height/value/envelope callback are **historical rejected interface**, not current wire. Opaque IDs do not prove privacy: watcher/IVK/source maps, stable app keys, timing and shared operator access remain gates. IVK cannot spend; message route stays disabled on funded critical path and diagnostic OFF by default. Message delay/reorg/disconnection cannot credit, release holds, pay or refund; compiled shared libraries are a separate Kerb-owned reuse direction, not that route.

## Roles, powers, and information [I]

| Actor | Necessary authority / view | Not entitled to infer |
|---|---|---|
| Trader client | Creates and signs domain-separated limits/expiry/nonce, encrypts order fields, controls own payout/refund destinations and the source funding transaction. | Sending a signed order is not evidence that a lock/credit exists. Client never gives its spending/viewing key to ingress. |
| Eligibility + FAA | Later candidate verifies identity/eligibility, privately reads full signed order, ownership and funding hold, then certifies SAME bounded tuple for MPC. | Privileged order+identity view must be disclosed and actually outside every matcher admin/log/support/recovery domain. Not an implemented blind credential or private verifier. |
| Separate intake/batching | Receives encrypted certified tuple, availability receipts and transport metadata; fixed eight slots with authorized padding and shared payer proposed. | Cannot join identity/request→slot map into matcher view, declare unfunded orders executable or prove pre-receipt noncensorship with a root. |
| MPC matching members | Later Cerberus/distributed-preprocessing candidate uses separately controlled domains and fixed sealed outputs; participants see own price/fill, independent coordinator privileged allocation. | No default public clearing price. Output signatures alone prove neither funded membership nor payment nor identity privacy. Actual runtime/config/independence unproven. |
| Kerb Ironwood scanner | Incoming viewing capability and private note/receiver/memo/value/finality evidence; **sole authority to assign single-use confirmed credit** against a pair liability ledger. | A txid or optional Zoss receive notification alone does not establish who owns the deposit. Viewing is not spending. |
| ZEC wallet/prover coordinator and custody signers | Build and inspect note witnesses, transaction effects, recipients/amounts and sighash; threshold controls ZEC spends and committee-initiated refunds. Each signer can see sensitive payment details. | FROST shares do not impose note-level conditions, force a refund, or prove that a recipient was paid. Corrupt threshold can misdirect; unavailable threshold can freeze ZEC. |
| Settlement coordinator / relayer | Reads signed fill, funding and observed chain states; proposes a recorded leg order, obtains separate authorizations, submits Solana instructions and watches Zcash payout. A relayer is replaceable and holds no custody key by default. | Can censor or mis-sequence messages; observing a broadcast is not observing finality. |
| Solana escrow program and its governance/attestors | Proposed custody of exact SPL mint in program-authorized vault and verification of a one-time authorized state transition. Upgrade authority and attestation roster are privileged loss/censorship boundaries requiring disclosure. | Solana cannot inspect a shielded Ironwood recipient/value merely because the committee signed. An attestation is a trusted assertion, not a light-client payment proof. |

**Separation is institutional, not server count.** One matcher sees all controlled/accessible intake, RPC, FAA/eligibility, scanner/custody, support/recovery/logs plus public chain data. It must recover neither accepted-order plaintext nor participant identity. The later candidate chooses **FAA with privileged full-order+identity view** outside matchers, durable prepared holds, SAME-input private certificate verification and separate intake; it does not assert an anonymous blind credential exists. Actual governance, verifier/client binding and transport/output joins remain hard blockers even for synthetic funded testnet. Ordinary public SPL amounts/accounts/timing can defeat the boundary despite private outputs; global anonymity is not promised. See [threat model](../security/07-THREAT-MODEL.md) and [verification](../security/08-TEST-AND-VERIFICATION-PLAN.md).

**KNOWN structural privacy FAIL (§6 of [later candidate](../../docs/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-kerb-production-demo-design.md)):** permitted padded8 may have one natural seller Alice plus attacker-owned contra orders. Public epoch-bound seller escrow exposes accepted identity, and legitimate attacker buy8 output distinguishes Alice ask6 vs10. No MPC break needed; padding/private price/FAA independence do not make the permitted execution private. GD2 fails this corpus, not merely unmeasured. Architecture is safety/operations review candidate, **not currently viable private production-demo release**. Owner requirement unchanged; hidden collateral/membership/compositional evidence or explicit owner-reviewed guarantee change required, no waiver selected.

## Pair identity and private versus public evidence [I]

Market domain = `(Solana cluster, exact SPL mint address, mint's actual owning token program, Zcash network, Ironwood pool, pair policy/rules version)`. A ticker, cross-chain representation or another testnet/mainnet mint does not match. One pair has its own receiver, credit/liability ledger, escrow namespace, authorized signer configuration and incident state. Amount arithmetic uses **raw SPL atoms and integer zatoshis**, with an explicit lots/price conversion and checked overflow/rounding policy from [mechanism](../mechanism/01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md); display amounts, xStock multipliers and floating-point `uiAmount` cannot authorize transfers. Transfers cannot net across pairs. No pool solvency, operator roster, threshold or limits have been approved.

| Observer / storage | Proposed contents | Exposure or limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Scanner/custody audit record, access restricted | Private source occurrence `(network,pool,txid,action_index)` plus matched cmx; canonical context/value/receiver/claimant; credit, prepared max-debit and hold-generation maps; payouts/refunds, inventory/change, signer decisions and observed legs. | Privileged scanner/FAA/coordinator/custody can correlate records; actual ACL/retention/recovery/operator isolation remains unproven. Never copy map or raw/private-record hash into Solana. |
| Eligibility/FAA's separate records | Full signed order, legal identity/eligibility, certified SAME tuple, durable funding hold and destinations. | Required privileged FAA view, not anonymous issuance. Must not be matcher-accessible through administration, support, exports or public joins; actual governance hard gate. |
| Matcher-facing accepted set | Fixed encrypted certified slots, ciphertext availability and properly hiding immutable root, rules/config and authority decision evidence. | Exact private certificate/client verifier unimplemented. Reused IDs, intake maps or public settlement tags can reveal identity; no default public clearing price. |
| Solana transactions/accounts | Exact mint/program, signer/source/destination and SPL amount/time, escrow state, minimal authorization `(domain, pair, epoch, opaque trade/transition nonce, kind, amount/recipient necessary for transfer, previous/next state, expiry, signer authorization)` as needed. | Solana token transfer and signer are public [P: [transaction structure](https://solana.com/docs/core/transactions/transaction-structure), [token transfers](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/transfer-tokens)]; an opaque tag can itself correlate funding and trade, especially if the matcher knows it. Minimizing Zcash fields is **not** a demonstrated identity-privacy solution. |
| Ordinary Ironwood public chain | Shielded ciphertext, chain-level metadata and public aggregate data; note recipient/value not normally readable publicly. | Timing and correlated transfers still leak. [P: [ZIP 258](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0258) for Ironwood-era rules]. |

An authorization payload must not expose source txid/block hash/`cmx`, memo, raw viewing data or real-world identity unless a distinct reviewed privacy decision permits it. A threshold authorizer is not the Zcash consensus; it may lie or refuse. Whether Solana records an opaque trade ID shared with the MPC output is especially sensitive: if it lets a matcher join an accepted ciphertext to a public funding signer or payout recipient, the owner privacy gate fails. The staging design does **not** claim a safe tag/credential format exists.

## End-to-end flow and the two non-atomic windows [I]

1. **Qualify and configure:** verify exact mint/program and issuer account behavior; select pair-specific receiver, network/confirmation policy and independent authority sets before any order. For D0 only, simulate balances with synthetic identities, no deposits/spends. Real experiment requires explicit separate authorization.
2. **Fund and PREPARE before executable acceptance:** seller exact raw SPL lock and buyer scanner-confirmed unique claimant-bound Ironwood credit are privately checked by FAA. Hold seller atoms and buyer **FULL signed max debit** durably at AdmissionPrepared, before any accepted root/certificate. FAA sees full order+identity; exact private certificate/client verifier and genuine matcher separation remain unimplemented hard gates. Underfunded/stale/reorged/duplicate assets never enter executable set; no funding through Zoss.
3. **Canonical commit, then match:** fixed available prepared ciphertext root binds hold generations, rules and exact certified orders. Unanimous three-domain business-decision acknowledgements plus seller on-chain bounds and trusted buyer journal evidence precede canonical COMMIT; observe finalized COMMIT before Prepared→EpochEncumbered. Unknown/lost acknowledgement keeps prepared value held. Cutoff/expiry/zero Fill records/slow MPC cannot refund/reassign **either** side. Missing availability/root mismatch stops executable output, not holds. Definitive finalized irreversible canonical ABORT or frozen-set exclusion must fence all stale commits/authorizations before preallocation release; no fictional DB+chain atomic transaction.
4. **Finalize disjoint liabilities:** authenticate sealed certified orders/permutation/allocation and exact hold/destination/conservation. **AllocationActive** requires canonical finalized FINAL selecting SAME digest, all fixed authenticated output chunks, unanimous business acknowledgements and fully applied matching private journal. Partial journal/chain FINAL alone permits **neither unused refunds nor first-leg release**. Only then M,N partition into N seller obligation and M−N buyer-unused returnable; seller split analogous. Portion-exclusive, disjoint simultaneous refund/fill allowed, change never new credit; candidate sponsor fees separate.
   **Source-level input/output selection, not runtime proof:** later candidate reuses vendor `@arcium-hq/client.arcisEd25519` certificate signer matched to Arcis verifier (ordinary wallet/Solana Ed25519 separate), generated `Pack<T>`, and pinned arcium-anchor0.15 `ArgBuilder.account(pubkey,offset,length)` over immutable sealed program-owned input accounts. Fixed-size authenticated extraction computations read immutable final encrypted book/result accounts; each callback bounded to ONE transaction, no assumed multi-tx feature. Exact encoding/layout/root/CU/size/availability/composite verifier remain unmeasured hard gates. Coordinator sealed output includes full certified orders, certificates, private permutation and allocations for an actual independent integer oracle; privileged plaintext role outside matcher.
   **Current exact binding details** are in the primary integrated spec: separate ciphertext-root `Rct`, private-opening prepared-membership commitment `Cholds`, and FAA plaintext-order commitment `Corder`; never treat one as proving the others. Manifest pins whole-book coordinator encryption owner/version for every extraction, not caller-selected/client/matcher key; client result key must be SAME slot's FAA-certified one-use key. Typed `SellerPayout` requires finalized exact SPL delivery, while `BuyerRefund` requires returnable disjoint claim and **does not** require SPL delivery. Entity-scoped operation IDs, coherent certificate/order/hold cutoff-expiry and non-rollback signer/journal recovery are gates, not observed properties.
5. **Selected SPL-first non-atomic settlement:** allocation/`ReserveFill` records liabilities but creates **NO live Release authorization**. Before handing any live Release signatures to a relay, all business domains durably record exact `FirstLegPrepared` then canonical first-leg fence and observe finalized policy; unknown ack/crash keeps hold. No first leg before finalized FINAL+journal activation agree. One fill at a time: exact SPL transfer finalized, then native ZEC payout from reserved buyer portion. 2-of-3 spend keys plus unanimous business decisions/one fenced writer is not BFT consensus; corrupt threshold may steal. Seller bears withholding/reorg loss, ZEC-first only historical comparison. Complete requires actual both final legs+reconciliation.
6. **Refund/incident:** PREPARED/accepted buyer FULL max debit and seller atoms cannot refund/reassign during matching delay. Final allocation permits disjoint unused refund; preallocation release needs definitive canonical irreversible ABORT/exclusion and stale-commit/authorization fences with acknowledged journals. No timer/reset qualifies. Ordinary abort forbidden after first-leg authorized/sent/ambiguous. **Selected safe filled cancellation:** all business domains fence `CancelPrepared`, stop release authorization; `cancel_fill` and `release_spl` consume SAME monotonic Solana fill state. Return filled portions only after finalized Cancelled proves SPL unreleased and ALL signer journals show no native payout intent/signature/broadcast. Release wins/prior ZEC intent/missing journal/disagreement⇒hold/Disputed, not refund; no negative Zcash proof. ZEC unused refund needs live quorum+controlled inventory/change, original note need not unspent; SPL transfer issuer-dependent. Pending/filled units cannot also refund, change never credit, dead quorum may strand claims.

**Conservation before, during and after matching:** buyer `C = available + admission_prepared + epoch_encumbered + seller_payout_outstanding + buyer_unused_outstanding + seller_payout_final + buyer_refund_final`; seller `locked total = available/locked + admission_prepared + epoch_encumbered + filled_outstanding + final_unfilled_returnable + released + returned`. Categories disjoint, pending spends outstanding, change/in-flight inputs counted once in separate inventory. Whole-credit exclusivity is not required; unit/portion exclusivity is. These are untested policy invariants, not evidence of solvency or crash-safe runtime.

## Release boundary

D0 is a **non-settling** synthetic-order exercise, not a custody demo or proof of MPC privacy. R1 requires adversarial tests against one matcher plus all services/logs it can access, **both** order-content and accepted-order identity unlinkability, one-time funded admission, independent custody/access governance, precise liabilities, release ordering and loss rules, security/legal review and explicit authorization **before testnet funding**. USDC and any xStock each require new issuer, eligibility, legal, demand and real-asset authorization gates; no xStock qualifies by default.
