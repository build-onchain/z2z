# 04 — Solana escrow program proposal

**Status:** PROPOSED staging document, 2026-09-29; advisory correction 2026-10-01. No Kerb program has been built, compiled, simulated, audited or deployed. This is not an IDL, ABI, production-ready spec, approval to deploy or authority to lock assets. The [earlier owner-review spec](../../docs/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-kerb-zolana-design.md) and [integrated production-demo candidate](../../docs/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-kerb-production-demo-design.md) remain proposed; the latter selects synthetic legacy SPL Token on Solana devnet / native Ironwood Zcash testnet as the first funded-demo target, not permission to fund it. [I] = proposed rule; [P] = source claim (retrieved 2026-09-29 unless dated), not Kerb runtime evidence; [U] = unproved implementation/dependency. Three isolated markets remain on the roadmap, not the legacy two-SPL-token auction. See [architecture](03-SYSTEM-ARCHITECTURE.md), [mechanism](../mechanism/01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md) and [threats](../security/07-THREAT-MODEL.md).

## What the program can and cannot decide

[I] The on-chain side can custody and transfer a **seller's exact SPL mint** under the mint's actual Token or Token-2022 program and enforce replay-safe transition rules. It cannot read Ironwood shielded note contents, evaluate Zcash consensus/finality, prove that a buyer owns a ZEC deposit, force a committee ZEC payout/refund, or ensure atomic settlement. The buyer's ZEC goes to pair-specific **threshold-committee custody**, not a Solana vault. A committee's on-chain authorization is a **trusted claim** about checked private evidence; the program can verify that the right authorized signers signed exact bytes, but not that their claim is true. The committee may collude, censor or lose shares. Separate signer keys/verification are required for Solana authorization and Ironwood spending; do not treat a [ZIP 312](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0312#threat-model) FROST spend signature as automatically verifiable by Solana or as an on-note condition [P, ZIP draft].

A public SPL source/epoch escrow and eventual recipient/amount are observable [P: [transaction structure](https://solana.com/docs/core/transactions/transaction-structure), [token transfer](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/transfer-tokens)]. **Known structural privacy FAIL [I], not merely untested:** with one natural seller Alice plus attacker-owned contra orders/padding, epoch-bound seller membership reveals Alice. Attacker buy8 and its legitimate own fill distinguish her secret ask6 versus10 without breaking MPC. Opaque IDs, sealed price, FAA separation/padding do not satisfy the unconditional matcher content+accepted-identity guarantee. [Parent §6](../../docs/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-kerb-production-demo-design.md#6-matching-rules-and-confidentiality-of-results) marks GD2 failed for this permitted corpus. Current public membership design is **not viable private production-demo release**; hidden collateral/membership plus compositional output evidence or explicit owner-reviewed guarantee change is prerequisite. No waiver selected; funded matching remains disabled.

## Domain, accounts and authority [I]

No deployed addresses, seeds, program ID or named live roster are selected. The integrated candidate selects **three independent business-decision domains with unanimous acknowledgement**, native Solana Ed25519 authorization keys separate from **2-of-3 Zcash FROST spend keys**, zero participant protocol fees and separately sponsored network fees. One unavailable business domain pauses policy-authorized state changes; two corrupt spend domains can still bypass policy and steal ZEC. This is fail-stop trust, not Byzantine ledger consensus or proof of independence. Account names below are proposed and require owner/discriminator/version/bump checks; a PDA or opaque ID does not establish private admission.

| Account / derived namespace | Owner, contents and authority | Invariant |
|---|---|---|
| `Config` (program-owned, domain-separated singleton) | Program/cluster version, disclosed upgrade/pause authority, three independent business authorization domains with native Ed25519 key roster/version, unanimous acknowledgement policy and expiry. | No sweep or liability erasure. No local two-signature downgrade on offline signer, rotation or recovery; authority migration is separately reviewed. Upgrade authority can bypass checks and remains a trust risk. |

No account stores Zcash txid, `cmx`, memo, viewing key, witness or legal identity; source and buyer credit/hold mapping stay in restricted custody audit. Do not publicly open per-order certificate/commitment/index→escrow/fill mapping; only epoch-level root/context and the necessary eventual SPL transfer are public. Public prepared-set commitments must be properly hiding/randomized, not dictionaryable hashes of identifiable raw maps. The FAA candidate privately certifies full client-signed plaintext plus funding/identity outside matcher access; actual encrypted certificate relation, access graph and public settlement joins remain unproved. Layout/seeds/size/rent/replay retention and paused exits need executable review; no account closes while liabilities or replay markers remain.

### Proposed authorization envelope [I]

A canonical Solana authorization binds Kerb/program/cluster domain, Zcash network/pool, exact mint/token program, pair/receiver-policy version, epoch/root/rules/final activation digest, canonical phase and monotonic fence, opaque transition/fill ID, seller escrow and SPL recipient/raw atoms when needed, previous/next state, one-use nonce, created/expiry and roster version. Private buyer hold/portion IDs, journal version, FAA certificate and shielded evidence stay in signer audit; public decision commitments/claims authenticate minimum custody context without exposing a per-order input→hold map. The candidate uses **all three distinct authorized native Ed25519 authorizers over identical canonical bytes**, with exact instruction-sysvar/precompile validation. Encoding, offsets, transaction size/CU and verified signer behavior remain [U]. FAA's proposed Arcis SHA3-512 certificate signature is **not native Ed25519** and is verified privately in MPC, not passed to this program; FROST spends use separate keys/ciphersuite. A coordinator signature or merely signer-looking bytes is never sufficient.

Program checks current accounts/vault/canonical phase/entity/generation/prior version/nonce, native signatures and no reserve/release after Abort/Cancel. Each policy acknowledgement signs exact predecessor journal head/hash/writer generation, entity prior/next version and effects digest, durably persisted before signatures. Private non-rollback high-water/used business intents/signing nonces reconcile independently retained heads plus **all potentially live signed transactions/input locks**, including different physical notes paying same liability. Stale/missing restore history halts; nullifiers only prevent same-note spend, not double business payment. Policy remains fail-stop trust, not DB+chain consensus. Freeze client order/certificate admission eligibility/expiry **at canonical cutoff**; delayed computation cannot shrink accepted set or free holds by wall-clock, separate from new monetary-intent expiry. Known privacy FAIL is not solved by these safety checks.

First-leg preparation is **two-store fenced**, not atomic: unanimous journal `FirstLegPrepared`, compatible canonical phase/fence, observed agreement, then live Release signatures. Unknown decision/acknowledgement retains exposure; Reserve signatures never transfer. Explicit safe filled cancellation is the separate exception to ordinary abort prohibition: unanimous `CancelPrepared` fences native payout generation, canonical `cancel_fill` competes with `release_spl` on one transition, and only observed finalized Cancelled + SPL unreleased + all durable journals with no native payout intent allow private release. A previously issued SPL Release becomes unusable at that cancelled fence; if release wins, cancellation cannot return value. Solana cannot independently verify absence of native intent; quorum testimony/common journals remain trusted. Missing evidence preserves Disputed liabilities.

These are candidate interfaces, not proof of hidden FAA certificate/rules/custody predicates. Do not repurpose old maker-ladder, two-SPL-vault, fee-vault or settle-maker semantics. The integrated candidate specifies zero participant fees, operator-sponsored real fees, fixed eight orders, no oracle/residual/amend/min-fill and sealed outputs with no public price/per-order identifiers. Actual bounds/rent/fees/signature fit need measurement; missing manifest values disable funding rather than invent permissive defaults.

## Two-ledger state and expiry [I]

```text
Seller atoms: LockedAvailable → AdmissionPrepared → EpochEncumbered
                                      (finalized root + matching hold generation)
     active immutable FINAL + journal activation ─┬→ FillReserved → Released
                                                 └→ FinalUnused → RefundFinal
     finalized exclusion / safe fenced pre-first-leg abort → FinalUnused
     uncertain commit/result/intent or any first leg → Disputed/held

Buyer value (PRIVATE journal; not Solana-verified):
 Available → AdmissionPrepared(full max debit) → EpochEncumbered(full max debit)
     active immutable FINAL + journal activation ─┬→ SellerPayoutOutstanding
                                                 │      → InFlight → PayoutFinal
                                                 └→ BuyerUnusedOutstanding
                                                        → InFlight → RefundFinal
     same fenced exclusion/abort policy; timeout/zero fills NEVER frees held debit

Canonical epoch: Prepare → Commit → Final → FirstLegAuthorized/Sent/Final
                                       → SecondLegSent/Final → Complete
     pre-Final/no-first-leg irreversible Abort; any ambiguity → Disputed/held
```

Each order funds its own side. AcceptedHeld requires finalized exact root plus matching prepared/private encumbrance; certificates/receipts/Zoss alone cannot execute. **AllocationActive** means exact canonical finalized Final + all authenticated chunks + all three durable acknowledgements + **complete private allocation application**. This gates **unused refunds and first-leg Release alike**, not just result status. NativeSellerPayout then additionally requires exact observed SPLFinal; NativeBuyerRefund needs its own unused/excluded/aborted/safe-cancelled returnable portion and does **not require SPL transfer**, which no-cross cases lack. Authorized/Sent/Final remain distinct; Complete needs both actual final effects. SPL-first is selected loss-review target, not atomic or viable private release under the known membership/inference counterexample.

For a buyer credit `C`, retain disjoint `available + admission_prepared + epoch_encumbered + seller_payout_outstanding + buyer_unused_outstanding + seller_payout_final + buyer_refund_final = C`. The full signed max debit includes required quantity×limit and the chosen signed fee cap; demo participant fees are zero and actual network fees use separate sponsor inventory. In-flight sends are **substates of outstanding**, not final or free value. A final immutable allocation splits filled debit and unused price/quantity/fee slack; disjoint payout/refund portions of one credit can coexist, never the same entitlement unit. Physical input-note locks separately prevent competing spends. Seller funded atoms have the analogous disjoint available/prepared/epoch-held/filled-outstanding/final-unused/released/refunded partition. [Off-chain reconciliation](06-OFFCHAIN-SERVICES.md) defines inventory/change backing.

Admission order/certificate eligibility/expiry freezes at canonical cutoff; expiry stops **new** admission but delayed MPC cannot mutate accepted membership. Monetary authorization/chain tx expiry separate. Neither cutoff nor timeout erases prepared/accepted/fill exposure. Active-Final unused value requires AllocationActive; immutable exclusion, safely pre-Final fenced abort or explicit finalized filled cancellation require matching own-portion journal disposition. No DB+chain atomic transition; uncertainty retains holds. First-leg prepared/authorized/sent/unknown forbids ordinary abort; selected cancel/release shared-state exception requires finalized Cancelled/SPL unreleased/all durable no-native-intent evidence. [ZIP203](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0203) [P] is unmined expiry, not refund covenant. NativeBuyerRefund needs controlled usable inventory/change/live quorum, **not original note unspent nor SPL delivery**. Reorg/loss preserves obligations; no recovery guarantee.

## Token/account and failure checks [I]

* Verify the **mint account owner** equals the configured Token/Token-2022 program, account token owners/authorities/mint/decimals/state exactly match configuration, and CPI program is allowlisted; verify PDA derivation and discriminator for each account. Checked raw integer arithmetic prevents overflow, underflow, fraction rounding and amount-for-different-mint confusion. No floating-point UI amount or xStock display multiplier is a custody amount. Token-2022 extensions, default account states, permanent delegate, transfer hook, transfer fee and issuer powers require [per-mint qualification](05-TOKEN-2022-ISSUER-INTEGRATION.md), not assumptions from the mint name. [P: [Solana token transfers](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/transfer-tokens), [Token extensions](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions)].
* Re-evaluate vault **spendable** balance against remaining recorded obligations before each action. A shortfall freezes affected claims and halts new fills; do not pay first claimant from insolvent inventory or count donations as authorization. Program logic cannot prevent an issuer with freeze/delegate/upgrade authority from later immobilizing or draining tokens. Freeze/shortfall and partial leg-finality are visible as incidents, not auto-success.
* Reject cross-pair/chain/cluster replay, duplicate `fill_id` or nonce, stale roster/version, invalid signer threshold, wrong recipient, mixed epoch/rules root, over-reservation and release after cancellation. Pause new work on reorg/issuer uncertainty; a rollback of off-chain observations does not reverse a finalized Solana transfer. All administrative or emergency powers require a public governance and loss-allocation policy **before** any authorized funded trial.
