# 05 — Exact-mint, Token-2022 and issuer qualification

**Status:** PROPOSED staging draft, 2026-09-29; not an allowlist, issuer approval, on-chain finding, legal advice, or authorization to fund/deploy. No live mint or xStock has passed Kerb qualification, and no Kerb issuer transfer has been exercised. [P] = primary protocol/vendor statement, not independent confirmation of an exact live account; [I] = proposed Kerb policy; [U] = missing evidence. External sources cited here retrieved 2026-09-29. This profile applies to the **seller's SPL asset** in three separate Solana↔Ironwood markets; the **buyer's native shielded ZEC** is committee custody on Zcash, not a Token-2022 balance. See [system architecture](03-SYSTEM-ARCHITECTURE.md) and [Solana escrow proposal](04-ONCHAIN-PROGRAM-DESIGN.md).

## Identity and denomination [I]

A market binds `(Solana cluster, exact mint pubkey, actual owning token program, mint decimals, Zcash network, Ironwood pool, pair policy version)`; a trade/funding authorization repeats that domain, epoch, rules and one-time nonce. Ticker, issuer family, an Ethereum counterpart, wrapped ZEC or a different Solana token address never substitutes for an exact mint. Separate pair receiver, scanner credit ledger, Solana vault namespace, risk/issuer review and legal cohort; no shared approval or fungible inventory. USDC does **not** become Token-2022 merely because this document is named Token-2022. [P: [Solana token transfers](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/transfer-tokens)] require a source/destination of the same mint; verify actual mint owner/token program rather than infer it from a ticker. [P: [Token-2022 extension enumeration](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions)] describes TLV extensions, not the configuration of any proposed xStock.

Use **raw SPL atoms** and integer zatoshis only. For the [D0 reference mechanism](../mechanism/01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md), `q` lots consume `q × lot_raw` SPL atoms and a price of `p` ticks with `tick_zat` zatoshis per lot obligates `q × p × tick_zat` zatoshis; all three positive integer parameters and transfer-range checks are part of that synthetic reference, **not selected production market values**. Never compute custody or fee from floating-point `uiAmount` or a share display; do not imply a fixed historical price survives a corporate-action multiplier change. Record asset denomination and maximum raw debit in the client's signed order before encryption. Funded-use fee cap/asset, dust and legal unit definition are unapproved [U].

The issuer's general [xStocks description](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs) [P for issuer self-description] and [corporate-action guide](https://docs.xstocks.fi/docs/dividends-and-stock-splits.md) [P] cannot prove a particular mint's extensions, security properties or permitted Kerb participants. The issuer says the Solana raw token account amount remains constant while a display multiplier represents corporate actions; its guide advises venues to pause around scheduled activation, including 00:30 UTC after ex-date. Solana [Scaled UI Amount](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/scaled-ui-amount) [P] likewise says a multiplier changes displayed units, not raw tokens, and UI conversions may not round-trip due to floating-point arithmetic. Therefore a pre-event raw lot can have a different *economic* meaning after the event; snapshots plus activation guard/abort and customer disclosure are needed, not a silent raw-atom relabel. The issuer can schedule **or immediately** update a multiplier [P]; a scheduled window alone is insufficient.

## Candidate technical policy for an exact mint [I]

The policy below is deliberately conservative because a seller's token can remain in escrow while ZEC moves irreversibly. It is **proposed** and untested for actual Solana accounts. A type's *presence* is not proof of compatibility; an account's dynamic state can change after approval. A future exception requires a separately reviewed mechanism, tests and owner approval, not a UI warning.

| Feature / authority [P: [Solana extension inventory](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions)] | Proposed qualification and live guard [I] | Loss path |
|---|---|---|
| Legacy Token program versus Token-2022 | Parse actual mint account owner and supported state; qualify exact source, PDA vault, return account and payout account against that same program. Do not call Token-2022 CPI against a legacy mint. | Wrong program or account mint can mis-route or fail transfers. |
| `TransferHook` | **Refuse** an involved mint with the extension at all in the first proposal, even when its program ID is unset. [P: [Transfer Hook](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/transfer-hook)] invokes a custom CPI for transfers. | Hook authority/program upgrade may change behavior after deposit; a hook that fails blocks payout, while a hook that succeeds can have other effects. A successful CPI is not proof future transfers are safe. |
| `PermanentDelegate` | **Refuse** when this extension grants a delegate on the mint; conservative proposal also refuses the extension pending a reviewed absence/rotation rule. [P: [Permanent Delegate](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/permanent-delegate)] lets the delegate transfer/burn from any account and rotate. | Delegate can debit Kerb vault without Kerb PDA authorization; a fingerprint does not stop it exercising existing power. |
| `TransferFeeConfig` / withheld fee variants | **Refuse** until a new fee-aware exact-received-amount mechanism is reviewed. [P: [Transfer Fees](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/transfer-fees)] withholds fees on recipient. | Raw debit differs from recipient's spendable credit; inventory/payout promise and conservation fail. |
| Freeze authority, `DefaultAccountState`, `Pausable` | If present, conditional **only** on verified ability to initialize and keep vault/return/buyer destination transferable at each action; re-read dynamic frozen/paused flags. No account can be assumed thawed merely because it exists. | Issuer may freeze vault or recipient / pause mint after ZEC finality, preventing SPL delivery or refund indefinitely; pausing admission cannot repair the already-paid ZEC leg. |
| `ScaledUiAmount` | Conditional on recording current/next multiplier and update authority, guard the whole admission→match→two-leg settlement window, stop new work on changes/uncertainty and show raw atoms plus effective display. Requalification for an unannounced immediate change. | Economic exposure of raw lots changes mid-epoch even if transfer amount remains identical. No price oracle/corporate-action policy is established. |
| `ConfidentialTransferMint` and account confidential states | Mere presence does not make Kerb private. Only a specifically qualified **public-balance/public `TransferChecked`** route with zero need for confidential pending/available/proof workflows; otherwise refuse. [P: [Confidential Balances](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer)] keeps addresses public even when amounts are private. | Hidden and public balances have different spendability and proof requirements; naive vault accounting overstates funds. Solana public lock still exposes amount/signer and can link identity. Auditor keys may grant issuer-side amount visibility. |
| `NonTransferable`, incompatible `CpiGuard`, required `MemoTransfer` | Refuse if the escrow and return/delivery transfers cannot be executed under exact source/recipient states and transaction shape. An approved memo requirement needs its own reviewed instruction/account policy. | A profitable fill can be impossible to transfer; a sidecar memo can leak a trade. |
| Unknown extension, authority or mutable metadata pointer | Refuse pending versioned parsing and reviewed semantics. Mutable name/symbol/metadata cannot choose a market; exact mint and program do. | Permissive decode or ticker fallback can select an impostor or unsupported privilege. |

None of these checks waives policy for an otherwise technically transferable mint. In particular, a frozen-by-default xStock account may need issuer thaw, a freeze authority can act later, and restrictions on holder/jurisdiction require counsel/issuer review. Kerb cannot unconditionally promise SPL refund just because its own program allows it. The opposite Ironwood deposit never has user-enforceable timeout refund under the selected committee trust model; [ZIP 203](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0203) [P] expires only *unmined* Zcash transactions.

## Qualification evidence record and recheck points [I]

For each candidate mint, prepare a versioned private qualification record with: full mint pubkey and owning token program, Solana cluster/slot/time, decimals, raw TLV type and value decode (or legacy state), all mint/freeze/delegate/hook/pause/multiplier authorities, current and scheduled dynamic values, source/vault/destination account owner/mint/program/state, observed transfer conditions, issuer terms and eligibility/counsel decision. Store a hash/reference in the proposed market configuration, but keep the source bytes and source retrieval date for audit. Do not claim byte-level verification from this draft; no live account was queried [U].

Candidate lifecycle:

1. **Before opening a market:** independently verify Circle or issuer-published address and exact on-chain owner/extension bytes, token-account initialization/thaw route, real recipient/return rules, unit display, signer authorities and issuer/legal restrictions. Qualification of one mint cannot qualify another. Obtain consent/authorization for any transaction-based test separately.
2. **Before admitting funding or an epoch:** reread authority/extension identity and dynamic pause, account state and multiplier schedule; halt if a risky change overlaps admission→clearance/settlement horizon. Include the actual network/mint/program in every signed order and escrow. An on-chain guard must enforce safety-critical conditions it can observe; an off-chain monitor is not a substitute for CPI checks.
3. **Before seller lock:** validate source and proposed vault account state and raw transfer result; record exact spendable balance, not client-provided amount. If source/vault cannot transfer, reject before creating an executable accepted order.
4. **Before SPL release or safe refund:** reread mint/program, vault spendable balance, paused/frozen flags and recipient state; refuse wrong or closed destination, check correct CPI result and leave liability outstanding if transfer fails. If issuer freezes **after** ZEC payout, mark disputed and communicate the non-atomic loss; do not mark complete or promise a wait bound. A stale fingerprint or issuer event after matching requires incident judgment, not pretending the already-final ZEC spend can be reversed.
5. **On upgrade, corporate action, freeze or discovered shortfall:** stop affected pair's new work, snapshot private/public evidence, compare every raw entitlement with actual vault inventory, quarantine surplus, and reconcile claims; resume only under reviewed policy. Upgrade authority, mint issuer, ZEC spend quorum and Solana program governance are separate privileged domains.

A synthetic local proof should cover exact-mint wrong-program rejection, transfer-hook/delegate/fee refusals, frozen/paused/default-frozen return and recipient, multiplier change crossing match/settlement, delegated vault shortfall, late refund after ZEC payout, and account closure/replay. No such Kerb tests exist or ran for this staging suite. See [off-chain incident service](06-OFFCHAIN-SERVICES.md), [verification plan](../security/08-TEST-AND-VERIFICATION-PLAN.md) and [legal questions](../legal/09-REGULATORY-AND-LEGAL.md). R2/R3 need separately reviewed real-asset and legal gates even after R1 research; an issuer document is not consent to operate a market.
