## Scanner and single-use funding credit [I]

The buyer, not the seller, deposits **native shielded Ironwood ZEC** to the **pair-specific Kerb custody receiver**. This is committee custody, not a Solana SPL token and not a ZEC timeout escrow. [ZIP 258](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0258) [P] specifies the NU6.3 Ironwood pool and post-upgrade restrictions on adding to Orchard; target Ironwood receipts for this design. The scanner's IVK-derived view may reveal receiver, value, memo/reference and note identifiers to its operators. Select and independently govern receiver/IVK holder, checkpointed chain source, per-pair confirmation depth, reorg tolerance and key-rotation policy **before** any authorized deposit. Zcash tx expiry [ZIP 203](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0203) [P] only limits mining of an *unmined* transaction; it is not a refund condition on a mined note.

A private ledger [I] records each confirmed external deposit's `(network, pool, pair receiver, private source occurrence, note/cmx, txid/block reference, raw zatoshis, claimant authorization, confirmation/reorg history)` and separate **amount entitlements**. A txid can contain multiple notes; `cmx` alone is not the business credit ID or a globally unique source occurrence. Scanner receipt detection plus claimant-right verification and a separately governed **custody wallet/full-view/nullifier/spend-authority inventory process** must establish controlled, unallocated value before one credit is issued. An IVK alone cannot verify outgoing spends [P: [ZIP 316](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0316), accessed 2026-09-29]. Chain note nullifiers track chain spends; one-use certificate/credential redemption IDs and order/transition nonces track business replay. They are not interchangeable. Reindexing, re-inclusion or change never creates another claimant credit.

For each credit amount `C`, conserve disjoint entitlement portions at every transition: `available + admission_prepared + epoch_encumbered + seller_payout_outstanding + buyer_unused_outstanding + seller_payout_final + buyer_refund_final = C`. `admission_prepared` is a durable provisional hold before the accepted root; `epoch_encumbered` is the **full maximum debit** of an order included in the finalized canonical root, before any fill reservation exists. For a buyer this debit covers its signed quantity at its signed limit plus the chosen signed participant-fee cap, never exceeding signed max funding. The demo selects zero protocol/participant fees and separately sponsored actual network fees: sponsored fees cannot silently reduce `C`. A later charged-fee policy needs a new version and disjoint fee partitions, not a hidden deduction. A truly separate, never-prepared surplus stays `available`; no predicted price, fill or fee/bound slack **within a held debit** becomes unused before an immutable final result, fenced final exclusion or safely invalidated epoch.

Per portion: `Available → AdmissionPrepared → EpochEncumbered → SellerPayoutOutstanding → PayoutFinal`; unused/unfilled/slack instead enters `BuyerUnusedOutstanding → RefundRequested → RefundInFlight → RefundFinal` **only after canonical finalized Final and matching journal activation agree**. Prepared excluded portions need irreversible finalized membership/abort plus matching fence. `PayoutInFlight`/`RefundInFlight` are substates of **outstanding**, not extra summands; disputes preserve liabilities. Both prepared and accepted portions reject refund/reassignment/another order while MPC delayed, even with zero fills. Filled payout and final-unused buyer portions may coexist, including simultaneous disjoint reservations; same unit cannot appear in both. `ReserveFill` alone is not a live `Release` signature: exact filled cancellation is separately reviewed/fenced before first-leg authority, never an epoch timeout.

Never-prepared genuinely `available` value may be reassigned or moved once into `BuyerUnusedOutstanding` for an owner-authorized refund under current unanimous business/input-lock policy; it is not an accepted max-debit portion. For prepared value, finalized exclusion/safe abort is the alternative to active Final, not a requirement to produce a clearing result for an excluded order. Exclusion must be immutable at the canonical generation with matching private disposition; unknown commit or possibly stale membership is held. Final allocation cancellation returns only the exact safely fenced filled unit, never calls all credit unused.

Example [I, arithmetic-only]: credits24+10 fully held at signed max debit have34 `epoch_encumbered`, zero returnable slack before active Final. The immutable canonical result **and matching private activation** allocating20+5 move25 to seller outstanding and9 to buyer unused; final payout moves only paid units final. The9 remains a liability after original note→change. Refund needs live quorum and **reconciled controlled inventory/change**, not original note unspent; no unilateral ZEC refund/recovery deadline. Disjoint liabilities do not permit same-input races: wallet locks serialize sends or combine exact approved effects. Unconfirmed change is restricted provisional backing, never available spendable value/new credit; do not count it and consumed input together. Reorg/uncertain effects quarantine portions/pause assignment; impairment preserves liabilities rather than erasing them or recreating credit. Witness/key usability, multi-note selection, fee/dust/finality/insolvency policy still need proof [U].

**Historical optional Zoss messaging profile only — OFF / non-money, not the overall current shared core.** [Current consumer successor](../../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md), [architecture](../../../../../../zoss/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#52-private-observation-và-public-receipt) and [sole core blocker register](../../../../../../zoss/docs/BLOCKERS.md) now separately cover shared-library reuse. The retained message scope is authenticated **non-money Ironwood testnet → Solana devnet messaging**. Watchers retain private signed provenance and sign a destination-domain **opaque public receipt**; default public bytes contain no cmx-derived locator, txid/action/block hash, height/value/raw memo or private-record hash. Public route/app key/nonce, disclosed fields, ciphertext digest, accounts/timing/private maps still permit joins; opacity is not matcher privacy evidence. Only the same Kerb custody receiver **and same note** makes diagnosis relevant; Kerb financial authority stays independent of delivery. Message/source dedup is not funding ownership/one-use admission/monetary replay. Source observation/receipt cannot authorize spends, payout, absence/refund or reverse effects. No message route, funding gate or payout fallback is enabled; core gate definitions/statuses are not copied here.

If a separately approved diagnostic route is later researched, use one operator app identity, never participant/order diagnostic keys or public credit/hold/trade tags. Route stays OFF by default; happy/fault Kerb workflows must work with **Zoss message runtime disconnected**, even if compiled shared libraries are used, and reconnect must have zero credit/hold/settlement effects. Exact public/private byte, app-key/timing and operator-access attacks remain required; receipt opacity is not an exemption. Destination rollback retries only the same immutable still-valid receipt, not cross-chain recovery. One-source→one-receipt route/deployment namespace is unresolved; Kerb financial uniqueness and funded evidence/witness/recovery lifetime remain independent of message epochs/revocation.

## Admission, availability and matching contract [I]

The integrated candidate uses the **independently governed FAA alternative**, not an assumed complete anonymous proof primitive [I]. FAA checks a standard client wallet signature off-chain and certifies the exact bounded canonical plaintext order, network/pool/cluster/exact mint/program/pair/epoch/rules, maximum debit/fee cap, private hold ID/version/fence, one-use client authorization/result keys, destinations, business redemption ID, nonce and expiry. Client encrypts that same tuple, hidden certificate and possession authorization; MPC verifies the pinned scheme **inside encryption**, derives commitment from the same plaintext it clears, and rejects changed quantity/limit/domain/hold/cap/owner/destination. FAA knows order + identity + funding and remains outside every matcher control/access domain. Ordinary wallet Ed25519, the Arcis-compatible certificate candidate (SHA3-512 Ed25519 is **not native Ed25519**) and Solana authorizer keys/encoding are separate; circuit/interoperability and one-use possession checks are [U]. No blindness/global anonymity is claimed. Certificates neither unlock funds nor independently prove a dishonest authority funded them; canonical hold/custody evidence remains mandatory.

The [parent candidate](../../docs/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-kerb-production-demo-design.md) selects SDK `arcisEd25519`, immutable sealed input accounts/account-backed packing-extraction and bounded fixed result computations, not assumed arbitrary multi-transaction callback. **Distinct bindings:** `Rct` authenticates exact ordered ciphertext accounts/bytes/owner/nonce/layout/slot/generation; privately opened randomized hiding `Cholds` binds exact entitlement membership/versions; FAA `Corder` is recomputed from certified plaintext **inside MPC**. Plaintext oracle cannot derive `Rct`; coordinator separately authenticates account/queue/computation provenance and checks `Corder`/holds privately. Their relation is unanimous business testimony, not independent ZK funding equality. Manifest pins coordinator whole-book output key/version, authenticated by queue and **every extraction**; never caller-selected owner, client key or matcher key. Each client result uses only its FAA-certified one-use result key. Missing pinned key ⇒ hold/versioned reviewed recovery, no reveal/fallback recipient. Full certified book/permutation sealed to coordinator enables independent oracle. Hiding commitments do not hide public seller membership or cure known FAIL; interoperability/authenticity/fit remain [U].

**Prepare and canonical decision, not a distributed database/chain transaction:** prepare that order's own seller raw SPL **or** buyer full max-debit portion for domain/epoch/monotonic generation, not an unmatched trader's opposite leg. Buyer prepare uses one serializable journal transaction and identical **unanimous three-domain business acknowledgements**; seller prepare is a checked escrow transition. Receipts denote prepared availability, not executable commitment. Private manifest retains ciphertext and hold/version evidence; public context is epoch-level root and properly hiding prepared-set commitments, not per-order hold/slot→escrow mapping. At cutoff, `commit_accepted_set` records one root after actual seller prepared bounds and trusted buyer-ledger hold testimony match the unanimous prepare decision. Finalized canonical Solana membership governs idempotent promotion to `epoch_encumbered`; no computation/authorization until included holds/availability reconcile at that fence. Crash, uncertain commit or delayed journal acknowledgement keeps **both** classes held. Solana verifies seller state and identical signed bytes, **not private buyer ledger truth**. Runtime fault evidence is [U]; this is not simultaneous consensus across ledgers.

Reject wrong-domain/unfunded/duplicate/insufficient/late orders before cutoff. Canonical cutoff **freezes order/certificate eligibility and expiry disposition**; delayed MPC cannot wall-clock-expire or mutate accepted membership/free backing. Monetary intent/chain transaction expiry is separate. Operation identity is `(deployment,pair,epoch,stable entity/hold/portion/intent ID,generation,prior version,transition)`, not just epoch/generation shared by distinct entities; IDs/terminal fences survive restore/rotation/closure, generations never ABA-reused. Availability receipt/root conflicts keep holds; final exclusion or pre-Final irrevocable abort consumes exact fences so stale claims reject. Abort requires unanimous durable/current canonical no-first-leg intent reconciliation; FAA cannot cancel alone. Active Final filled portions need explicit cancellation, never ordinary epoch timeout. First-leg ambiguity retains Disputed; disjoint unused rights remain separate.

**`AllocationActive` is conjunctive:** canonical finalized Final selects exact result digest, **all authenticated fixed output chunks present**, all three business domains acknowledge the same decision, and private journal **fully applies the entire allocation** at that digest/generations. Coordinator privately checks full certified orders/permutation with independent oracle and current holds. Prepare allocations → unanimous exact decision → canonical Final → idempotent full journal activation. **Neither unused seller/buyer refunds nor any first-leg Release may execute before all four conditions**; chain Final alone/partial apply is insufficient. Missing chunk/ack, restart/reorg or activation gap retains all affected encumbrance; recover same decision, never reprice/replace it. Final unfilled/bound/fee slack becomes returnable only under AllocationActive, with separate fenced exclusion/abort/cancel paths for those cases.

A matching committee may use fixed-size MPC for auditable integer rules/root/lot/tick/ties/conservation. Candidate selects eight orders and Arcium Cerberus **distributed preprocessing**; backend/independence/recovery/upgrade access and certificate checks remain [U]. No public `(placed_slot, owner)` ties. [Cerberus](https://docs.arcium.com/multi-party-execution-environments-mxes/mpc-protocols), [governance](https://docs.arcium.com/clusters/permissioned-clusters), [limitations](https://docs.arcium.com/developers/limitations) [P] are vendor leads, not Kerb proof. Signatures do not independently prove inclusion/clearing/funding. Fixed ciphertext slots go to independent coordinator and sealed own results to clients: **no public price or per-order IDs/openings/index→hold/escrow/fill relation**. Epoch-only root/context still permits timing joins, not identity proof. Reference vectors/planned attacks are in [mechanism](../mechanism/01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md), integrated candidate and [verification plan](../security/08-TEST-AND-VERIFICATION-PLAN.md).

## Settlement coordination and signer checks [I]

For each sealed fill, check immutable active root/result/rules, seller's already-encumbered vault portion and buyer's held debit, destinations and canonical generation. Split filled/final-unused once; no funding reacquisition. One **authoritative serialized journal with one fenced writer per market** supplies decisions, all three business domains persist identical acknowledgements, and signers check canonical Solana. Overlapping 2-of-3 quorums can authorize contradictory histories: require **unanimous business decisions** separately from **2-of-3 FROST spend**. Offline acknowledgement halts policy progress; corrupt spend threshold can still steal. Persist exact intent/portion/input locks/version/fence/effects before signing/broadcast; reconcile ambiguity before new intent. Demo is **SPL-first, one concurrent fill**, full payout pre-reserved; seller bears non-atomic ZEC withholding/misdirection loss. ZEC-first is buyer-loss fault comparison only. Manifest confirmation/grace/caps/loss terms need measured approved inputs, not safe guessed values/funded permission [U].

**Reserve is not Release authority:** `FillReserved`/buyer payout reservation does not sign a leg. Before handing out live SPL Release signatures, unanimously persist exact `FirstLegPrepared` and compatible canonical fence; unknown delivery blocks **ordinary** abort/refund. The selected explicit **filled-cancellation exception** can still terminate an unreleased SPL-first fill: every domain persists `CancelPrepared`, stops new Release/native payout generation and proves in its durable journal no native intent/signature/broadcast; `cancel_fill` and `release_spl` compete for the same monotonic transition. Observe finalized canonical Cancelled with SPL unreleased and apply that exact decision privately before returning either filled portion. Previously signed/submitted SPL Release loses validity at the cancelled fence. If release wins, native intent exists, any journal/chain evidence is missing or race remains unknown, retain payout liability in `Disputed`. No deadline-only/ordinary abort, negative Zoss proof or independent shielded no-spend claim; corrupt spend quorum may still ignore policy.

Native intent request binds **kind** plus exact domain/pair/portion/prior version/generation, canonical decision/journal head, current controlled inputs/nullifier/witness state, immutable recipient/value/change/sponsored fee/effects/expiry/SIGHASH. **`NativeSellerPayout`** requires AllocationActive seller liability and exact independently observed **SPLFinal** recipient/atoms. **`NativeBuyerRefund`** instead requires the disjoint buyer returnability decision (AllocationActive unused, immutable exclusion/pre-Final abort or finalized explicit safe cancel), recorded buyer destination and no competing same-portion intent/signature/broadcast; it **does not require SPL delivery**, which no-cross/excluded/aborted orders lack. Sponsor-only spend uses separate authority/inventory. Original deposit note need not remain unspent; current inventory/change/witness must be usable and not double allocated. Each signer recomputes exact effects/SIGHASH and typed predicates, never opaque hash. [ZIP312](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0312#threat-model) [P draft] discloses payment view, not conditional refunds; [ZF591](https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/frost-tools/issues/591) contributor report is research, not Kerb proof. FAA/Arcis, MPC, native Solana Ed25519 and FROST keys remain distinct.

A Solana authorization binds domain/mint/program/pair/epoch/root/rules/activation digest, canonical phase/fence, opaque transition/fill ID, SPL amount/recipient, expiry, prior/next state, nonce/roster; [escrow](04-ONCHAIN-PROGRAM-DESIGN.md) checks **all three distinct native Ed25519 authorizers on identical bytes**. Buyer portion/hold/journal/FAA/shielded evidence remains private. Never publish per-order certificate/slot/commitment→hold/escrow linkage or txid/block/`cmx`/memo/witness/identity/Ironwood address. Necessary public fill→escrow/recipient/amount belongs in matcher attacks. Solana checks accounts/signatures/replay/fence; buyer-hold/payout assertions are **trusted testimony**, not private-ledger/shielded verification. Rotation cannot revive fenced claims; resubmission cannot transfer twice. No bridge/Zoss dependency.

## Reconciliation, incident states and evidence

[I] One serializable journal/fenced writer records exact entities/versions, decisions/chunks/activation, input/effect locks and canonical states. Every unanimous acknowledgement signs **predecessor head/hash + writer generation + entity/portion prior version + next state + immutable effects digest**, persisted before monetary signing. Signers retain **non-rollback high-water, consumed business-intent and never-reused signing-nonce history**; backups cannot reset these. Restore reconciles independently retained signed heads, canonical decisions and **all possibly live signed transactions**, including alternative distinct-note inputs; restoring three pre-intent snapshots cannot permit a second business payment. Note nullifiers stop same-note double spend, not duplicate liability payments from different notes. Missing/inconsistent retained head/history/intent/lease ⇒ Disputed/policy halt, never fresh writer or signature. Unavailable business replica halts honest policy; corrupt 2-of-3 spends can bypass it. Non-rollback recovery/availability implementation remains [U], not BFT consensus. Status includes AllocationActive distinct from chain Final; source/FAA mapping stays restricted.

At every transition check, for each pair and asset separately:

- For ZEC, reconcile credit liabilities with a **backing disposition**: controlled confirmed spendable inventory, exclusively locked signed/unmined inputs, or exclusively attributed provisional canonical recipient/change effects pending finality, each counted once. Canonical input consumption replaces input entry with approved recipient effect plus controlled change—not both. Pending recipient effect backs only its own in-flight outstanding payment; change awaiting policy confirmation is restricted attribution to existing liabilities, never available/reassignable/sendable. Broadcast replaces nothing; observed final recipient effect moves only corresponding liability final. This is chain-risk accounting, **not reusable spendable custody proof**. Assets supporting new available value, admission, epoch activation or another monetary intent must have usable controlled witnesses/keys and meet confirmation policy. Existing prepared/accepted/unused claims retain liability while attributed backing is restricted; no refund/reassignment/settlement can consume it until usable. Missing/uncertain/short backing quarantines portions, freezes affected assignment/sends and reports impairment without erasing obligations. Reorg reconciles same input/effects, never duplicates credit. Fees use separate sponsor inventory, never buyer deduction; IVK or historical credited-minus-paid arithmetic is insufficient.
- For each seller escrow `S`, disjoint `available_locked + admission_prepared + epoch_encumbered + buyer_release_outstanding + seller_unused_outstanding + buyer_release_final + seller_refund_final = S` raw atoms. In-flight/dispute are statuses, not duplicate sums. Transferable vault atoms cover **all not-yet-transferred** claims; observed canonical SPL transfers still pending policy finality are attributed exclusively to their own outstanding recipient effect, never simultaneously counted in vault or available again. Program-local successful CPI may record `Released`; private journal/user status remains `Sent` until independently observed finality. Final observation moves only those units final. Failed CPI consumes nothing; fork/ambiguity holds/reconciles the same units. Issuer-frozen assets stay recorded held liabilities, not paid/spendable. Excluded/active-Final unfilled atoms move once to unused; accepted atoms remain through MPC. No cross-vault/market netting.
- A one-use accepted funding certificate/hold binds exactly one order/domain/epoch at a time. **Each entitlement unit**, not the entire credit, belongs to at most one active category and one compatible terminal payout/refund. Disjoint portions of one credit can carry concurrent seller payout and buyer refund obligations; their sum plus final payments never exceeds `C`. Physical note input/witness locks are a separate exclusion rule and may serialize those sends. Unique credit issuance, portion ID, epoch/hold fence, fill/transition nonce and finality evidence survive crash/reorg; duplicate notification or change cannot mint credit or authorize value twice.
- A `Complete` report requires both actual Solana release finality and observed ZEC payout finality under separately approved policies; a signed instruction, broadcast, Zoss delivery or committee intention cannot fulfill this. Finality is chain-specific and can be invalidated by an exceptional reorg; publish incident/correction rather than rewriting history.

| Incident | Immediate honest response [I] | Residual exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Scanner lag/duplicate/false positive, conflicting ownership or Zcash reorg | Suspend affected credit and new pair admission, compare chain and note audit, protect reservations, disclose unconfirmed status, do not release or spend based on Zoss/txid alone. | A false credit already spent can create insolvency; even confirmed notes have reorg risk. |
| Matching member offline/abort or missing accepted ciphertext | Before first-leg intent/send, durably fence every would-be authorization and finalize canonical epoch invalidation; reconcile both prepared/accepted legs before releasing excluded portions. If commit, signed-spend or first-leg status is uncertain, retain holds in `Disputed`, not ordinary abort. | Censorship and custody withholding remain; no DB+chain distributed atomic transaction or independently proven no-spend. ZEC refund requires live quorum/inventory and SPL transfer issuer permission. |
| ZEC-first finalized; SPL release fails, freezes or refunds | `Disputed`; stop new payouts; preserve private payout evidence and public vault/issuer state, notify affected parties, invoke approved loss rule. | Buyer may have paid ZEC without SPL; no reversal of mined ZEC and no guaranteed issuer thaw. |
| SPL-first finalized; ZEC payout delayed, diverted or never signed | `Disputed`; stop new releases; compare signer-approved effects with mined transaction, liabilities and recipient receipt under approved procedure. | Seller may have lost SPL with no ZEC; signature threshold is not a payment covenant enforced by Solana. |
| Late/duplicate/wrong-pair quorum message or overlapping refund/payout | Reject same-portion conflict by exact domain/epoch decision/hold fence/state/nonce and authoritative journal; inspect both chains and roster. Disjoint final-unused refunds remain owed, but accepted/filled/in-flight value never times out into free balance. | Conflicting quorum histories or a false valid attestation can cause loss if journal/fences are unsound; opposite final legs cannot be reversed. |
