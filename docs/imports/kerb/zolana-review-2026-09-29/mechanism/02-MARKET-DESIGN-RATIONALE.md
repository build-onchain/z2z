## 1. Economic problem as a falsifiable hypothesis [U]

A buyer with ZEC and a seller holding an exact Solana token *might* prefer crossing directly with each other rather than requesting competing dealer quotes, if both arrive in compatible size/limit/time and the combined custody, privacy, fee, waiting and non-atomic loss costs are acceptable. **No observed opposite-side intentions, willingness to wait, trader identity-protection requirement, market-maker inventory, expected spread saving or viable fee basis has been established.** A public executed-swap tape alone omits unfilled intents and executable contemporaneous quotes; correlated on-chain timestamps cannot establish the overlap an auction needs. The archived [Idea Bank row #5](../../docs/sources/notion/idea-bank-row-5.md) [P for what its author proposed on 2026-09-28; U for commercial claims] motivates discovery but addresses tokenized-stock dark pools/other architectures and is **not** validation for native Ironwood ZEC custody or any Kerb user. The row's “How Kerb departs” paragraph refers to the older Solana-only V1, not this proposed private pivot.

The three **separate** pair hypotheses have materially different trust/economic prerequisites:

| Pair | Testable reason someone might trade; what we *do not* know |
|---|---|
| **SYNTHETIC Solana token / Ironwood testnet ZEC** [I] | D0 can stress private order presentation and deterministic integer batch arithmetic **without any funding**. A later separately authorized R1 could research deposit scans, custody/escrow/reorg and identity gates; test tokens do not demonstrate real demand, safe assets or profitable execution. |
| **Canonical Solana USDC / Ironwood ZEC** [I/U] | A stable Solana quote asset could let ZEC holders express a familiar price bound. But neither enough two-sided flow nor liquidity, custody appetite, eligibility, market access or price competitiveness exists in evidence. Exact [Circle-identified mint](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/usdc-contract-addresses) [P for issuer identification, accessed 2026-09-29] and real token program/issuer controls require recheck before use; no USDC is approved for trading here. |
| **One exact issuer-qualified xStock mint / Ironwood ZEC** [I/U] | Could test interest in ZEC-funded exposure to a specified tokenized stock. Display multipliers, freeze/transfer restrictions, corporate actions, market hours/reference pricing and holder/venue eligibility make value and legal risk pair-specific. **No xStock mint qualifies yet**, and a ticker cannot be an execution identifier. This is not the old same-mint xStock/USDC crossing thesis. |

The buyer deposits ZEC into a **Kerb committee-controlled Ironwood receiver**; the seller locks exact SPL in proposed Solana escrow. This gives no unilateral buyer ZEC exit after a mined deposit and cannot make the two ledger transitions atomic. Buyer-first ZEC payout risks losing ZEC if SPL release fails; seller-first SPL release risks losing SPL if the ZEC payout is withheld. Custody insolvency, collusion or liveness may make the theoretical price improvement worthless. Compare *all-in executable outcomes* (including expected waiting, failed fills, custody exposure, chain/issuer freezes, settlement delays and fee/slippage) against the actual authorized route for the **same pair, exact mint, size and time**, not a fabricated spread or a Solana-only market.

## 2. Why test a private batch cross first [I, not yet chosen production]

A batch can compute one candidate volume-maximizing integer price for accepted opposite-side orders, then seal per-order fills. Conditional on a fixed, complete admitted set and correctly run reference rules, every crossed lot satisfies its submitted limit and receives the **same candidate auction price**. This is a narrow *mechanism property*: it does not prove truthful bidding, anonymity, low costs, honest admission, finality or that the price is a contemporaneous outside-market fair value. The candidate midpoint of the maximal-volume plateau favors no solver-chosen endpoint but can be moved by strategic orders; the tie-break's proposed commitment-derived key can be ground or influenced by admissions. Price-level allocation helps competitive limits, pro-rata at the marginal level shares scarce lots, and leftover whole lots require an explicitly unbiased key. All require adversarial tests and owner selection before real use. [Mechanism §2](01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md#2-candidate-uniform-price-call-auction-i-d0-reference-not-selected-production-rule) lays out the exact D0 reference rule and arithmetic vector.

**Privacy is a separate gate, not a consequence of batching.** Client encryption and independently controlled MPC may hide order quantities/limits from an individual matcher if the protocol and governance achieve their stated corruption assumptions; an intake operator, RPC, shared eligibility database, public Solana signer/lock, unique deposit amount or ZEC scanner log can still reveal which person owns an accepted ciphertext. A matching operator includes all services/logs it controls or can access. Both *plaintext confidentiality* **and** *identity unlinkability to an accepted order* must be demonstrated against that full control domain before **any funded** trial. Distinct custodians/eligibility actors may know private mappings required for legal duties and ZEC signing; that is a disclosed trust cost, not a reason to weaken the matcher-specific requirement. See [threat model](../security/07-THREAT-MODEL.md). A public auction price, Solana escrow account, transfer amount and claim timestamp leak information despite sealed individual fills; a private computation is not globally anonymous trading.

**Fairness depends on admission:** an intake-signed root authenticates what intake included but cannot prove it did not omit traders *before* acceptance. For a receipted order, inclusion and ciphertext availability must be independently checkable; a missing accepted input aborts rather than permit selective clearing. Matcher selective abort and unbounded retry timing need a separately reviewed policy; making a root “on chain” without complete funding and confidentiality does not solve fairness. A threshold output signature is not proof that `V*` was computed on the right funded order set or that the seller received ZEC. Independent custody scan/credit assignment differs from matching. The **historical Sep29** recommendation kept Zoss v1 messages disabled for funded use due to public source metadata [P/I: [assessment, current successor](../../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md), then read2026-09-29]; current shared-library reuse is the separate docs direction in the banner, never a receipt-based funding substitute.

A candidate call epoch aggregates coincident demand but adds latency, cutoff races and reorg/refund complexity. **No one-minute duration or order-cap figure is inherited from old Kerb.** Choose cadence and maximum accepted set only with privacy-circuit capacity measurements, failure traces and observed limit-intent arrivals, or report them as unknown; a speed benchmark alone does not establish economic benefit.

## 3. Alternatives and choice boundary

| Alternative | Potential advantage | Changed privacy/custody/economic assumption and reason not to silently substitute |
|---|---|---|
| **Bilateral maker/solver RFQ** | Immediate executable dealer response might win when peer contra flow is sparse; maker may offer inventory rather than waiting for a cross. | The maker reads the bilateral request, can reject or reprice depending on rules, and learns counterpart/flow data unless separately hidden. It is *not* the requested no-single-matcher market-wide private batch price discovery; switching to maker-visible RFQ requires an **explicit new owner choice**. [JupiterZ v2](https://jupiterz.jup.ag/docs/v2) [P for vendor description, accessed 2026-09-29] is a serious comparator, not a Kerb integration. [NEAR supported chains](https://docs.near-intents.org/resources/chain-support) [P for vendor list, 2026-09-29] names transparent-only Zcash in the source-spec review, not native Ironwood↔SPL atomic settlement. |
| **Continuous visible order book** | Less batch wait, familiar standing liquidity/priority. | Visible orders and addresses break the intended pre-trade privacy; encrypted continuous matching would itself need independent admission/order-content/identity proofs and the same non-atomic custody path. Current sparse-contraflow assumption is [U], not a reason to rule it out. |
| **Self-operated TEE or simple commit–reveal** | May reduce computational setup or stage partial order secrecy. | One operator controlling the enclave/attestation, ingress or RPC can still link identities; a reveal can expose orders; neither proves accepted-set completeness or removes ZEC spend-quorum and two-chain loss risk. The Idea Bank author's TEE suggestions are hypotheses, not Kerb security evidence [P/U: preserved row, 2026-09-28]. |
| **Transparent Zcash HTLC comparator** | Could investigate a different user-controlled refund/timeout model. | [ZIP 300](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0300) [P, accessed 2026-09-29] concerns transparent Zcash script path, not an Ironwood shielded note; a matching Solana SPL lock still needs design. It changes the chosen product and does not inherit this committee-custody pitch. [ZIP 203](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0203) [P, accessed 2026-09-29] expiration only governs an *unmined* transaction; it is not a shielded deposit refund. |
| **Private batch cross / candidate D0 auction** | Direct price discovery across an accepted order set, potential peer crossing with sealed individual fills; reference arithmetic can be falsified cheaply on synthetic data. | Settlement stays **non-atomic** and committee-trusted; private funded admission and single-matcher identity protection are unmet; no observed demand or selected economic parameters. Preferred *research candidate*, **not deployed or adopted production market rules**. |

**No automatic residual maker stage.** The old same-chain market had prefunded maker ladders and residual execution after a user cross. Here a residual maker would need separate inventory and settlement on **both** chains, maker identity/privacy decisions, fee and credit policies, safe payout/refund sequencing, and whether maker prices/obligations are published. A maker receiving individual order contents contradicts this design unless the owner deliberately revises the privacy requirement. Primary cross has no hidden fallback: if `V*=0`, the D0 candidate makes **no fill**. A default maker route, residual fee, maker rebate, RFQ last look or dealer liquidity is not a Kerb feature merely because it appeared in the legacy docs.

## 4. Incentives, manipulation and settlement exposure to test

| Failure or strategic behavior [I/U] | Impact and required discriminating evidence |
|---|---|
| **Fake or duplicate funding / root omission** | A cryptographically correct clearing on unfunded, duplicated or selectively accepted orders creates phantom liabilities or advantaged counterparties. Need a one-to-one scanner credit / SPL escrow lock ↔ signed accepted ciphertext proof, accepted-set receipts and ability to detect omission. No such construction exists today. |
| **Limit manipulation / self-cross** | One economic controller can submit both sides, shift `[p_lo,p_hi]` or manufacture apparent volume; encryption alone does not prevent wash-like behavior and eligibility records may be private. Test adversarial order books and legal surveillance design, not an invented self-trade-prevention promise. |
| **Order splitting / tie-key grinding** | Splitting marginal quantity across commitments can improve last-lot outcomes; a trader choosing nonce/commitment can grind favorable keys. Quantify under small integer books; choose a final unpredictable, non-matcher-biasable tie source only after threat analysis. Public `(slot, owner)` tie priority is not portable to private orders. |
| **Cutoff sniping, abort and side channels** | Public Solana lock timing and one-off ZEC values can reveal interest, while intake can delay receipts and cluster members can abort after observing aggregate outputs. Test observer-specific identity joins and every receipt/abort trace; hiding plaintext prices does not make inclusion order irrelevant. |
| **Sparse flow or price discovery gap** | A single empty epoch is safe but offers no fill; wide plateau exposes ambiguity about outside value. Compare actual independent, time-matched executable alternatives and paired limit intents, not historical swaps alone. Price publication may increase information leakage without improving execution. |
| **Solana freeze, wrong recipient, custody quorum outage or false attestation** | Both chains can finalize separately; signed committee statements may be false or late and ZEC refund cannot be forced by a user. Choose and publish non-atomic order, liability/dispute/loss policy, scanner finality and Solana vault conditions before any funded order; neither uniform price nor MPC eliminates this risk. |

A concrete demand study would collect *consented, eligible* paired buy/sell **limit intents** (without accepting funds), their exact pair/size/arrival window, and simultaneous executable RFQ/AMM/other-route quotes (with validity, restrictions and all-in cost). Segment by pair and user type and record no-fill and abandoned-intent outcomes. This is a **proposed measurement design**, not interviews run, results observed or approved thresholds. Quote availability for canonical USDC/ZEC or one xStock/ZEC cannot be inferred from a test token demonstration. No claim that off-hours flow is automatically matched or economically superior follows from tokenized-stock activity.

## 5. Fees and decision criteria [U]

No basis-point fee, payer, minimum fee, per-order rounding, maker pricing, protocol/distributor/crank share, liquidity rebate, custody spread, insolvency backstop, insurance, revenue projection or viable unit economics has been selected. The old Solana-only **5 bps per side / residual maker** schedule does **not** transfer to a ZEC/SPL cross. The [D0 reference vector](01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md#2-candidate-uniform-price-call-auction-i-d0-reference-not-selected-production-rule) deliberately computes **zero fees to isolate conservation**, not a product promise. Before a funded design, propose exact fee asset on both legs, max signed fee and buy-side prefunding reserve, transfer/issuer costs, integer rounding with partial fills, accounting treatment for all unfilled credits, operator/custodian expenses, payout allocation and whether fees alter the all-in route comparison. Every fee dollar collected during a default/non-atomic loss window raises additional claims/loss questions; no profitability conclusion can be made without a budget, real quotes and volume evidence.

**Decision method, not an approved pass threshold:** first falsify the private *content and identity* gate with adversarial full-domain traces; then show correct one-time funded-order admission and an independently reproducible integer reference; then challenge both settlement release orders and refunds under reorg, freeze and custodian failure; only then compare consented crossable intents against actual executable alternatives and proposed all-in fees. A public clearing price or successful local arithmetic demo alone would not authorize R1 funding. Each future R1/R2/R3 decision needs a new security/legal/asset assessment and explicit owner authorization for any testnet or real-asset action.
