## Customer, operator and asset boundary

**[I]** Potential counterparties are legally eligible traders with opposite needs in **the same exact pair and epoch**; a channel might be an eligible broker/distributor or self-directed interface; a separate liquidity provider could submit fully specified funded interest only if the private-match and admission rules allow it. None has agreed. Matching operators, legal identity/eligibility providers, ZEC scanner/custodians, Solana escrow authority, infrastructure providers and security auditors are distinct costs and potential power centers. A committee threshold is not solvency insurance. The ZEC custodian can withhold, lose or misdirect funds; a Solana escrow can be frozen/blocked by issuer or program policy. There is no demonstrated atomic swap or unilateral ZEC refund. Document any custodian fee and liability before proposing a commercial payer.

**Pair A:** SYNTHETIC SPL/testnet Ironwood ZEC is research. D0 has no deposits or exchange, hence **no transaction revenue**; hypothetical future authorized R1 funding would not establish commercial willingness to pay. **Pair B:** canonical Solana USDC/Shielded Ironwood ZEC can be studied only with actual legal/technical permissions and exact mint/program checks. **Pair C:** one exact qualified xStock/Shielded Ironwood ZEC adds issuer transfer/freeze, scaled-unit, corporate-action and jurisdiction costs and has **no qualified mint**. Neither real pair may borrow demand, licensing, risk premium or liquidity from the synthetic pair, another ticker or a wrapped/transparent ZEC route. See [issuer integration](../architecture/05-TOKEN-2022-ISSUER-INTEGRATION.md) and [legal gates](../legal/09-REGULATORY-AND-LEGAL.md).

## A model that does not manufacture economics

Let `p` denote a single exact pair and period, not all three pooled. Define `Q_p` as **unique completed traded base-asset lots**; `N_p` as the value of completed transactions counted **once per matched buyer/seller pair** in a clearly specified quote currency at each execution price, not submitted interest or double-counted buyer-plus-seller turnover. For xStock, define raw atoms, display multiplier and underlying-equivalent convention separately; do not compare an adjusted UI amount to an unadjusted transfer. Let `f_b,p` and `f_s,p` be unknown collected buyer and seller fee rates on their explicitly defined bases, `r_p` any agreed distributor/participant rebate, `c_chain,p` all chain fees sponsored by the operator, `c_cust,p` scanning, key governance, signer/prover, insurance if actually bought, inventory and reconciliation costs, `c_mpc,p` independent cluster/admission overhead, `c_ops,p` incidents, support and eligible-user servicing, and `c_acq,p` acquisition/integration cost allocated per period. Let `F_p` allocate fixed security audits, legal/regulatory, engineering and monitoring cost. None has an observed number.

**[I]** A simplified contribution identity for one executed trade `j` is

`net_j = collected_buyer_fee_j + collected_seller_fee_j − rebates_j − sponsored_chain_cost_j − custody_cost_j − MPC/admission_cost_j − operations_and_disputes_j − allocated_acquisition_j − expected_loss_or_reserve_j`.

Then `period_contribution_p = Σ(completed, collectable j in p) net_j − F_p`. Disputed, stuck or refunded legs do not automatically create collectible fee revenue; escrowed money is a **liability**, not revenue. Reserve/loss treatment requires an actual legal/accounting model; an expected-loss parameter cannot turn an unbounded non-atomic exposure into insured execution. Fiat valuation of ZEC/SPL across delays introduces price/inventory risk; measure asset units and conversion time separately. Refund network cost, transaction failures, relays, eligibility checks and leak/incident response belong in cost. If a zero-volume period still requires quorum availability, fixed operating burn remains.

**Conditional break-even [I]:** if the per-completed-trade contribution is positive under a specified mix, required completed trades `Q* ≥ F / average_positive_net_contribution`; if contribution is nonpositive, increasing fills does not resolve losses. Alternatively, for fixed mix/price and a *verified* effective retained fee rate `t_p` on unique notional and all-in variable-cost rate `v_p`, `N*_p = F_p/(t_p − v_p)` only if `t_p > v_p`; this omits tail loss and is invalid if risk cannot be priced. An attractive gross fee is not user surplus. No fee schedule, revenue target, annual volume, ROI, price saving or venture-scale multiple is set in this design. The old [5-bps/user, distributor share and revenue scenarios](../../docs/business/11-BUSINESS-MODEL.md) apply to another Solana-only mechanism and **must not** be used as Kerb Zolana estimates.

## Two-sided user comparison, not a spread slogan

For the same pair, size, eligible user, decision time and available executable route, record buyer's total asset outlay and seller's net asset receipts under that alternative **and** under the proposed matched outcome. Include actual execution price, fees on each side, time-to-finality, partial-fill/abort probability, FX movement, blocked-refund cost, custody exposure and user risk tolerance; do not call a quoted route executable if its size, geography or expiry excludes the user. Since the alternative can return funds without threshold custodian exposure, a headline lower fee or private-limit feature does not guarantee better utility. Specify whether a participant wants shielded ZEC payout or another destination and who controls its disclosure. No measured all-in comparison exists.

**[U] Required before any commercial claim:** attributable non-duplicate order-intent episodes from independent economic owners, same pair/window/compatible limits, paired executable alternative quotes, elapsed times, consented interviews, written trial conditions, named legal operator and permission analysis, verified MPC/custody operating budget, independent key-holder responsibilities, prospective loss allocation, issuer terms for C, measured adversarial privacy and settlement failure outcomes. A distribution agreement cannot be inferred from Idea Bank [row #5](../../docs/sources/notion/idea-bank-row-5.md) **[P]**, captured 2026-09-28, or from founder advice in the [X capture](../../docs/sources/x/status-2038643299221729462.md) **[P]** for that author's opinions only.

**Kill or redesign [I]:** when no pair produces risk-adjusted two-sided interest at a lawful price; when matcher identity linkage cannot be prevented; when funding binding is not both private and single-use; when a viable independent custody quorum and insured/explicitly accepted loss policy cannot be assembled; or when operators' true cost exceeds any fee users accept. Counsel decisions and owner authorization are independent gates, not numbers that can be averaged into a positive contribution. [GTM gates](12-GTM-AND-VALIDATION.md) test need without handling funds.
