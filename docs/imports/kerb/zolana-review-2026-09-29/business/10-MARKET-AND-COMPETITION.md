## What market is being tested

**[I]** One proposed Solana↔Zcash architecture, **three separate order books, custody liabilities, admission rules and eligibility decisions**, not one interchangeable ZEC pool: (A) a **SYNTHETIC** Solana mint against Zcash **testnet Ironwood ZEC** for authorized later research; (B) canonical Solana USDC against native shielded Ironwood ZEC; (C) **one exact issuer-qualified Solana xStock mint/program** against native shielded Ironwood ZEC. D0 is a non-settling synthetic-order demonstrator, not even funded pair A. No xStock is qualified. `ZEC` never denotes wrapped ZEC, a transparent Zcash leg, or a balance on a solver bridge. A buyer funds ZEC in proposed settlement and receives SPL; a seller locks SPL and receives ZEC, with committee custody risk on ZEC and issuer/program transfer risk on SPL. See [Solana program design](../architecture/04-ONCHAIN-PROGRAM-DESIGN.md), [issuer integration](../architecture/05-TOKEN-2022-ISSUER-INTEGRATION.md) and [off-chain services](../architecture/06-OFFCHAIN-SERVICES.md).

**[I]** The prospective job is confidential market-wide limit matching with independently governed nodes, rather than bilateral price-taking from a solver. But matching-content MPC is insufficient: an intake/RPC/funding record may link an accepted order to a person. The owner requires **no single matcher or control domain to learn plaintext orders or the identity linked to an accepted order**. Ingress and funded-order credential design and test remain unsolved; no funded market can claim this property. Separately governed eligibility and custodians may see identity/payment data. Public Solana signer, account, mint, amount and timing can still leak. See [privacy threat model](../security/07-THREAT-MODEL.md).

## Distinct demand hypotheses, not one pooled TAM

| Pair | Plausible user/workflow **[I]** | Strongest contrary case / missing evidence **[U]** | Discovery artifact that could update belief |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — SYNTHETIC SPL/testnet Ironwood ZEC | Developers/researchers wanting a reproducible privacy and failure-state demonstrator, not a payable venue. | No demonstrated commercial purchaser, genuine counterparty demand or custody safety. Synthetic D0 participation is not live demand. | Independent reproduction of D0 privacy/admission trace and specific technical collaboration request; label interest research-only. |
| B — canonical USDC/Ironwood ZEC | Legally eligible ZEC holders and Solana-dollar holders might seek limit-based shielded-ZEC conversion with less order disclosure to a matcher. | Existing convenient routes can win on fees, time, inventory, compliance and trusted-custody avoidance; Solana amounts/identity can remain public. No executable two-sided quotes, eligibility, custody provider, counsel opinion or legal permission verified. | Consented last-trade histories for both sides, same-window executable alternatives, signed-off jurisdiction/role review and a non-binding specific paper trial request; never solicit funds. |
| C — exact qualified xStock/Ironwood ZEC | An eligible xStock holder and ZEC buyer might seek this exact exposure without publishing limits to a matcher. | Two-sided overlap may be vanishingly thin; xStock issuer, rights, freeze/transfer extensions, scaled units and corporate actions complicate settlement; no mint has passed qualification. Same ticker across issuers/chains is not fungible. | One exact mint/program qualification and issuer/counsel review **before** counting candidate orders, plus independently sourced actual opposite-side intent and alternative quotes for the same eligibility/window. |

The public [Idea Bank row #5 capture](../../docs/sources/notion/idea-bank-row-5.md) **[P] for its author's hypothesis only**, retrieved 2026-09-28, suggests tokenized-stock dark pools and institutional desks in SG/HK/Brazil. Its row ID was not captured for direct attribution and its asserted target buyers are **not** interviewed Kerb customers; it neither validates xStock/ZEC nor substitutes for eligibility review. Secondary [Bitquery xStock analysis](https://bitquery.io/investigations/solana-tokenized-stocks-xstocks) **[S]**, cited in the [old market note](../../docs/business/10-MARKET-AND-COMPETITION.md), samples Solana xStock turnover, not unfilled opposing ZEC orders or cross-chain willingness to pay. Neither award nor aggregate swap volume proves this market.

**Investment decision [I]:** examine B or C only after an artifact-backed repeat need, authorized legal pathway, viable two-sided terms, verified independent operators and tested privacy/funded-admission/settlement gates. Stop the specific pair if a qualified exact-asset cohort is absent, users choose existing quotes after risk-adjusted comparison, or matcher identity linkage persists. This is not a conclusion that competitors lack comparable features. [Economics](11-BUSINESS-MODEL.md) and [falsifiable discovery](12-GTM-AND-VALIDATION.md) intentionally keep volume and customer counts unknown.
