## Separate questions before writing code or soliciting liquidity

A (SYNTHETIC SPL / Ironwood **testnet** ZEC) is a technical learning path, **D0 has no funded transactions**. B (canonical Solana USDC / native shielded Ironwood ZEC) asks whether eligible users have repeat conversion demand that justifies price, wait and committee custody risk. C (one exact qualified Solana xStock mint/program / native shielded Ironwood ZEC) additionally asks whether the actual issuer, holder eligibility, corporate actions, and market rules permit this pair and whether opposite economic owners coincide. Do not mix these denominators or convert an xStock ticker, wrapped-ZEC balance, transparent ZEC route or another issuer's token into the same market. [Idea Bank row #5](../../docs/sources/notion/idea-bank-row-5.md) **[P] for author hypothesis**, captured 2026-09-28, proposes dark-pool demand; it has **no verified Kerb contact**. Competition examples and source limitations appear in [market](10-MARKET-AND-COMPETITION.md).

**Risks [I/U]** by category: value (actual costly unmet conversion), usability (wait, exposure, withdrawal, dispute comprehension), viability (fees versus operator cost and tail loss), feasibility (single-use funding/admission and privately governed MPC), ethics (misleading privacy/guaranteed-refund language), GTM (eligible channel/consent), strategy (other venues or simple RFQ solve need), team (independent matching, custody and legal capacity). These are hypotheses, not solved requirements. Legal eligibility, owner privacy and real-fund authorization are **hard gates**; commercial metrics cannot override them.

## Draft XYZ hypotheses — denominators and actions explicit

The numbers below are **proposed thresholds for prospective experiment design**, not owner-approved success criteria, factual conversion rates or predictions. Freeze cohorts, date windows, method and exclusions **before** sampling; record failures and nonresponse. Revise only as a newly labeled experiment. Do not put a payment button, deposit address or live order in an unapproved pretotype.

1. **B, demand:** “At least **2 of 10** independently sourced, legally plausible Solana-USDC/ZEC decision-makers with a documented recent conversion episode will supply a redacted timestamped actual order/quote or declined-order artifact and agree to a scheduled **paper-only** paired-route comparison.” This tests costly engagement, not revenue; a desk's repeated staff interviews count as one economic decision-maker. Unknown eligibility excludes from the numerator but remains visible in denominator/exclusion log.
2. **C, demand conditional on exact mint qualification:** “At least **2 of 10** independently sourced, provisionally eligible exact-mint xStock holders or ZEC conversion decision-makers with a past episode will supply a specific written hypothetical trial ticket (side, raw size, time window, constraints) for the **same exact qualified mint** and allocate time for a simulated paired comparison.” Do not start this test until issuer/counsel screening defines eligible cohort and mint; otherwise report **not testable**, not a zero or a pass.
3. **Technical privacy and safety are not popularity tests:** for **every** synthetic accepted order in the defined D0 adversarial corpus, an individual matcher plus all intake/RPC/funding/eligibility logs it controls or can access must neither recover plaintext order nor join it to an identity; all funded-set double-credit, omission, false-attestation and conflicting two-leg transitions must detect/abort. One counterexample **fails the relevant gate**. Plaintext reference matching can test arithmetic/invariants, not MPC privacy. Successful D0 never authorizes testnet funds.

## Low-risk pretotypes and behavioral artifacts [I]

| Experiment | Method, target and observable action | Failure/counterevidence |
|---|---|---|
| Past-episode interviews | After permission, ask each B/C participant about the **last** buy/sell or abandoned trade: exact assets/network/pool, jurisdiction constraints, timestamp/time zone, size/limit, quote available then, outcome, participant's workaround, waiting/refund pain and what actually changed behavior. Record same-owner repeats and negative episodes. Ask distributor execution/risk owners separately about the last route they integrated and why. | Opinions (“sounds good”), contest votes, requested feature lists, unverified personas and synthetic trades are not actions or two-sided demand. |
| Explainer plus paper trial | Show one-page non-settling sequence and **two** unresolved loss paths, privacy boundaries and custody/quorum powers, before requesting a written paper trial owner/date/asset/size/time window under compliance review. Measure completed specific artifacts divided by all eligible invites and refusal reason. No deposit, signing or waitlist-as-liquidity. | Trial request without risk comprehension, anonymous signup or verbal interest is not a qualifying commitment; informed refusal is useful counterevidence. |
| Historical paired-quote reconstruction | With consent and appropriate rights, inspect redacted **real historical** decision episodes from independent economic owners. Reconstruct opposite side for exact pair/compatible limit and contemporaneous executable route for each side at size, time, eligibility and fees; include failed windows and quote expiration. Simulate candidate auction; separately report buyer cost, seller proceeds, waiting and uncompensated custody risk. | On-chain executed swaps alone lack unfilled interest and cannot prove matched intent; stale quotes or recycled self-cross cannot support a benefit claim. No historical artifact means result unknown, not assumed improved. |

**Question prompts:** “What was the last conversion you tried, and why this route? Can you show a redacted quote/receipt and time?” “What failed or caused abandonment? What would the other party's executable alternative have been?” “Who would approve a new settlement/custody arrangement, and what happened at your last onboarding?” “How did a delayed payout, locked transfer or identity disclosure affect you?” “Would your risk committee allocate time to a non-settling paper exercise after reviewing both worst-case loss paths?” Ask for contradictory examples and permission to retain/cite; never ask for keys, viewing keys, client identities, live trading instructions or confidential trade data without rights and consent.

## Draft decision gates, not reported milestones

| Gate | Evidence required to say **research is promising** | Stop/qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Pair-specific need | For B or C independently: above relevant XYZ threshold, source-diverse historical records, documented repeat need or actual failed alternative, and paired buyer/seller interest same exact pair/window. Record all invites, qualifying episodes and exclusions. | If denominator not recruited, label inconclusive; if positive responses are generic, insufficient. If no same-window compatible natural opposite interest, stop that pair hypothesis; do not pool across assets. |
| User outcome | At least **two independent qualifying paired historical windows per candidate pair** show each side's risk-adjusted outcome versus an actually executable alternative; complete failure/partial cases and denominator disclosed. Threshold is a **draft design choice**, not a prediction. | If delay, fees, freeze/custody loss or compliance cause either party to prefer an alternative, no cheaper/better-market claim. A simulated quote does not validate realized fills. |
| Technical owner gate | Demonstrate full accepted-set integrity, one-time funded credential **without matcher identity linkage**, independent MPC governance/content protection, adversarial metadata trace, quorum view/spend boundaries and both non-atomic loss orders per [test plan](../security/08-TEST-AND-VERIFICATION-PLAN.md). | One matcher/control-domain identity mapping, plaintext recovery, duplicate funding credit or unverifiable admission blocks funded matching. D0 cannot pass funded R1 by itself. |
| Operational/legal gate | For each proposed real pair, named operator roles, reviewed exact mint/program/network, custodian roster/threshold and key recovery, loss allocation/incident disclosure, external security review and competent jurisdiction/issuer analysis. | Missing counsel/issuer qualification or inability to honor transfer/refund terms stops real-asset work. Even passing research gates still requires separate owner authorization for **each** transaction/deployment. |
| Economics/channel | Specific paper-trial owner at an eligible channel and independently estimated full costs/terms from real proposed providers; a route that benefits **both** sides and contributes after costs/loss provisions. | A feature request, source-author claim, award or hypothetical revenue model is not a customer or margin. No identified lawful distribution path means stop commercial positioning. |

**Evidence handling:** private episode ID, source organization (pseudonymous), consent and jurisdiction eligibility status, exact mint/program and Zcash network/pool, side, timestamp, size/limit, executable alternative and quote expiry, outcome, economic owner, duplicate flag, contrary evidence, provenance date and artifact reference. De-identify, minimize, store securely and publish only aggregates/consented quotes. Participants may decline disclosure; do not infer privacy or customer approval from their silence. Track event, published and retrieved dates separately for competitor changes; recheck source pages before external claims. Never use a named organization's support without consent.

**Decision wording [I]:** “As of [date], pair B/C has [qualifying episodes]/[eligible approached], [paired windows]/[windows observed], [written paper trials], [risk-informed refusals]; privacy/settlement/legal gates [passed/failed/untested], with [source limitations].” Brackets are reporting fields **not numbers or completed findings**. If there are no interviews, say **none conducted**. D0 can report runnable technical observations with logs but cannot turn an unvalidated market into a business.
