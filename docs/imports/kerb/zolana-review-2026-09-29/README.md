## What the proposed product is

One Solana–Zcash market architecture with three **separate** pairs: (A) SYNTHETIC Solana test mint versus Ironwood ZEC on testnet as a *research target*, (B) exact canonical Solana USDC versus native Ironwood ZEC, (C) one issuer-qualified Solana xStock mint at a time versus native Ironwood ZEC. No pooled liquidity, cross-pair netting, wrapped ZEC substitution or implied real-asset eligibility. The later candidate chooses an independent **Funding Admission Authority (FAA)** that sees the full signed order and participant identity, verifies ownership and durable funding holds, and certifies the SAME bounded tuple consumed by MPC. FAA/intake/custody/coordinator must actually be outside every matcher control/access domain; this governance and the private certificate verifier remain hard unproven gates, not an implemented blind credential. Independent MPC would produce sealed fills and prices for entitled participants/coordinator, **not a default public clearing price**. **No end-to-end funded flow or no-single-matcher identity mechanism is implemented or proven.**

**Known privacy FAIL in the later candidate (§6), not merely missing measurements:** permitted padded8 with one natural seller Alice and attacker-owned contra orders exposes accepted identity through public epoch escrow; with Alice ask known to be6 or10, attacker buy8 fills iff6, exposing private limit via legitimate own results. Padding/private price/FAA separation do not fix this. GD2 fails for this allowed corpus; **no currently viable private production-demo candidate** under the unchanged owner guarantee. Hidden collateral/membership with compositional evidence or an explicit owner-reviewed guarantee change is required; no waiver selected. Full safety/operations workflow remains design-review material, not funded release readiness.

Threshold custody means a live quorum controls ZEC spends and refunds; a colluding, unavailable or key-losing committee may permanently freeze or lose user funds. SPL release and Ironwood ZEC payment are two non-atomic legs: either leg can finish without the other. A user cannot compel a mined shielded deposit to refund. **Symmetric admission holds:** buyer FULL max debit and seller atoms are encumbered at PREPARE before the accepted root; delayed matching, zero fills, expiry or restart cannot refund/reassign them. Only finalized allocation yields disjoint unused portions, or definitive canonical irreversible ABORT/exclusion with stale-commit/authorization fencing safely releases a hold. Each portion, not an entire credit, has at most one reservation. **Historical message-profile scope, not overall current Zoss:** optional same-receiver/same-note diagnosis cannot supply money/admission/settlement authority. Sep30 opaque public receipts exclude default source locator/height/value; cmx-derived ID/height/value/full-envelope defaults are rejected preSep30 history. App-key/timing/operator privacy remains unproven; diagnostics stay OFF. Shared libraries are now a separate docs direction in the banner; source observation still needs Kerb funding-owner/canonical/inventory/dedup checks. See [historical architecture](architecture/03-SYSTEM-ARCHITECTURE.md) and [current consumer contract](../../../../../zoss/docs/kerb.md).

## Decision and proof boundaries

**Owner-selected thesis, not a working implementation:** three separated Solana/Zcash pairs; no single matcher/control domain sees an accepted order's plaintext or linked identity; threshold signers may control, withhold or lose ZEC and there is no unilateral Ironwood refund. The Sep29 dossier left admission/rules/provider/threshold/release order unresolved. The later proposed candidate chooses FAA privileged admission, fixed eight-slot zero-fee uniform-price auction with private results, distributed-preprocessing MPC, 2-of-3 spends with unanimous three-domain business decisions, canonical epoch fences and SPL-first settlement. These are **design selections awaiting owner review**, not observed independent operators, a working verifier, privacy proof, safe parameter manifest or legal clearance. Historical D0 researcher-only arithmetic remains separate from that candidate. Companion historical discussions remain in [mechanism](mechanism/01-MECHANISM-SPEC.md), [security](security/07-THREAT-MODEL.md) and [roadmap](product/16-ROADMAP-AND-DECISIONS.md).

R1 is **settlement research**, not authorization to fund testnet. R2/R3 involving real assets require independent security, issuer/custody, legal and demand evidence plus **explicit later user authorization**. The 2026-10-01 shared-library documentation adoption does not approve this suite's future financial replacement plan, signing, deployment, code publication or transactions.
