## 3. Payment classifier cho complete single T — Proposed, chưa solved

`T != P` is not `DoesNotPayObligation`. A fee/effect replacement P′ or arbitrary T may spend the agreed input **and pay S**; same-effect authorization change remains P. Earlier admissible bound P/T is retained once obligation funded, even if client first observed it later. U self-paying before any escrow exists does not force S funding.

### Gross credit không phải user consideration — M3 gate

There is **no implemented complete current classifier**. Proposed receiver trial-decryption establishes only aggregate incoming **gross credit**, not origin/debit/net user consideration:

- `grossCreditS(T)`: all admitted, correctly recoverable outputs to the bound S receiver set, in exact source integer units.
- `solverDebitS(T)`: all real S-owned/contributed inputs across supported pools/accounts/transparent legs, under exact funding/fee policy.
- `netCreditS(T) = grossCreditS(T) - solverDebitS(T)`: checked signed accounting; never clamp negative/debit to paid. Even positive net S credit does not establish user funding if third-party inputs contribute.

**Initial meaningful P/Q path:** authenticate the complete real-input set equals the same **one U-owned note**, no extra real S/third-party/other-pool/transparent funding; every padded dummy must be constrained zero. With this complete no-S/no-third-party gate, admitted S credit may instantiate C under bound fee/output predicate. Claimed signer list/local wallet policy cannot establish it.

**Arbitrary mixed-input T remains reachable** when U uses another wallet. Full terminal acceptance must authenticate all real inputs/owners/values and complete S debits/user contribution/third-party semantics; unrelated IVK or one account view cannot exclude hidden S inputs elsewhere. Gross-only economics have not been selected. Unknown ownership/debits/completeness produces `Unknown/EvidencePending`, neither payout nor inferred full refund.

Required counterexample: T spends committed U n plus S note A, returns A to S and U principal/change to U minus fee. Gross S credit A, debit A, net0: **not user payment of A**. Also handle gross≥A/net<A, hidden other-pool/account inputs and zero dummy semantics; no caller-supplied owner labels as proof.

### Receiver-side proof route

The proposed receiving relation processes **complete authenticated T**, not compact prefix or selected paying/nonpaying output:

1. Bind actual effects/auth/full action and output vectors, every admitted receiver/alias/pool/transparent leg and agreed fee scope. Unsupported leg/set means no complete negative result.
2. Bind private decryption key/derived receiver to quoted S identity/network/context. Wrong-account IVK failing decrypt is not nonpayment proof.
3. Verify full Ironwood V3 ciphertext/plaintext/recipient/value/note-commitment relation; separately opened cmx or malformed ciphertext is not recoverable consideration.
4. Aggregate every admitted output, including late/second/split payments, with checked occurrence-aware arithmetic; then prove complete no-S-input or full debit/contribution relation to obtain C. Gross sum alone never payout.
5. Bind classification to same history/T/real input/context/predicate; demonstrate sound negative-decryption/completeness, not watcher “nothing seen”.

S IVK has actual account-view scope for a prover receiving it; do not call it note-scoped or require U FVK. Concrete circuits/key binding/complete negative predicate remain missing.

### Independent U sender-side payout

U retains `PayoutWitnessU` **before authorization release**: exact output openings/encryption randomness/input/dummy evidence and original effect/context. Actual sender-output recovery already exists without S IVK; the **composed accepted-history/net/classifier payout proof** does not. Both sender and receiver routes must validate the same economic predicate, not different policies; U's eligible payout cannot wait for S API/IVK/new signature after payment.

Sender witness for intended P does not contain arbitrary other-party hostile-T openings. Permissionless submission/local proving does not make witnesses discoverable. No normal proof worker needs U spending key; authorizing Q is a separate wallet operation.

### Partial/overpay/adversarial allocation vẫn Open

**Implementation/independent terminal construction remains Open, not proportional economics.** For authenticated complete consideration `0 <= C <= A`, `A>0`, funded `D>0`, approved allocation is:

```text
U share = floor(D * C / A)
S share = D - U share
```

Use full-width checked multiplication/division, dust to fixed S, no hidden fees or submitter-selected beneficiary. `C=0` needs actual valid nonpaying same-real-input conflict; `0<C<A` needs complete irreversible source resolution/net amount proof; `C=A` gives U all D. Pure `protocol::allocate`/EVM arithmetic does not authenticate C or history. Target verification/consumption/both actual transfers must commit/rollback together.

|---|---|
| Paying different-effect T/P′, split outputs, earlier P/T | Classify actual complete admitted net consideration, not exact-id-only refund or activation exclusion. |
| Partial `0<C<A` | Apply approved floor/remainder **when authenticated**; composed classifier/escrow/manual execution missing. No underpay→full refund policy. |

Q cannot spend S-received funds; target contract cannot sign ZEC. No gift/clamp/accounting credit/full destination refund or hoped-for S cooperation substitute. Capability remains missing. |
| Nonpaying T spends source elsewhere | Verified C0 conflict may justify S destination refund; does not establish U self-return as Q does. |
| Unsupported receiver/pool/malformed ciphertext/missing inputs | Reject invalid evidence or Unknown; no automatic nonpayment. Missing necessary private facts is enablement blocker. |
